# Find

A user finds cards by searching, narrowing with qualifiers that become removable chips, filtering by type or sort in the View menu, or browsing Favorites, Archive, Trash, Tags and folders. The command palette jumps to views, folders, cards and commands.

## Sub-features

- `find-search` filters the grid live as you type, keeping the query in `?q=` (at most 500 characters: the box stops taking more; the URL never truncates).
- `find-word-match` never marks a card holding the typed words `matchedByMeaning`, with or without qualifiers; a strong meaning match holding only some of the words stays `matchedByMeaning`.
- `find-slash` focuses the search box with `/`, and on Tags the "Find a tag" box.
- `find-qualifiers` turns `tag:`, `type:`, `is:`, `site:`, `folder:` and quoted phrases into chips, through the "Search suggestions" list or Enter. `type:` also takes everyday words (`note`/`notes` → Text, `pdf`/`doc` → Document, `photo`/`picture` → Image, `color`/`colour` → Palette, `voice`/`memo` → Audio, `url`/`bookmark` → Link, `movie` → Video); an unknown `type:` word shows a dashed chip. A tag chip in a card's dialog filters by that tag (never a keyword search) and closes the card.
- `find-enter` on Enter turns a plain word into a keyword chip (a search icon, not `#`) and color, style and time words into their own URL filters (`?color=blue,ff5733&style=minimal&date=This+Week`) kept through a reload and Back; `fav`, `trash` and `archive` navigate. A time phrase beside words splits off, but a lone month, year or number stays a word. A URL's `q` loads as the same chips, in order.
- `find-clear` clears with "Clear all", a chip's remove button, Backspace (the chip just left of the cursor, the last one shown), or "Clear filters" ("Clear search" when there are no chips).
- `find-view` uses the View menu: card types (a `type:` qualifier becomes a checked type; checking another adds to it), sort (Newest, Oldest, Shuffle, Revisit) and "Save as smart folder…". In Revisit, Space on a focused button presses it; on the deck it opens details. The deck's keys (← or `A` archives, → or `K` keeps, Space, `Z` undoes, Escape leaves) are listed under "Revisit" in the `?` sheet, and the deck's hints name the same `Z`.
- `find-saved-order` orders "Newest first" and "Oldest first" by when a card was last saved, and time words such as "yesterday" match that date, in every view and search.
- `find-results` shows "N cards match" above the grid for a search or smart folder ("50+ cards match" while more can load) and "· M related by meaning", with "Hide related" / "Show related". A card holding the search's words never counts as related. With no word match, the closest meaning matches show even below the usual bar, so a plural or near word finds something; nonsense words still find nothing.
- `find-nav` covers the sidebar views (Favorites, Archive, Trash), Tags, folders and smart folders. A folder's tab title follows its name ("Reading list · Teak") through renames. Tags shows each tag in the casing it was last typed in on a card ("Design Systems"), or its lowercase key when no card was saved or edited with it since casing was recorded or Teak only suggested it; its link (`/?tag=design+systems`) and a rename's toast use the lowercase key, and a case-only rename ("design" → "Design") is allowed. Sidebar folders show a folder icon in their color and a card count. "Collapse sidebar" (always shown) folds the sidebar to an icon rail that only "Keep sidebar open" reopens; there, with no folders yet, "New folder" asks for the name in a dialog "New folder" (the open sidebar names it in place). The sidebar is the navigation landmark "Menu" in every state, so axe's `region` rule finds nothing outside a landmark.
- `find-header` keeps library page headers in three parts at 640 px and wider: title left, search in a fixed middle slot, labelled actions right ("View", "Sort: Most used", "Empty Trash").
- `find-palette` covers the command palette: with two or more characters typed it always offers "Search all for “…”", even when no card matches, and its list's accessible name follows the UI language.
- `find-empty` shows each view's empty state, and for a search "Nothing found for “<words>”" ("Nothing found matching your filters" when only filters are set), "Did you mean <word>?" built from your own tags, folder names and card titles, and, with two or more filters, a "Remove …" button per filter ("Remove −heron").
- `find-save-note` offers "Save as a note" beside "Clear filters" when a search of only words finds nothing in Everything, disabled until that save settles. It saves the words as a note through the composer's path ("Saved", or "Already in Teak", "This link is in Trash" with Restore, the card-limit toast), and the same search then finds it. Not offered with a filter (the favorites filter too) or search syntax (`-word`, `#tag`, `key:value`, `OR`, parentheses), or in Trash, Archive, Favorites and folders.
- `find-search-everything` offers "Search everything" before "Clear filters" when a search finds nothing in Favorites, Archive, Trash or a folder (smart folders too), never in Everything. It opens Everything with the same words, chips and filters, and Back restores the view and its search.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- The combobox in every view's header: "Search everything", "Search favorites", "Search archive", "Search Trash", or "Search <folder>".
- Press `/`. Press Ctrl+K (⌘K) for the palette.
- Sidebar links "Everything", "Favorites", "Tags", "Archive", "Trash", and folder links by folder name.
- Key sequences `g e`, `g f`, `g a`, `g t` (Trash), `g l` (Tags), `g s`.

## Driving it with e2e MCP

Preconditions:

- Signed in, with a card holding this run's marker (make one with [capture](./capture.md) or the API).
- Search by a marker that isn't a color, style, time or command word.

Steps:

- **Search.** `press` "/" (focus outside a field): the combobox is focused. `type` the marker: the URL gains `?q=` and the marked cards are listed. Related seeded cards can also appear by meaning; judge by the marked cards.
- **Word match with a qualifier.** `navigate` to `/?q=tag%3Arecipes%20soup` and wait about 10 s. "Text: Recipe idea: roasted tomato soup…" and "Link: Tomato soup - Wikipedia" are listed. Read the mark from the page's Convex WebSocket frames (Playwright `page.on("websocket")`, `framereceived`): in the last `Transition` whose value has a `page` of cards, neither card has `matchedByMeaning: true`. Repeat for `is:fav soup` (only the recipe card) and `tag:design bauhaus` ("Link: Bauhaus - Wikipedia").
- **Some of the words.** Make two API cards: "<run-id> espresso tamping guide: pull a double shot of espresso with an even tamp" and "<run-id> cappuccino guide: steam the milk and pour a latte art heart over a double shot". Wait a minute for embeddings (a search sent earlier caches an empty meaning ranking for 10 minutes), then `navigate` to `/?q=<run-id>%20cappuccino` and wait about 15 s: both cards are listed; in the frames the espresso card (only the run id) has `matchedByMeaning: true` and the cappuccino card has none.
- **No match.** `type` "tag:zzzz-no-match-<run-id>" with `replace: true`: "Nothing found matching your filters" and a button "Clear filters" appear (a typed word that finds nothing offers "Clear search" instead). Use `tag:`, not a plain word (see Gotchas).
- **Did you mean.** Tag a marked card `kestrel-<run-id>`, search `#zzzz-no-match-<run-id> kestral-<run-id>` and `press` "Enter": "Nothing found for “…”" offers "Did you mean …kestrel-<run-id>…?" (a button) and one "Remove …" button per chip; `tap` "Remove #zzzz…" (or the suggestion) and only that chip goes.
- **Save as a note.** Search two nonsense words, `pr8vrf<run-id> qzxwvplm` (ordinary words pull in meaning matches), and `press` "Enter": "Nothing found for “…”" with buttons "Save as a note" and "Clear filters". `tap` "Save as a note": the toast "Saved", and within a second "1 card matches" with the note "pr8vrf<run-id> qzxwvplm" as the result; the URL keeps `?q=`. Add `?type=image` to the same search: no "Save as a note". Delete the note for good in cleanup.
- **Search everything.** `navigate` to `/archive`, `type` "soup" into combobox "Search archive" and `press` "Enter": "Nothing found for “soup”" with buttons "Search everything" and "Clear filters". `tap` "Search everything": the URL is `/?q=soup`, the heading "Everything", the chip "Remove soup" is kept and the seeded "Text: Recipe idea: roasted tomato soup…" is listed. Back returns to `/archive?q=soup` with the same empty state. The button also shows on `/trash`, `/favorites` and a folder; `/?q=<nonsense>` shows "Save as a note" and no "Search everything".
- **Closest by meaning.** `navigate` to `/?q=flowers` and wait about 10 s. Only the seeded "Coral lily.webp" is listed, the status reads "1 related by meaning" (no "0 cards match") with "Hide related", and "Nothing found" never shows while it waits.
- **Result count.** Search the marker and `press` "Enter": the status above the grid reads "N cards match", plus "· M related by meaning" when the server added meaning matches. `tap` "Hide related": only the word matches stay and the button reads "Show related".
- **Clear.** `tap` button "Clear all" (the X in the field): the query empties and the full grid returns.
- **Keyword chip.** `type` the marker and `press` "Enter": the input empties and a chip button "Remove <marker>" appears (lowercased). `navigate` to the current URL: the chip is still there.
- **Tag chip in a card.** Tag a marked note `verify tag 🎉 with spaces` ([card-actions](./card-actions.md), "Tags"), then in its dialog `tap` the chip's name "verify tag 🎉 with spaces". The dialog closes, the search shows the chip "Remove tag verify tag 🎉 with spaces", only cards with that exact tag are listed, and the URL has `q=tag:"…"` and no `card=`. Reload: the dialog stays closed.
- **Qualifier and View.** `navigate` to `/`, `type` "type:quote" and `press` "Enter": the URL becomes `/?type=quote`, the chip "Remove type Quote" appears, only "Quote:" cards remain and the View menu has "Quote" checked. Check "Palette": the URL becomes `/?type=quote,palette` and quotes and palettes are both listed (not "Nothing found"). Remove the chips. `type` "type:notes" and Enter: the chip is "Remove type Text". `type` "type:banana" and Enter: a dashed chip "No type named banana" (accessible name "Remove type:banana: no such card type").
- **Date beside words.** `type` "<marker> last week" and `press` "Enter": chips "Remove <marker>" and "Remove Last Week", and the URL gains `date=Last+Week`.
- **URL search.** `navigate` to `/?q=<marker>%20-heron`: the box is empty, the chips read "<marker>" then "−heron", and the URL keeps that order.
- **Backspace.** From `/?tag=design`, `type` the marker and `press` "Enter": chips "Remove tag filter design" then "Remove <marker>". `press` "Backspace" in the empty box: only "Remove <marker>" goes.
- **Colors and dates in the URL.** On `/`, `type` "blue", Enter, then "this week", Enter: the URL becomes `/?color=blue&date=This+Week` with chips "Remove This Week" and "Remove Blue". `navigate` to that URL again: both chips stay. `tap` "Remove Blue", then go `back`: "Remove Blue" is back.
- **Suggestions.** `type` "ty": listbox "Search suggestions" opens with options such as "type:". "Escape" closes only the list.
- **View menu.** `tap` the button whose name starts "View" (labelled "View" beside its icon at 640 px and wider). The menu has checkboxes "Text" … "Quote", "Show all types", radios "Newest first", "Oldest first", "Shuffle", "Revisit", and "Save as smart folder…", and no "Density". `tap` "Image": the URL gains `?type=image`, the chip "Remove type Image" appears and the button becomes "View: Image". `press` "Escape", then remove the chip.
- **Search sort survives a reload.** `navigate` to `/?q=design`; the View menu has "Best match" checked. Pick "Newest first": the URL becomes `/?q=design&sort=newest`. `navigate` to it again: still checked.
- **Saved order.** Seed the `ordering-saved` pack from `packages/convex` (needs this checkout's backend pushed): `bunx convex run devSeed:seedScenario '{"workosUserId":"<id>","scenario":"ordering-saved"}'`, with the id from `.agents/.state/dev-account.json`. On `/?tag=scenario-ordering-saved` the cards read "Resaved: first saved 400 days ago, saved again yesterday", "Two days…", "Last month…", "Two years…"; "Oldest first" puts "Two years…" first and "Resaved…" last. On "Newest first", search "yesterday" (not "last week", which holds "Two days…" only early in the week): only "Resaved…" remains. The folder "Saved again", `site:ordering-saved.example.com`, Favorites (with the tag), Archive ("Archived: … saved again 3 days ago" before "Archived: saved 10 days ago…") and Trash keep the same order. Screenshot each to `$E/saved-order-*.png`, then permanently delete the pack's cards and the folder.
- **Revisit keys.** View menu → "Revisit": dialog "Revisit" with "1 of N", and under the card its type and "Added <date>". `press` "Space" on "Keep": "2 of N", "Keep" keeps focus and no card dialog opens. The deck has one `role="status"` reading "1 of N. <card label>", then "2 of N. <card label>" (the grid's label, such as "Link: Bauhaus - Wikipedia"; "All caught up" at the end, "Nothing to revisit here" when empty, "Couldn't load cards" on failure), no `aria-live` around the card, and the page keeps one `main`. Use Keep, not Archive, so the run changes no card.
- **Views.** `tap` link "Favorites": heading "Favorites", tab title "Favorites · Teak" (in the UI language, as are Archive, Trash and Tags), combobox "Search favorites", and the seeded favorites, including "Welcome to Teak…". Repeat for "Archive" (empty state "Nothing archived. Archive a card to keep it out of your library.") and "Trash" (seeded with one card: "Text: Old draft I meant to throw away.").
- **Tags.** `tap` link "Tags" (or `g l`): searchbox "Find a tag", button "Sort: Most used" (labelled on screen too), and links "<tag> N cards", three to a row at 1280 px and wider. `press` "/": "Find a tag" is focused. `type` part of a tag: the URL becomes `/tags?q=<text>`, kept through a reload. `type` text no tag has: "No tags match “<text>”." with "Clear search"; `tap` it: the box is empty and focused, the URL is `/tags`, and "<n> tags", the first page and "Show more" (when there are more) return. - **Header slot.** At 1440 px, the search field's left edge is the same on `/`, `/trash`, `/tags` and a folder (screenshot each header); "Empty Trash" sits after "View" without moving the search.

## Gotchas

- Enter turns "orange" into a color filter, "minimal" a style, "today" a time, and "fav", "trash" and "archive" navigate, even when Enter lands as the typed word is written to the URL (the page's own search write must not cancel the move; `__tests__/searchWiring.test.tsx`).
- The dev deployment also searches by meaning, so even a nonsense word can show cards: "zzzz-no-match-…" matched the seeded "Document: Teak search cheat sheet.pdf". For a no-match check use `tag:zzzz-no-match-<run-id>`, which matches exactly. A search that finds no words waits for its meaning matches (up to 6 s) before the empty state.
- An API-made card reaches server search after a short delay; search again after a few seconds before calling a miss a bug.
- Processing can rename a card ("Text: …" to "Palette: …", or a link's title), so match by marker, not by full name.
- Home and search results stay mounted, hidden, behind folder pages, so text can appear twice in the DOM. Trust what `observe` lists as visible.
- Cards tagged "secret" or "hidden" appear only when the search names the tag (`#hidden`, `tag:secret`).
- At phone width, the search field hides behind a "Search" button and shows one chip (truncated with an ellipsis, never cut off) with the rest under "+N"; wider screens show three. While a chip shows, the phone field drops its placeholder (`/?q=<marker>` at 390 px shows most of the marker, not two letters).
- A folder's card count and its "More for …" button never overlap: a mouse hides the button until hover; otherwise (touch, or no pointer, as in headless Chromium) the count sits to the button's left.
- `g t` is Trash, so Tags is `g l`.
- "Hide related" lasts while the page stays open; it isn't saved.
- Undo isn't offered for a tag merge or delete: no record is kept of the cards it changed.
