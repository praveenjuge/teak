# Find

A user finds cards again by typing in the search box, narrowing with qualifiers that become removable chips, filtering by type or sort in the View menu, or browsing Favorites, Archive, Trash, Tags and folders. The command palette jumps to views, folders, cards and commands.

## Sub-features

- `find-search` filters the grid live as you type, keeping the query in `?q=` (at most 500 characters: the box stops taking more, and the URL never cuts what you typed).
- `find-word-match` lists a card that holds the typed word as a word match, not one "related by meaning", with or without qualifiers: `tag:recipes soup` and `is:fav soup` mark no soup card `matchedByMeaning`, though both also match by meaning. A strong meaning match that holds only some of the typed words stays `matchedByMeaning` and listed, though the word index also found it by one word.
- `find-slash` focuses the search box with `/`, and on Tags the "Find a tag" box.
- `find-qualifiers` turns `tag:`, `type:`, `is:`, `site:`, `folder:` and quoted phrases into chips, through the "Search suggestions" list or Enter. `type:` also takes everyday words (`note`/`notes` → Text, `pdf`/`doc` → Document, `photo`/`picture` → Image, `color`/`colour` → Palette, `voice`/`memo` → Audio, `url`/`bookmark` → Link, `movie` → Video), and a `type:` word that names no type shows a dashed chip "No type named banana". A tag chip in a card's dialog applies the same `tag:"…"` filter (never a keyword search) and closes the card, and the URL drops `?card=`.
- `find-enter` turns a plain word into a keyword chip (a search icon, not `#`) on Enter; color, style and time words become their own filters, kept in the URL (`?color=blue,ff5733&style=minimal&date=This+Week`) through a reload and Back; `fav`, `trash` and `archive` navigate. A time phrase before or after other words splits off (`kestrel last week` → a date chip and "kestrel"), but a lone month, year or number beside words stays a word. A URL's `q` loads as the same chips Enter makes, in the URL's order (`/?q=verify-navx -heron` → "verify-navx", "−heron", with an empty box).
- `find-clear` clears with "Clear all", a chip's remove button, Backspace (the chip just left of the cursor, the last one shown), or "Clear filters" ("Clear search" when the search is only typed words, with no chips).
- `find-view` uses the View menu: card types (a `type:` qualifier becomes a checked type, and checking another adds to it), sort (Newest, Oldest, Shuffle, Revisit), "Density" ("Comfortable", or "Compact": tighter gaps and up to seven columns, kept in this browser through a reload) and "Save as smart folder…". A smart folder lists exactly the cards its saved search lists. A search keeps "Newest first" picked over "Best match" through a reload (`&sort=newest`). In Revisit, Space on a focused button presses that button; on the deck it opens details.
- `find-saved-order` orders "Newest first" and "Oldest first" by when a card was last saved, and time words such as "yesterday" find it by that date too, in the library, Favorites, Archive, Trash, folders, tags, sites and search: a card first saved last year and saved again yesterday leads Newest first.
- `find-results` shows how many cards a search or smart folder found above the grid ("5 cards match", "50+ cards match" while more can load), and how many only share its meaning ("· 12 related by meaning"), with "Hide related" / "Show related" to leave those out. A card that holds the search's words is never counted as related, however close its meaning, so "Hide related" never hides it.
- `find-nav` covers the sidebar views (Favorites, Archive, Trash), Tags, folders and smart folders. A folder's tab is titled with its name ("Reading list · Teak", and the new name after a rename); a missing folder ("Folder not found") has no search box or View menu. At 390 px a smart folder shows its search as chips under the header. Tags keeps its "Find a tag" text in the URL (`/tags?q=des`); its merge picker moves with the arrows, Enter picks the highlighted tag and a second Enter merges; a rename's toast names the tag as Tags lists it (lowercase). The sidebar lists "Everything", "Favorites" and "Tags", then the folders (each with a folder icon in its color, and its card count), with "Archive", "Trash" and "Settings" at its foot. Its header button "Collapse sidebar" is always shown and folds it to an icon rail, where the same button reads "Keep sidebar open"; hovering the rail never widens it, only that button reopens it; the page header's "Toggle sidebar" exists only on phones, where it opens the drawer.
- `find-header` keeps every library page's header in the same three parts at 640 px and wider: the title on the left, the search in a fixed middle slot (the same place on Everything, Trash, Tags and folders), and labelled actions on the right ("View", "Sort: Most used", "Empty Trash").
- `find-palette` covers the command palette. With two or more characters typed it always offers "Search all for “…”", even when no card matches; its list's accessible name ("Suggestions") follows the UI language.
- `find-empty` shows each view's empty state, and for a search "Nothing found for “<words>”" ("Nothing found matching your filters" when only filters are set), "Did you mean <word>?" built from your own tags, folder names and card titles, and, with two or more filters, a button per filter ("Remove −heron") to drop just that one.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- The combobox in every view's header: "Search everything", "Search favorites", "Search archive", "Search Trash", or "Search <folder>".
- Press `/`. Press Ctrl+K (⌘K) for the palette.
- Sidebar links "Everything", "Favorites", "Tags", "Archive", "Trash", and folder links by folder name.
- Key sequences `g e`, `g f`, `g a`, `g t` (Trash), `g l` (Tags), `g s`.

## Driving it with e2e MCP

Preconditions:

- Signed in, with a card holding this run's marker (make one with [capture](./capture.md) or the API).
- Search by a marker that isn't a color, style, time or command word.

Steps:

- **Search.** `press` "/" (focus outside a field). The combobox is focused. `type` the marker. The URL gains `?q=` and the marked cards are listed. On the dev deployment related seeded cards can also appear by meaning; judge the step by the marked cards, not by an empty remainder.
- **Word match with a qualifier.** `navigate` to `/?q=tag%3Arecipes%20soup` and wait about 10 s for the meaning matches. "Text: Recipe idea: roasted tomato soup…" and "Link: Tomato soup - Wikipedia" are listed. The grid doesn't show the mark, so read it from the page's Convex WebSocket frames (Playwright `page.on("websocket")`, `framereceived`): in the last `Transition` whose value has a `page` of cards, neither card has `matchedByMeaning: true`. Do the same for `is:fav soup` (only the recipe card) and `tag:design bauhaus` ("Link: Bauhaus - Wikipedia").
- **Some of the words.** Make two cards through the API: "<run-id> espresso tamping guide: pull a double shot of espresso with an even tamp" and "<run-id> cappuccino guide: steam the milk and pour a latte art heart over a double shot". Wait a minute for their embeddings, since a search sent earlier caches an empty meaning ranking for 10 minutes. `navigate` to `/?q=<run-id>%20cappuccino` and wait about 15 s. Both cards are listed. In the frames, the espresso card (which holds only the run id) has `matchedByMeaning: true` and the cappuccino card has none.
- **No match.** `type` "tag:zzzz-no-match-<run-id>" with `replace: true`. The text "Nothing found matching your filters" and a button "Clear filters" appear. A typed nonsense word that finds nothing offers "Clear search" instead. Use a `tag:` qualifier: a plain nonsense word still matches cards by meaning on the dev deployment (see Gotchas).
- **Did you mean.** Tag a marked card `kestrel-<run-id>`, then search `#zzzz-no-match-<run-id> kestral-<run-id>` and `press` "Enter". The empty state reads "Nothing found for “…”", offers "Did you mean …kestrel-<run-id>…?" (a button) and one "Remove …" button per chip; `tap` "Remove #zzzz…" (or the suggestion) and only that chip goes.
- **Result count.** Search the marker and `press` "Enter": the status above the grid reads "N cards match", plus "· M related by meaning" when the server added meaning matches. `tap` "Hide related": only the word matches stay and the button reads "Show related".
- **Clear.** `tap` button "Clear all" (the X in the field). The query empties and the full grid returns.
- **Keyword chip.** `type` the marker and `press` "Enter". The input empties and a chip button "Remove <marker>" appears (lowercased). Proof that it persists: `navigate` to the current URL; the chip is still there.
- **Tag chip in a card.** Tag a marked note `verify tag 🎉 with spaces` ([card-actions](./card-actions.md), "Tags"), then in its dialog `tap` the chip's name "verify tag 🎉 with spaces". The dialog closes, the search shows the chip "Remove tag verify tag 🎉 with spaces" (the same as typing `tag:"verify tag 🎉 with spaces"`), only cards with that exact tag are listed, and the URL has `q=tag:"…"` and no `card=`. Reload: the dialog stays closed.
- **Qualifier.** `type` "type:quote" and `press` "Enter". The chip "Remove type Quote" appears and only "Quote:" cards remain. `tap` the chip to remove it. `type` "type:notes" and Enter: the chip is "Remove type Text". `type` "type:banana" and Enter: a dashed chip "No type named banana" (accessible name "Remove type:banana: no such card type").
- **Date beside words.** `type` "<marker> last week" and `press` "Enter": chips "Remove <marker>" and "Remove Last Week", and the URL gains `date=Last+Week`.
- **URL search.** `navigate` to `/?q=<marker>%20-heron`: the box is empty, the chips read "<marker>" then "−heron", and the URL keeps that order.
- **Backspace.** From `/?tag=design`, `type` the marker and `press` "Enter": chips "Remove tag filter design" then "Remove <marker>". `press` "Backspace" in the empty box: only "Remove <marker>" goes; the tag chip stays.
- **Type qualifier and View.** `navigate` to `/`, `type` "type:quote", `press` "Enter". The URL becomes `/?type=quote`, the chip is "Remove type Quote", and the View menu has "Quote" checked. Check "Palette": the URL becomes `/?type=quote,palette` and quotes and palettes are both listed (not "Nothing found").
- **Colors and dates in the URL.** `navigate` to `/`, `type` "blue" and `press` "Enter", then `type` "this week" and `press` "Enter". The URL becomes `/?color=blue&date=This+Week` with chips "Remove This Week" and "Remove Blue". `navigate` to that URL again: both chips are still there. `tap` "Remove Blue", then go `back`: "Remove Blue" is back.
- **Suggestions.** `type` "ty". Listbox "Search suggestions" opens with options such as "type:". `press` "Escape" to close only the list.
- **View menu.** `tap` the button whose name starts "View" (it shows the word "View" beside its icon at 640 px and wider). The menu has checkboxes "Text" … "Quote", "Show all types", radios "Newest first", "Oldest first", "Shuffle", "Revisit", "Density" radios "Comfortable" and "Compact", and "Save as smart folder…". Pick "Compact": at 1440 px the grid shows up to seven columns with smaller gaps; reload and it stays compact (it lives in `localStorage` `teak.compactGrid`). Pick "Comfortable" to undo. `tap` "Image": the URL gains `?type=image`, the chip "Remove type Image" appears and the button becomes "View: Image". `press` "Escape", then remove the chip.
- **Search sort survives a reload.** `navigate` to `/?q=design`; the View menu has "Best match" checked. Pick "Newest first": the URL becomes `/?q=design&sort=newest`. `navigate` to that URL again and open the View menu: "Newest first" is still checked.
- **Saved order.** Seed the `ordering-saved` pack from `packages/convex`: `bunx convex run devSeed:seedScenario '{"workosUserId":"<id>","scenario":"ordering-saved"}'`, with the id from `.agents/.state/dev-account.json` (it needs this checkout's backend pushed). `navigate` to `/?tag=scenario-ordering-saved`: the cards read "Resaved: first saved 400 days ago, saved again yesterday", "Two days…", "Last month…", "Two years…". Pick "Oldest first" in the View menu: "Two years…" comes first and "Resaved…" last. Back on "Newest first", `type` "yesterday" and `press` "Enter": only "Resaved…" remains, though it was first saved 400 days ago. (Avoid "last week": "Two days…" falls in it only early in the week.) The folder "Saved again", `site:ordering-saved.example.com`, Favorites (with the tag) and Archive ("Archived: … saved again 3 days ago" before "Archived: saved 10 days ago…") keep the same order, and so does Trash. Screenshot each to `$E/saved-order-*.png`, then permanently delete the pack's cards and the folder.
- **Revisit keys.** From the View menu pick "Revisit": dialog "Revisit" with "1 of N", and under the card its type and "Added <date>" (the date it was added). `press` "Space" on button "Keep": the counter reads "2 of N", "Keep" keeps focus and no card dialog opens. The deck has one `role="status"` that reads "1 of N", then "2 of N" (and "All caught up" at the end), no `aria-live` around the card, and the page keeps one `main`. Keep, not Archive, so the run changes no card.
- **Views.** `tap` link "Favorites". Heading "Favorites", the tab title "Favorites · Teak" (in the UI language, as are Archive, Trash and Tags), combobox "Search favorites", and the seeded favorites (including the "Welcome to Teak…" card) are listed. Do the same for "Archive" (empty state "Nothing archived. Archive a card to keep it out of your library.") and "Trash", which the seeded vault starts with one card in: "Text: Old draft I meant to throw away."
- **Tags.** `tap` link "Tags" (or press `g l`). Searchbox "Find a tag", the button "Sort: Most used" (labelled on screen too), and links named "<tag>, N cards" are listed, three to a row at 1280 px and wider. `press` "/": "Find a tag" is focused. `type` part of a tag: the URL becomes `/tags?q=<text>` and a reload keeps the filter. `tap` a tag: the URL becomes `/?tag=<tag>` with the chip "Remove tag filter <tag>", even when a search was open on Everything before. Go `back` once: it lands on `/tags`.
- **Tag merge and rename.** On a marked tag, "More for <tag>" → "Merge into…": combobox "Find a tag" is focused; `press` "ArrowDown" (the highlight moves) and "Enter": the option is selected and the button reads "Merge into “<tag>”"; Enter again merges. "Rename…" to `Verify-<RUN>-T1`: the toast reads "Renamed “…” to “verify-<run>-t1”". The dialog's "Close" X sits in the dialog's own corner.
- **Sidebar.** At 1440 px, the links run "Teak home", "Everything", "Favorites", "Tags", "Moodboard", "Start here", then "Archive", "Trash", "Settings", and no hover badges sit beside them (go-to keys are in the tooltips). `tap` "Collapse sidebar": the rail keeps "Keep sidebar open", "New" and every link as an icon, and the page header has no "Toggle sidebar". `hover` "Everything" in the rail and wait a second: the rail stays icon-wide with no labels. `navigate` to the same URL: it stays collapsed. `tap` "Keep sidebar open" to restore it. At 390 px, `tap` "Toggle sidebar": the drawer opens with the same links and no collapse button.
- **Header slot.** At 1440 px, the search field's left edge is the same on `/`, `/trash`, `/tags` and a folder (screenshot each header); "Empty Trash" sits after "View" without moving the search.
- **Folder.** `tap` link "Start here". The URL becomes `/f/<id>`, the combobox reads "Search Start here" and the tab title is "Start here · Teak". `navigate` to `/f/doesnotexist`: heading "Folder not found" (level 1, in the header), "It may have been deleted." with "Go to Everything", the tab reads "Folder not found · Teak", and there is no combobox and no "View" button.
- **Smart folder.** `tap` link "Moodboard", a sibling of "Start here" in the sidebar. The URL becomes `/f/<id>`, its saved query (`type:image type:palette`) shows as locked chips with an "Edit search" button, and only image and palette cards are listed. At 390 px the same chips show under the header (list "Search: …"). Search `<marker> -heron`, "Save as smart folder…", open it: it lists the same cards the search did, with the same "N cards match".
- **Transcript.** `type` "crowded", a word only the seeded voice memo's transcript holds. Only "Audio: Brand refresh voice memo.m4a" is listed. `tap` it: dialog "Brand refresh voice memo.m4a" shows "Transcript" with "Quick note for the studio brand refresh…". Proof: `navigate` to `/?card=<id>`; the transcript is still there.
- **Palette.** `press` "Control+k". Dialog "Command palette" with combobox "Search commands, folders and cards". `type` the marker: a "Cards" group lists the card. `press` "Enter": the card's dialog opens and the URL gains `?card=<id>`. Reopen it and `type` "kestral-zzzz": the "Cards" group still has "Search all for “kestral-zzzz”", and in Settings → Language → Deutsch the list's accessible name is "Vorschläge", not cmdk's English "Suggestions".

## Gotchas

- Enter changes the query: a plain word becomes a chip and the input empties. "orange" becomes a color filter, "minimal" a style, "today" a time; "fav", "trash" and "archive" navigate.
- The dev deployment also searches by meaning, so even a nonsense word can show cards: "zzzz-no-match-…" matched the seeded "Document: Teak search cheat sheet.pdf". For a no-match check use a qualifier (`tag:zzzz-no-match-<run-id>`), which matches exactly. Empty results wait up to 1.5 s before the empty state.
- A card made through the API reaches server search after a short delay. If the UI misses it, search again after a few seconds before calling it a bug.
- Processing can rename a card ("Text: …" to "Palette: …", or a link's title), so match by marker, not by full name.
- Home and search results stay mounted, hidden, behind folder pages, so text can appear twice in the DOM. Trust what `observe` lists as visible.
- Cards tagged "secret" or "hidden" appear only when the search names the tag (`#hidden`, `tag:secret`).
- At phone width, the search field hides behind a "Search" button, and the field shows one chip (truncated with an ellipsis, never cut off) with the rest under "+N"; wider screens show three.
- `g t` is Trash, so Tags is `g l` (as Gmail's go to label).
- "Hide related" lasts while the page stays open; it isn't saved.
- Undo isn't offered for a tag merge or delete: a tag operation keeps no record of which cards it changed.
