# Card actions

A user opens a card into a dialog to edit its notes, tags and folders, favorites, archives, trashes, restores or deletes it forever, or selects several for bulk actions. Every change shows in the matching view after a reload.

## Sub-features

- `card-open` opens a card from the grid, a deep link or the palette.
- `card-code-preview` shows uploaded source files with syntax colors on a dark background, keeping text and line breaks, never executing markup.
- `card-dialog-layout` gives the dialog no header; on wide screens everything sits at the top of "Card details": a line with the site and the type button ("Link", which shows every card of that type, with "Saved" at its end after an edit), the title (textbox "Title" for a link, wrapping to three lines), one main button ("Open" for a link, "Download" for a file) and a row of captioned buttons: "Favorite", the done button (links only: "Mark as read", "Mark as watched"…, captioned "Done" so it isn't mistaken for the reader's "Read"; once done, pressed and "Marked as read"), "Copy link" or "Copy text" ("Copy"), "Add to folder…" ("Folder", the dialog's only add-to-folder button; a card in a folder gets section "Folders" above "Tags" with the chip "In <folder>. Change folders", never among the tags) and "More actions" ("More"). Once that row scrolls out of "Card details", a one-line copy stays pinned at the panel's top. "More actions" holds only "Download" (a link with a file), "Save PDF", "More like this", "Refresh details", "Pin to top" (or "Unpin"; see [pinning](./pinning.md)), "Archive" and "Move to Trash". "Previous card", "Next card" and "Close" sit outside the card on wide screens, at one spot whatever the card's size, and every quick click moves exactly one card. Image and video cards fill the stage, uncovered; the image is named by the card's text, Teak's description or its file name, never just "Image". The **Preview · Read** toggle fills the picked one in the text colour. "Added …" is said once: a note shows it with the time on its top line (the dialog's description, "Added today at 1:42 PM"), with no "Added" row in "Details"; a link's title is announced only by its textbox "Title". On phones the title tops the stacked details, the header has only "Close" and the save status, the bottom bar ("Card actions") has the arrows, "Favorite", "More actions" (also holding favorite, done, "Add to folder…" and copy) and the main action, and a one-line note is only as tall as its text, so "Tags" follows right under it.
- `card-details-always` keeps "Card details" beside the card on wide screens: no "Hide details", and `i` does nothing.
- `card-photo-details` lists a photo's camera facts under "More details" in "Card details": Camera, Taken, Exposure, Aperture, Focal length and ISO, never its location. "Taken" is the camera clock's time in every viewer's zone, plus the offset ("UTC+5:30") when recorded. Photos processed before Teak stored the clock time still read "Taken" in the viewer's zone.
- `card-saved-again` shows "Saved again" under "Added" in "Card details" when the link was saved again over a minute after it was added ("Added" keeps the first date). Both share a date format and follow the UI language ("Erneut gespeichert" in Deutsch). The re-save adds no "Edited" row.
- `card-notes` adds notes, which save on blur. A note (text card) has no "Notes" field unless it already has notes (from the API).
- `card-tags` adds and removes tags; a typed tag is lowercased (dialog, grid "Add tags…" and bulk bar), and one differing only in case from an existing tag is "Already tagged"; "secret" and "hidden" hide the card. Tags Teak added fold under "N suggested tags", where "Keep tag …" keeps one and "Remove Teak tag …" drops it.
- `card-folders` adds the card to a folder with "Add to folder…" (the card's right-click and hover menus, the dialog and the bulk bar). Its dialog "Folders" searches as you type: a new name offers "Create folder “<name>”", an existing one (any case) offers none, and at the 500-folder limit it offers none and says "You can have up to 500 folders", as the bulk bar's picker does. Inside a manual folder, those menus also have "Remove from this folder" (toast "Removed 1 card from <folder>" with "Undo"); smart folders and other views don't.
- `card-dialog-size` opens a short note (≤280 characters, ≤6 lines) in a smaller dialog (896×480 at 1440×900) and a longer one in the full reading dialog (1152×832). The size is chosen on open and holds while the text grows. The text keeps clear of its "Focus" button.
- `card-dialog-media-loading` pulses a placeholder in an image's box, or over a PDF's frame, until it loads.
- `card-audio-length` shows an audio card's length the same on its tile and in its player: whole seconds rounded down, with hours past an hour (the seeded 17.8-second "Brand refresh voice memo.m4a" reads 0:17; a 75-minute recording 1:15:00).
- `card-dialog-media-resume` resumes a video or audio card at the same moment, still playing, when its signed link expires mid-playback.
- `grid-video-release` plays a video card's clip on hover (one without a scrub sprite) and removes it about two seconds after the pointer leaves, so a sweep doesn't leave one loaded per card. A card with no still keeps its clip as its picture.
- `card-copy` copies the same thing from the grid and the open card (`cardCopyKind` in `lib/cardCopy.ts`): an image its picture ("Copy image"; an SVG, which the clipboard can't hold, copies its link if it has one), anything else with a link its link ("Copy link"), a note or quote its text ("Copy text"), and a card in Trash nothing. In the grid the key is ⌘C on a Mac and Ctrl+C elsewhere (the other platform's key does nothing); in the open card, `c`. From the card itself (right-click or hover menu, or ⌘C / Ctrl+C on the focused card) a note reads "Copied to clipboard"; a refused first write (the page lost focus) is retried once and still reads "Copied to clipboard", never "Link copied"; only two failures read "Failed to copy to clipboard". The dialog's "Copy text" ("Copied text"), "Copy link" ("Copied link") and the bulk bar's "Copy links" ("Copied 1 link") write once, so a refusal there reads "Failed to copy to clipboard" and "Couldn't copy the links".
- `card-favorite` toggles favorite from the grid or the dialog.
- `card-menu-keys` runs a menu item's listed shortcut when pressed while the menu is open, and closes the menu: a card's right-click and hover "More actions" menus (`F`, `T`, `E`, `X`, Delete or Backspace, ⌘C/Ctrl+C), the open dialog's "More actions" (`P`, `E`, Delete or Backspace, and on phones `F`, `D` and `C`), the bulk bar's "More actions" (`F`, `T`, Delete) and the header's "New" menu (`N`, `U`, `R`). Each passes `runMenuItemShortcut` (`lib/menuShortcuts.ts`) as its content's `onKeyDown`; the shadcn primitives don't. Every key a card menu, the dialog, the grid and the shortcuts list show comes from `lib/cardActions.ts`.
- `card-touch` keeps a card's "More actions" shown at its top right where there's no hover (`(pointer: coarse)`: phones, tablets), with a favorite's heart beside it and "Open link", "Favorite" and "Select" inside that menu. Folder rows' "More for <name>" and Tags rows' "More for <tag>" stay shown too, and menus show no key letters. With a mouse they appear on hover or focus.
- `card-keyboard-focus` shows a card's hover buttons ("Select", "Favorite", "More actions", "Open link") while it has keyboard focus, and lists its keys in `aria-keyshortcuts`: "Enter F P T E Delete X" ("Backspace" for Delete on a Mac), plus "Control+C" ("Meta+C" on a Mac) for a text, link or image card, which copies; "Enter Delete X" for a card in Trash; "Enter F P T E Delete" in the Pinned row, which has no selection.
- `card-archive` archives and unarchives.
- `card-trash` moves a card to Trash with "Undo": "Moved 1 card to Trash" from the grid, a key, the bulk bar or the dialog.
- `card-restore` restores from Trash ("Restored 1 card").
- `card-delete-forever` deletes from Trash for good ("1 card permanently deleted").
- `empty-trash` deletes every card in Trash for good from "Empty Trash" at the top of `/trash` (or the command palette there), after a confirmation stating the count; disabled while Trash is empty.
- `edit-link` edits a link's address from "Edit link…" in the grid's right-click menu or the pencil "Edit link…" beside "URL" in "Card details"; the preview refreshes from the new address.
- `link-image` replaces a link's preview image with "Replace image" (captioned "Replace") in the "Image" row of "Card details"; "Reset to original image" ("Reset") restores it.
- `card-bulk` selects several cards for the bulk action bar. Its main button ("Archive", "Unarchive", "Restore") is grey, not red; only "Add to <folder>" while picking cards for a folder is red. In selection mode ⌘A (Ctrl+A off a Mac; not the other platform's key) selects every card shown, not the page's text, and a card selected with `x` keeps the red selected ring while focused.
- `trash-bulk` selects cards in `/trash` too (hover "Select", the menu's "Select", or `x`): the bar offers "Restore" and "Delete forever" (or Delete), which asks "Delete N cards forever?" first. A line under Trash's header says "Cards in Trash are deleted forever after 30 days."
- `menu-release` never runs a right-click menu's item on its own: releasing the right button over an item under the pointer (or any release as the menu opens) does nothing; a real click still picks it.
- `dialog-escape` closes the card dialog with one Escape, even while a tooltip shows.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- `tap` a card button ("Text: …", "Link: …", "Quote: …", "Palette: …", "Image: …"), or Enter on it.
- `/?card=<id>` opens the dialog directly.
- Each card's hover buttons ("Favorite", "More actions", "Select"), or `right_click` for the same menu.
- Keys on a focused card, or in a menu that lists them: `f` favorite, `e` archive, `t` tags, `x` select, Delete moves to Trash.
- Keys in the open dialog: `o` (or ⌘↵) opens the link or downloads the file, `c` copies the link or text, `d` marks a link done, `n` notes, `t` tags.
- `/archive` and `/trash`.

## Driving it with e2e MCP

Preconditions:

- Signed in, with a text card holding this run's marker ([capture](./capture.md)), found by a marker search ([find](./find.md)).
- Act on marked cards only; undo anything done to a seeded card.

Steps:

- **Open.** `tap` "Text: verify-<run-id> …". Dialog "Note" opens, the URL gains `?card=<id>`, with "Previous card", "Next card" and "Close" outside the card and no header. "Card details" starts with the type button "Note" and "Added today at <time>" (or "yesterday", or the date) on one line (no visible title), then "Favorite", "Copy text", "Add to folder…" and "More actions", captioned "Favorite", "Copy", "Folder" and "More". No "Notes" field, no "Add to a folder" button beside "Add a tag", "Added …" once in the accessibility tree, and no "Added" row in "Details".
- **Sidebar for a link.** Open the seeded "Bauhaus" link: "Card details" starts with "en.wikipedia.org · Link", textbox "Title", button "Open", then "Favorite", "Mark as read", "Copy link", "Add to folder…" and "More actions". Scroll down to "Details": a one-line bar with "Open" and the same buttons stays pinned at the top until you scroll back up. `tap` "Add to folder…": dialog "Folders"; Escape returns to the card, keys working.
- **Rapid next.** Click the "Next card" button's center point 25 times quickly (or `press` "ArrowRight"): the dialog stays open, the button never moves, and `?card=` lands 25 cards on (or on the last, where "Next card" is disabled). `?` opens "Keyboard shortcuts" over the card; Escape returns to the card, keys working.
- **Right then left.** With five marked notes, open `/?q=verify-<run-id>&sort=oldest` (relevance order can move an edited card to the end), open the middle one, type a few letters, then while the save check runs send ArrowRight and ArrowLeft in one task (`page.evaluate` dispatching two bubbling `keydown` events on `[role="dialog"]`; arrows do nothing while the note's text has focus). `?card=` ends on the starting card, never one before, and the text is saved: right presses minus left presses is how far the card moves.
- **Details stay.** `i` changes nothing; "More actions" has no "Hide details".
- **Source file preview.** Drop `packages/tests/src/web/preview-fixtures/code-preview.ts` onto Everything. Open its card: blank lines and indentation stay, on a dark background with syntax colors, and the `<script>` string shows as text, never runs. Once the URL has `?card=…`, reload: text, line breaks and colors remain. `src/web/file-code-preview.e2e.ts` checks the same locally.
- **Photo details.** Upload a JPEG `verify-<run-id>-photo.jpg` with an EXIF capture time ([capture](./capture.md)); with Pillow: `img = Image.new("RGB", (640, 480))`, `exif = img.getexif()`, set `exif[0x010F] = "Teak Camera Co"` (Make) and `exif[0x0110] = "Verify One"` (Model), then in `ifd = exif.get_ifd(0x8769)` set `ifd[0x9003] = "2026:05:17 14:30:05"` (DateTimeOriginal), `ifd[0x8827] = 400` (ISO) and, for the offset, `ifd[0x9011] = "+05:30"`; save with `img.save(path, "JPEG", exif=exif.tobytes())`. After processing, open "Image: verify-<run-id>-photo.jpg" and `tap` "More details": rows "Camera" ("Teak Camera Co Verify One"), "Taken" ("May 17, 2026, 02:30 PM", plus " UTC+5:30" with the offset) and "ISO" ("400"). Proof: reopen it from a browser in another zone (Playwright `timezoneId: "America/Los_Angeles"` or `"Pacific/Auckland"`, `apps/docs/content/docs/(open-source)/development.mdx`, "Cloud sessions in detail"): "Taken" stays 02:30 PM while "Added" moves with the zone. An older photo (`fileMetadata.exif` with `takenAt` but no `takenAtClock`) still moves with the zone (07:30 AM in Los Angeles). Delete the card forever afterwards.
- **Saved again.** Seed `ordering-saved` ([find](./find.md)), search `#scenario-ordering-saved` and open the link "Resaved: first saved 400 days ago, saved again yesterday". "Card details" shows "Added" 400 days ago, then "Saved again" yesterday, and no "Edited"; the "Two days" link shows only "Added". For a real save, `POST /v1/cards` with the link of a card added over a minute ago ([api-cli-mcp](./api-cli-mcp.md), `api-resave`): "Saved again" reads that save's time. In Settings → Appearance → Language → Deutsch the row reads "Erneut gespeichert"; switch back after.
- **Notes.** `type` "verify-<run-id> note" into textbox "Notes", then `press` "Tab" to blur. The top line of "Card details" ends in "Saved" for about two seconds (slow: "Saving…", failed: "Couldn't save"), in one `role="status"` region present, empty, before any save, so screen readers hear each change. Proof: `navigate` to `/?card=<id>`; "Notes" shows the text.
- **Leave right after typing.** In a marked note, type a few characters and within a second leave without blurring (`tap` the type button "Text", a tag chip, or go Back): reopened, the text is there. Type again and reload at once: "Leave site?" (`beforeunload`); staying keeps the edit and it saves.
- **Tags.** `type` "verify-<run-id>-tag" into textbox "Add a tag", `press` "Enter": button "Remove tag verify-<run-id>-tag" appears. Proof: it survives a reload, and `tag:verify-<run-id>-tag` finds the card.
- **Quick tag edits.** Enter three tags back to back (`verify-<run-id>-t1`, `-t2`, `-t3`): all three chips appear at once and survive a reload. In the empty box, `press` "Backspace" twice quickly: after a reload only `-t1` is left. `VERIFY-<run-id>-T1` + Enter adds nothing, clears the box and toasts "Already tagged #verify-<run-id>-t1".

- **Favorite.** In the dialog, `tap` "Favorite": it becomes "Unfavorite" (pressed). Proof: link "Favorites" lists it. Undo with "Unfavorite".

- **Same action, every surface.** Favorite one marked note from its right-click menu, the grid's `f`, the dialog's "Favorite" and the bulk bar ("Select", "More actions" → "Favorite"): the heart shows at once each time, and every surface agrees after a reload. - **Touch.** New context with `viewport: 820×1180, hasTouch: true, isMobile: true` (`matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` true). Every card shows "More actions" (opacity 1), no "Favorite" or "Open link"; a favorite's heart sits to its left. Its menu: "Select", "Add tags…", "Add to folder…", "Favorite", "Pin to top", "Refresh details", "Archive", "Move to Trash", with no key letters. The sidebar's "More for Start here" and `/tags` rows' "More for <tag>" are visible. At 1440 px with a mouse all are opacity 0 until hover.

## Gotchas

- Every card, the dialog and the bulk bar have a "More actions"; scope to the dialog or the right-clicked card.
- Hover buttons are invisible and untabbable until hovered; right-click instead.
- The dialog focuses itself, so letters act at once: `f` favorites, `e` archives, `c` copies, `o` opens the link in a new tab, Delete deletes. Click into a field before typing.
- "Mark as …" is a link's second action-row button (captioned "Done"; named "Mark as read", "Mark as watched"…), or `d`; on a phone it is in "More actions".
- Once the action row scrolls away, its buttons exist twice (row and pinned bar). Scope to the pinned bar, or scroll back up, before tapping by name.
- Toasts are brief; check the state (views, search, reload) too.
- Only "Delete forever" and "Empty Trash" are permanent; the Delete key moves to Trash. "Empty Trash" empties the whole account's Trash, hidden and seeded cards included: never press it on a shared account without asking.
- "Restored 1 card" and "1 card permanently deleted" (grid or dialog) can arrive just after the card leaves the grid.
- Leave Trash's seeded card, "Old draft I meant to throw away.", there.
- The grid's tag dialog (right-click → "Add tags…", or `t`) reads the card, not the list: on `/?tag=<tag>`, removing that tag empties the grid behind it, yet the dialog keeps the card's other tags and the next tag joins them.
- Adding the tag "secret" or "hidden" hides the card ("Hidden. Search #secret to see it.", with "Undo").
- A card holds at most 100 tags of up to 64 characters. A longer tag + Enter shows the alert "Each tag must be at most 64 characters" under "Add a tag" (`aria-invalid="true"`, described by it), keeps the text and adds nothing; typing clears it. At 100 tags it says "A card can have at most 100 tags" the same way. Neither toasts. A long tag saved before the limit stays and doesn't block other edits. Tags → "Rename…" says "Tags can be at most 64 characters" before you save.
- On dev, real AI adds its own "Remove Teak tag …" chips; don't mistake them for yours.
- The e2e MCP browser runs in UTC, where a "Taken" time-zone bug can't show; use a Playwright context with `timezoneId`.
- The Core Worker fixture `fixture-exif.jpg` isn't a decodable image ("Uploaded file is not a decodable image"); upload a real JPEG.
- Escape closes the dialog and returns focus to the card; in selection mode it clears the selection first.
- Tile previews strip Markdown before cutting, and a heading or list item ends a sentence: the seeded "Welcome to Teak…" note reads "Save anything. Paste a link, …" and never ends in a stray `*`.
- Closing a card opened from the grid goes Back, adding no history: from `/settings`, open Everything, open and close two cards, and one Back lands on `/settings`. A card reached by a link, or a close that changes the search (a tag, a type, "More like this"), replaces the URL.
