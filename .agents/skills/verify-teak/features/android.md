# Android app

The native Android app (`Teak Dev` in Debug) shows the same vault as the web: a Home grid with search, filter chips (Favorites, Archive, Trash, Done, To do, types, color), a sort menu, folders and smart folders, card details, Revisit, a Tags manager, and a share sheet that saves links, text and files. A Debug build can sign in to the local E2E stack as a fresh emulator account, so every recipe here runs without a real WorkOS login.

## Sub-features

- `android-sign-in` signs the Debug build in to the local E2E stack with `apps/android/scripts/e2e-session.ts`.
- `android-note-tables` shows a note's GFM tables read-only with a bold header row, scrolling a wide one sideways, with `[[` pills working in cells; aligned, ragged and listed tables stay literal monospaced text. Steps in [note-tables](./note-tables.md), **Native apps**.
- `android-tasks` shows `- [ ]` and `- [x]` items in an open note as checkboxes (done ones muted and struck through); tapping the row ticks that one item, moves it below the open ones as the web does, and saves the note, and the web shows it live. A failed save puts it back with "Couldn't update the checklist. Try again." Steps in [note-tables](./note-tables.md), **Native apps**.
- `android-sort` sorts Home by "Newest first", "Oldest first" or "Shuffle" (each Shuffle reshuffles).
- `android-archive` shows the Archive view, archives or unarchives from a tile's long-press menu, the detail overflow, the E key or the selection bar, with "Undo".
- `android-bulk` acts on selected cards: Archive or Unarchive, Add to favorites, Remove from favorites, Add tags…, Add to folder…, Move to Trash, Restore, Delete forever.
- `android-folders` lists folders and smart folders in the Folders tab, opens one, creates, renames, edits a smart folder's search, deletes with "Undo", and saves a search as a smart folder.
- `android-done` marks a link done from the button under its preview with the verb for its category, as on the web ("Mark as read" for a book, article or research link, "Mark as watched" for a film, "Mark as done" when the category is unknown), shows the check on tiles, and filters with the Done and To do chips (`is:done`, `is:todo`).
- `android-search` shows typed filters as chips ("Show Archived", "Price Under 50", "Text in image …", "Mood “…”", "Similar to …"), ranks free words and `like:`/`mood:` searches by meaning, and "More like this" from a tile or the detail overflow.
- `android-detail` shows "Refresh details", "Folders…", the "From example.com" row on text saved from a page, "Published", "Price" on product links, and camera rows (Camera, Taken, Exposure, Aperture, Focal length, ISO) on photos.
- `android-card-links` shows `[[` links as pills with live titles or "Missing card", and a "Linked from" section; Edit keeps the raw token.
- `android-hidden` shows "Hidden. Search #secret to see it." after adding a hidden tag in Edit, "Shows hidden cards" when searching one, and "Some cards are hidden…" on an empty view.
- `android-revisit` deals up to 20 shuffled cards from the current view: swipe or ← to archive, → to keep, Z to undo, and "All caught up" at the end.
- `android-tags` lists tags in Settings → Tags and renames, merges or deletes one, with progress.
- `android-share` saves a shared link or text, then offers "Add tags and a note"; left alone the sheet closes itself.
- `android-card-link-picker` opens a "Link a card" dropdown under a note's Edit field when `[[` is typed, searches as you type (`cards:searchCardLinkSuggestions`, as the web's menu: no Trash, not the note itself), and puts in `[[Title|teak:<id>]]` on a tap; Back, Esc, a tap outside or deleting the `[[` closes it.
- `android-empty-trash` offers "Empty Trash" in the Trash view's top bar (the Trash chip on phones; the same view the tablet rail opens), disabled when `cards:trashCount` is 0 (hidden cards count); the confirmation reads "This permanently deletes 3 cards and their files. This can't be undone." ("at least 500 cards" at the count's cap), calls `cards:emptyTrash`, and says "Deleting 3 cards forever"; the cards leave Trash on every client. The server's rate limit shows "Trash was emptied a few times just now. Try again in a minute."
- `android-link-edit` renames a link in the card's top bar (the title is a field; Done or leaving it saves `updateCardField metadataTitle`, and a blank title puts the old one back), and changes its address from More → "Edit link…": a non-link shows "URL must use the http or https scheme" without saving; a link saves through `updateCardField url` with "Link saved. The preview refreshes shortly."
- `android-suggested-tags` shows "Suggested by Teak" in an open card's Tags with Keep (tap the chip, `+`) and Remove (`×`) per tag Teak added and the card doesn't have; Keep writes the card's tags plus that tag, Remove uses `removeAiTag`; in Trash they show without either.
- `android-folder-errors` shows a smart folder's search error from the server in the app's language (the server sends folder errors as `INVALID_INPUT` with English words).
- `android-search-operators` sends `-`, `OR`, parentheses and `site:` as typed (no chips for a search with operators), lists suggestions under the search field ("Exclude" with `-tag:` … `-is:`, "Combine" with "OR · Either side matches", "Filters", "Types", "Show"), and searches the words beside a date ("figma last week" sends the date range and "figma"). See [search-operators](./search-operators.md).
- `android-rail` gives wide windows (width class Expanded, 840 dp and up) a navigation rail with Home, Favorites, Archive, Trash, Folders, Add and Settings. Favorites, Archive and Trash open the library as their own pages, titled with their name, with that filter fixed and its chip hidden; Clear only takes off the filters added on top. Home there leaves out the three chips too, unless one is still on. Compact and Medium widths keep the four tabs (Home, Folders, Add, Settings) and the chips; narrowing the window from one of the new pages goes back to Home.
- `android-wallpaper-colors` adds Settings → Appearance → "Wallpaper colors" (Material You), off by default: on, the app takes its colors from the wallpaper in light and dark; off, Teak's red. The choice survives a restart.
- `android-pdf-viewer` opens a PDF card's "View document" in the app: "Opening the PDF…", then its pages in a scrolling column, rendered at the screen's width, with a "Page 1 of N" pill that follows the scroll. The top bar's "Open in another app" hands the downloaded file to a PDF app (or the browser when there's none). A failed download shows "Couldn't open this PDF." with "Try again". Other documents still open in the browser.
- `android-tabs` keeps each tab as you left it: switching tabs keeps Home's search, filters and scroll, a note being written and a voice memo recording. Re-tapping a tab goes back to its first screen, except over a note or voice memo in progress. Back with the search field focused hides the keyboard, then closes the suggestions, before it leaves Home.
- `android-library-loading` keeps the cards on screen with a thin progress bar while a new search, filter or pull to refresh loads, instead of a full-screen spinner. Those kept cards can't be selected and have no long-press menu, and Select and Empty Trash stay off, until the new view's cards arrive. A page that fails to load further down shows "Couldn't load more cards." with "Try again", which asks for that page again without going back to the top.
- `android-palette-colors` adds and removes a palette card's colors in its detail: an "Add color" tile after the last swatch (hidden at 12 colors and in Trash) opens a sheet with a "Hex code" field, a live preview, "Pick a color" presets and "Add" (`updateCardField addColor`), which stays off with "Enter a hex code, such as #FF5500" or "#FB5607 is already in this palette" under the field; a saved color shows "Added #2A9D8F". Long-pressing a swatch offers "Remove color" (`removeColor`) while another color would stay, with "Removed #264653" and "Undo", which adds it back at the end. Tapping a swatch still copies it. A failed write says "Couldn't update the palette. Try again."
- `android-audio-seek` makes a voice memo's waveform a seek bar, as on the web: tap or drag to jump there, playing or paused; the time reads the length before playing, then "0:05 / 0:10". TalkBack reads "Seek", the position as "0:05 of 0:10", and adjusts it with the volume keys.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Home tab: the search field, the filter chips under it, and the top bar's Revisit, Sort and Select buttons. Long-press a tile for Select, Archive or Unarchive, and More like this.
- Folders tab: the "New folder" button (New folder, New smart folder) and each row's "More for <name>" menu. Home's Sort menu has "Save as smart folder…" while searching.
- A card: the done button under a link's preview; the floating toolbar's "More" menu (Archive, More like this, Folders…, Refresh details, Move to Trash); Edit.
- Settings → Library → Tags.
- Any app's Share → Teak Dev.
- Hardware keyboard on an open card: E archive, D done, R refresh details, F favorite. In Revisit: ← or A archive, → or K keep, Space open, Z undo, Esc close.

## Driving it with adb and the Debug build

Preconditions:

- JDK 17+ (`JAVA_HOME=/opt/homebrew/opt/openjdk@21` on a Mac), the Android SDK in `apps/android/local.properties`, and an emulator booted with `-gpu host` (software rendering is too slow to drive). Turn off stylus handwriting so typing lands: `adb shell settings put secure stylus_handwriting_enabled 0`.
- The E2E stack is up: `.agents/.state/stack.json` shows `mode: "e2e"` and `ready: true`.
- Install and sign in: from `apps/android`, `./gradlew :app:installDebug`; then from the repo root `bun --no-env-file run apps/android/scripts/e2e-session.ts`. It reverses the stack's ports onto the device, hands the app a fresh `e2e-android-…@example.org` account, restarts it, and prints that account's `workosUserId`. Run it again after an emulator restart only if the app shows the Welcome screen; port reverses must be redone after every restart (`adb reverse tcp:<port> tcp:<port>` for the stack's convex, convexSite, emulator, filesWorker and web ports).
- Seed from `packages/convex`: `env -u CONVEX_DEPLOY_KEY CONVEX_AGENT_MODE=anonymous bunx convex run devSeed:seed '{"workosUserId":"<id>"}'`, then `devSeed:seedScenario` with `archive-mix`, `smart-folders`, `done-states`, `hidden-mix`, `card-links`, `card-details`, `rich-sites`, `revisit-sizes`, `search-qualifiers`, `save-details` and `omnibox-titles`. Those packs put the account over the Free plan's 200 cards, so use a second account with only `devSeed:seed` for anything that creates cards (share, uploads).
- Find and tap controls by text or content description from `adb shell uiautomator dump`; type with `adb shell input text` (`%s` for spaces); capture with `adb exec-out screencap -p`. Dark theme: `adb shell cmd uimode night yes`.

Steps:

- **Sort.** Tap "Sort" → "Oldest first": old `rv…` notes come first and the button reads "Sort: oldest first". Pick "Shuffle" twice: the order changes each time. Pick "Newest first" to finish.
- **Archive.** Tap the "Archive" chip: the title is "Archive". Long-press "Archived design note" → "Unarchive": it leaves with "Returned 1 card to the library" and "Undo"; tap "Undo" (it stays 10 seconds): the card is back.
- **Bulk.** Search "Mix", tap "Select cards", pick two tiles, tap "More actions" → "Add tags…" → type `verify-<run-id>` → "Add tags": "Added tags to 2 cards". `tag:verify-<run-id>` shows a "Tag …" chip and only those cards. "Add to folder…" → type a new name → "Create folder “…”": "Added 2 cards to …".
- **Folders.** Folders tab: smart folders show their search under the name; open "Design links": only links tagged design. "More for <yours>" → "Rename…" → Save. "Delete" → "Deleted “…”. Its cards are still in your library." → "Undo" brings it back. Delete it again to clean up.
- **Done.** Search `is:todo`, open "A book with an ISBN-13" → "Mark as read": the button reads "Read", "Marked as read" with "Undo". Back in the grid it's gone from `is:todo`; `is:done ISBN` shows it with the check and the Done chip on. Press D on the card (or tap "Read") to put it back.
- **Refresh details.** On any card: More → "Refresh details" → "Refreshing details. The summary and tags update shortly."; press R right after → "This card is already refreshing. Try again in a minute."
- **More like this.** More → "More like this": Home shows the chip "Similar to <title>". The local backend's log (`bunx convex logs --history 400 --success`) shows `search/semanticActions:requestSemanticResults` for free-word and `like:` searches, and none for a filter-only search like `is:archived`.
- **Card links.** `tag:scenario-card-links` → "Cycle A → Cycle B": a pill with B's current title and "Linked from" listing Cycle C. "Broken links": three "Missing card" pills and "Renamed after linking". Edit shows the raw `[[…|teak:…]]` text; Close without saving.
- **Card link picker.** Open any note → Edit → in the note field type `%s[[garden`: the "Link a card" dropdown shows "Garden plan" and never the note itself, while the keyboard stays up. Tap it: the field reads `[[Garden plan|teak:<id>]] ` and the dropdown closes. Save: the note shows a "Garden plan" pill, and "Garden plan" lists the note under "Linked from". Type `[[` then press Back (`adb shell input keyevent KEYCODE_BACK`): the dropdown closes and stays closed until the `[[` is deleted. `[[trash` offers no card from Trash.
- **Detail rows.** `tag:scenario-omnibox-titles` → "Omnibox note: 1 < 2 > 0, saved from a page": Info "From · example.com". `tag:scenario-card-details` → "Author, a distinct publisher and a date": "Published · Nov 20, 2023". For camera rows, upload a JPEG with EXIF through Add → "Photos & Videos" on the second account: Camera, Taken, Exposure, Aperture, Focal length and ISO appear once processing finishes.
- **Empty Trash.** Select three cards → "Move to Trash" ("Moved 3 cards to Trash" with "Undo"). Tap the "Trash" chip: the top bar's "Empty Trash" is enabled. Tap it: "Empty Trash?" with "This permanently deletes 3 cards and their files. This can't be undone." → "Empty Trash": "Deleting 3 cards forever", the empty Trash state, and the button greys out. The web's Trash empties live.
- **Link title and address.** Open a link: its title in the top bar is a field. Type "Field notes", press Done: the preview's title reads "Field notes", and the web shows it. Clear it and leave the field: the old title comes back. More → "Edit link…" → `example.org` → Save: "URL must use the http or https scheme" under the field, nothing saved. `https://example.org/` → Save: the dialog closes, "Link saved. The preview refreshes shortly.", and the preview refreshes for the new page.
- **Suggested by Teak.** Open a card with tags Teak added (any processed link): Tags shows "Suggested by Teak". Tap a chip (TalkBack: "Keep <tag>"): it moves to the card's own tags (the web shows it as a tag). Tap `×` ("Remove <tag>") on another: it's gone, here and on the web. A card in Trash shows the suggestions without Keep or Remove.
- **Folder errors.** Switch the app to French (Settings → Language), Folders tab → "More for Design links" → "Edit search…" → search `trash` → Save: the error under the field is French, not "Smart folders don't show Trash…". Switch back to English.
- **Hidden.** `tag:secret`: header "Shows hidden cards"; a hidden card's Tags read "Hidden. Only a search for #secret or #hidden shows this card.". Edit a visible note → add tag "Secret" → "Hidden. Search #secret to see it." → Close → "Discard".
- **Revisit.** Clear the search, tap "Revisit": "1 of 20". Swipe right (keep), press ← (archive: "Archived 1 card" with "Undo"), press Z (back one, card unarchived), press → until "All caught up" with "N archived · N kept", then "Done".
- **Tags.** Settings → Tags → "Find a tag" → your run's tag → "More for …" → "Rename…" (card "Renamed “…” to “…” · N cards changed"), "Merge into…" (pick a tag → "Merge into “…”"), "Delete…" → "Delete tag" ("Deleted “…”"; "No tags match …"). Deleting the run's tags is also the cleanup for **Bulk**.
- **Search operators.** Seed `search-operators` and search the rows of [search-operators](./search-operators.md)'s table (each after `#scenario-search-operators`, in parentheses): the counts match the web's, and no filter chips show. Type `design -`: under the field, "Exclude" lists `-tag:` "Leave out cards with a tag" … `-is:`; tap `-tag:` and the field reads `design -tag:`. Type `design o`: "Combine" with "OR" "Either side matches"; tap it and the field reads `design OR `. `#scenario-search-operators figma last week` lists only cards saved last week that match "figma".
- **Share.** On the second account: `adb shell am start -a android.intent.action.SEND -t text/plain --es android.intent.extra.TEXT "https://example.com/verify-<run-id>" -n com.praveenjuge.teak.debug/com.praveenjuge.teak.feature.capture.share.ShareActivity` → "Saved" with "Add tags and a note"; tap it within 6 seconds, add tags and a note, "Save": the sheet closes and the card's detail shows both. Shared again without tapping, the sheet closes on its own.

- **Tabs.** Search `palette` on Home, tap Folders, then Home: the field still reads "palette" with its cards. Add → "Voice memo" → Record, tap Home, then Add, then Add again: still "Recording" and the time keeps counting. Stop it. Tap Search, type `is`: "Filters" suggestions show; Back hides the keyboard, the next Back closes the suggestions, and the app stays open.
- **Library loading.** Scroll Home, then type a search: the old cards stay with a thin bar at the top until the new ones arrive. Pull to refresh: the indicator stays and the cards don't blink.
- **Rail.** On a phone emulator the bottom bar has 4 tabs and Home shows the Favorites, Archive and Trash chips. Make the screen a tablet: `adb shell wm size 2560x1600 && adb shell wm density 320` (1280 dp wide). The rail lists Home, Favorites, Archive, Trash, Folders, Add, Settings, and Home has no Favorites, Archive or Trash chip. Tap "Trash": the page is titled "Trash" with Trash's cards and no view chips and no "Clear"; tap the "Notes" chip: "Clear" appears, and tapping it keeps Trash. Tap "Favorites" and "Archive" the same way, and open a card from one: it opens in the detail pane beside the list. With "Trash" selected, run `adb shell wm size 1200x1920` (600 dp, Medium): the bar is back to 4 items and Home is showing. Restore with `adb shell wm size reset && adb shell wm density reset`.
- **Wallpaper colors.** Settings → Appearance: "Wallpaper colors" is off; the selected tab and buttons are Teak red. Turn it on: they take the wallpaper's colors at once; `adb shell cmd uimode night yes` keeps them in dark. Change the wallpaper (long-press the home screen → Wallpaper & style) and come back: the app follows it. Force-stop and reopen: still on. Turn it off: red again.
- **PDF viewer.** On the second account, `adb push <any multi-page>.pdf /sdcard/Download/`, then Add → "Files" → pick it. Open the card → "View document": "Opening the PDF…", then the pages; scroll: the pill reads "Page 2 of N". Tap "Open in another app": a PDF app (or the browser) opens the file. Back returns to the card; "View document" again opens straight away, without downloading the file again. Turn on airplane mode, open a PDF never opened before: "Couldn't open this PDF." with "Try again"; turn it off and tap "Try again": the pages load. A Markdown or code document still opens in the browser.
- **Palette colors.** Open a seeded palette ("Citrus", five colors): the swatches end with an "Add color" tile. Tap it: the sheet's field has focus. Type `#ff55`: "Enter a hex code, such as #FF5500" and Add is off; `fb5607`: "#FB5607 is already in this palette"; `2a9d8f` (or tap a preset): the preview fills and Add turns on. Tap Add: the sheet closes, the swatch appears with "Added #2A9D8F", and the grid tile updates. Long-press the `#FB5607` swatch → "Remove color": it goes with "Removed #FB5607" and "Undo"; tap Undo at once (by position; `uiautomator dump` hangs while the snackbar animates): it's back at the end. Add presets until 12: the tile is gone. Remove colors from another palette down to one: long-press offers no menu (it copies, as a tap does). Move that palette to Trash and open it from Trash: no tile and no menu; Restore it. Read the colors back from `packages/convex` with `env -u CONVEX_DEPLOY_KEY CONVEX_AGENT_MODE=anonymous bunx convex data cards --format jsonl` (`colors` and `colorHexes` match). Dark: Settings → Appearance → Theme → "Dark" (the app sets its own night mode, so `cmd uimode night yes` doesn't reach it); put it back to "Auto".
- **Audio seek.** On the second account, Add → "Voice memo", record about 10 seconds, save and open it: the time reads "0:10". Tap the middle of the waveform: "0:05 / 0:10" and half the bars fill; play continues from 0:05. Drag along the bar while playing: the bars follow and playback jumps on release. With TalkBack, focus the bar: "Seek", "0:05 of 0:10"; the volume keys move it.

## Gotchas

- `adb shell wm size` and `wm density` outlast the app and survive reboots; reset both after the rail steps.
- Sign-in for tests exists only in Debug builds; Release ignores the launch file and has no cleartext exception.
- `uiautomator dump` takes a couple of seconds and hangs while a loading animation runs; tap Undo by position when timing matters.
- Sharing a MediaStore image from `adb shell am start` fails with "Teak couldn't read it", because the shell can't grant access to that URI; upload photos through Add → "Photos & Videos".
- No seed pack sets a link price, so the "Price" row and the tile's price pill need a real product page; on the local stack that page has to be reachable from the Files Worker.
- An emulator restart keeps the app and its session but drops `adb reverse`; the app then shows no cards until the ports are reversed again.
