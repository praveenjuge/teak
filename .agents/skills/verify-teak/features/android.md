# Android app

The native Android app (`Teak Dev` in Debug) shows the web's vault: a Home grid with search, filter chips (Favorites, Archive, Trash, Done, To do, types, color), sort, folders and smart folders, card details, Revisit, a Tags manager, and a share sheet for links, text and files. A Debug build signs in to the local E2E stack as a fresh emulator account, so no real WorkOS login is needed.

## Sub-features

- `android-settings` lays Settings out in four untitled groups: an account card (initial, email, and "Free · 8 of 200 cards" or "Pro · 12 cards"); Appearance (Auto, Light or Dark in a dialog), Language, Wallpaper colors and Tags; Version, Help and docs, Privacy policy and Sign out, above "Made with care by @praveenjuge"; and Delete account alone, the only red row.
- `android-sign-in` signs the Debug build in to the local E2E stack with `apps/android/scripts/e2e-session.ts`.
- `android-note-tables` shows a note's GFM tables read-only with a bold header row, wide ones scrolling sideways, `[[` pills working in cells; aligned, ragged and listed tables stay literal monospaced text. Steps in [note-tables](./note-tables.md), **Native apps**.
- `android-tasks` shows `- [ ]` and `- [x]` items in an open note as checkboxes (done ones muted and struck through); tapping a row ticks it, moves it below the open ones and saves, live on the web. A failed save puts it back with "Couldn't update the checklist. Try again." Steps in [note-tables](./note-tables.md), **Native apps**.
- `android-sort` sorts Home by "Newest first", "Oldest first" or "Shuffle" (reshuffles each time).
- `android-archive` shows the Archive view and archives or unarchives from a tile's long-press menu, the detail overflow, the E key or the selection bar, with "Undo".
- `android-bulk` offers on selected cards: Archive or Unarchive, Add to favorites, Remove from favorites, Add tags…, Add to folder…, Move to Trash, Restore, Delete forever.
- `android-folders` lists, opens, creates, renames and deletes (with "Undo") folders and smart folders in the Folders tab, edits a smart folder's search, and saves a search as one.
- `android-done` marks a link done from the button under its preview with its category's verb ("Mark as read" for a book, article or research link, "Mark as watched" for a film, "Mark as done" otherwise), shows a check on tiles, and filters with the Done and To do chips (`is:done`, `is:todo`).
- `android-search-facets` turns style, color and hex words into filters: `minimal blue #3b82f6 posters` sends `styleFilters: ["minimal"]`, `hueFilters: ["blue"]`, `hexFilters: ["#3B82F6"]` and `searchQuery: "posters"` to `cards:searchMobileCardSummariesPaginated`. Quoted words and searches with operators go as typed.
- `android-shared-rows` runs the shared rows of `packages/convex/__tests__/shared/fixtures` (`./gradlew :core:model:testDebugUnitTest`): a URL followed by a no-break space links only the URL, the grid shows 5 columns from 1300 dp, a shared file's name is sanitized for the backend (path separators and control characters become `_`, a long name keeps its extension), and a resolver's `application/binary` or empty type is read from the file's name.
- `android-search` shows typed filters as chips ("Show Archived", "Price Under 50", "Text in image …", "Mood “…”", "Similar to …"), ranks free words and `like:`/`mood:` by meaning, and offers "More like this" on tiles and the detail overflow.
- `android-detail` shows "Refresh details", "Folders…", "From example.com" on text saved from a page, "Published", "Price" on product links, and camera rows (Camera, Taken, Exposure, Aperture, Focal length, ISO) on photos.
- `android-card-links` shows `[[` links as pills (live titles or "Missing card") and a "Linked from" section; Edit keeps the raw token.
- `android-hidden` shows "Hidden. Search #secret to see it." after adding a hidden tag in Edit, "Shows hidden cards" when searching one, "Some cards are hidden…" on an empty view.
- `android-revisit` deals up to 20 shuffled cards from the current view (swipe or ← archive, → keep, Z undo) until "All caught up".
- `android-tags` lists tags in Settings → Tags and renames, merges or deletes them, with progress.
- `android-share` saves a shared link or text, then offers "Add tags and a note"; untouched, the sheet closes itself.
- `android-card-link-picker` opens a "Link a card" dropdown under a note's Edit field on `[[`, searches `cards:searchCardLinkSuggestions` as you type, and inserts `[[Title|teak:<id>]]` on a tap; Back, Esc, a tap outside or deleting the `[[` closes it.
- `android-empty-trash` offers "Empty Trash" in the Trash view's top bar, disabled when `cards:trashCount` is 0 (hidden cards count); it confirms, calls `cards:emptyTrash`, and Trash empties on every client (strings in **Empty Trash**). At the count's cap the confirmation says "at least 500 cards"; the server's rate limit shows "Trash was emptied a few times just now. Try again in a minute."
- `android-link-edit` renames a link from the title field in its top bar (Done or leaving saves `updateCardField metadataTitle`; a blank title restores the old one), and changes its address from More → "Edit link…" (`updateCardField url`; a non-link is refused). Strings in **Link title and address**.
- `android-suggested-tags` shows "Suggested by Teak" in an open card's Tags with Keep (tap the chip, `+`) and Remove (`×`) per AI tag the card lacks; Keep writes the card's tags plus that tag, Remove uses `removeAiTag`; in Trash neither shows.
- `android-folder-errors` translates a smart folder's search error (the server sends `INVALID_INPUT` in English).
- `android-search-operators` sends `-`, `OR`, parentheses and `site:` as typed (no chips), lists suggestions under the search field ("Exclude", "Combine", "Filters", "Types", "Show"), and searches the words beside a date ("figma last week" sends the date range and "figma"). See [search-operators](./search-operators.md).
- `android-rail` gives Expanded windows (840 dp and up) a navigation rail with Home, Favorites, Archive, Trash, Folders, Add and Settings. Favorites, Archive and Trash open as titled pages with that filter fixed and its chip hidden; Clear removes only filters added on top. Home there hides those three chips unless one is on. Compact and Medium widths keep the four tabs (Home, Folders, Add, Settings) and the chips; narrowing from those pages returns to Home.
- `android-wallpaper-colors` adds Settings → "Wallpaper colors" (Material You), off by default (Teak's red); see **Wallpaper colors**.
- `android-pdf-viewer` opens a PDF card's "View document" in the app as a scrolling column of pages at screen width, with a "Page 1 of N" pill and "Open in another app"; other documents open in the browser (**PDF viewer**).
- `android-tabs` keeps each tab's state (Home's search, filters and scroll, a draft note, a recording voice memo); re-tapping a tab returns to its first screen, except over a draft note or recording. With the search field focused, Back hides the keyboard, then closes the suggestions, before leaving Home.
- `android-library-loading` keeps cards on screen under a thin progress bar (no full-screen spinner) while a search, filter or pull to refresh loads; until new cards arrive, the old ones can't be selected or long-pressed, and Select and Empty Trash stay off. A later page failing shows "Couldn't load more cards." with "Try again", which retries it in place.
- `android-palette-colors` edits a palette card's colors in its detail: an "Add color" tile after the last swatch (hidden at 12 colors and in Trash) opens a sheet with a "Hex code" field, a live preview, "Pick a color" presets and "Add" (`updateCardField addColor`); long-pressing a swatch offers "Remove color" (`removeColor`) while another would stay ("Undo" re-adds it at the end); a tap still copies. A failed write says "Couldn't update the palette. Try again." Other strings in **Palette colors**.
- `android-audio-seek` makes a voice memo's waveform a TalkBack-accessible seek bar (tap or drag, playing or paused); see **Audio seek**.
- `android-offline`: after 3 seconds offline Home shows "You're offline. Changes sync when you're back." under the filters; favorite, archive, done, edit, Move to Trash, Restore (single or bulk), and a new note or link apply at once and wait in `no_backup/outbox.json` until `OutboxWorker` sends them (after a restart too). A never-loaded card says "You're offline". More → "Keep offline" saves the card and its file under `files/offline/<id>/` ("Kept offline") for offline use; "Remove offline copy" removes it. Signed card images are cached by URL minus `exp`/`sig`.
- `android-widgets` adds two home-screen widgets (Glance, Material You colors, uncaptioned tiles). **Quick Capture**: 2×2 tiles (New note, Voice memo, Photos & videos, Files); from about three cells wide, a large New note tile plus those and Camera. Each opens Add on that action (`MainActivity.ACTION_NEW_NOTE`, `ACTION_VOICE_MEMO`, `ACTION_PHOTOS`, `ACTION_CAMERA`, `ACTION_FILES`). **Favorites**: the newest favorites from `files/widgets/favorites.json`, written by `FavoritesWidgetWriter` while signed in (thumbnails about 480 px, no archived cards); 1 card narrow, 2×2 wide, 2×3 wide and tall, faint empty slots, "No favorites yet" with a heart when none. A card opens via `ACTION_OPEN_CARD` with `EXTRA_CARD_ID`; signing out deletes the snapshot.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Home tab: search, filter chips, and the top bar's Revisit, Sort and Select. Long-press a tile for Select, Archive or Unarchive, and More like this.
- Folders tab: "New folder" (New folder, New smart folder) and each row's "More for <name>". Home's Sort menu has "Save as smart folder…" while searching.
- A card: the done button under a link's preview; the toolbar's "More" (Archive, More like this, Folders…, Refresh details, Move to Trash); Edit.
- Settings → Tags.
- Any app's Share → Teak Dev.
- Widget picker → Teak Dev → Quick Capture or Favorites.
- Keyboard on an open card: E archive, D done, R refresh details, F favorite. In Revisit: ← or A archive, → or K keep, Space open, Z undo, Esc close.

## Driving it with adb and the Debug build

Preconditions:

- JDK 17+ (`JAVA_HOME=/opt/homebrew/opt/openjdk@21` on a Mac), the Android SDK in `apps/android/local.properties`, and an emulator booted with `-gpu host` (software rendering is too slow). So typing lands, turn off stylus handwriting: `adb shell settings put secure stylus_handwriting_enabled 0`.
- E2E stack up: `.agents/.state/stack.json` shows `mode: "e2e"` and `ready: true`.
- Install and sign in: from `apps/android`, `./gradlew :app:installDebug`; then from the repo root `bun --no-env-file run apps/android/scripts/e2e-session.ts`, which reverses the stack's ports onto the device, gives the app a fresh `e2e-android-…@example.org` account, restarts it, and prints the `workosUserId`. After an emulator restart, rerun it only if the app shows Welcome, but always redo the reverses (`adb reverse tcp:<port> tcp:<port>` for the convex, convexSite, emulator, coreWorker and web ports).
- Seed from `packages/convex`: `env -u CONVEX_DEPLOY_KEY CONVEX_AGENT_MODE=anonymous bunx convex run devSeed:seed '{"workosUserId":"<id>"}'`, then `devSeed:seedScenario` with `archive-mix`, `smart-folders`, `done-states`, `hidden-mix`, `card-links`, `card-details`, `rich-sites`, `revisit-sizes`, `search-qualifiers`, `save-details` and `omnibox-titles`. They put the account over the Free plan's 200 cards, so create cards (share, uploads) on a second account with only `devSeed:seed`.
- Find controls by text or content description via `adb shell uiautomator dump`; type with `adb shell input text` (`%s` for spaces); capture with `adb exec-out screencap -p`. Dark theme: `adb shell cmd uimode night yes`.

Steps:

- **Sort.** "Sort" → "Oldest first": old `rv…` notes come first and the button reads "Sort: oldest first". "Shuffle" twice: the order changes each time. End on "Newest first".
- **Archive.** The "Archive" chip titles the view "Archive". Long-press "Archived design note" → "Unarchive": "Returned 1 card to the library" with "Undo" (10 seconds), which brings it back.
- **Bulk.** Search "Mix", "Select cards", pick two tiles, "More actions" → "Add tags…" → type `verify-<run-id>` → "Add tags": "Added tags to 2 cards". `tag:verify-<run-id>` shows a "Tag …" chip and only those cards. "Add to folder…" → a new name → "Create folder “…”": "Added 2 cards to …".
- **Folders.** Folders tab: smart folders show their search under the name; "Design links" holds only links tagged design. "More for <yours>" → "Rename…" → Save. "Delete" → "Deleted “…”. Its cards are still in your library." → "Undo" brings it back. Delete it again.
- **Done.** Search `is:todo`, open "A book with an ISBN-13" → "Mark as read": the button reads "Read", "Marked as read" with "Undo". It leaves `is:todo`; `is:done ISBN` shows it with the check and the Done chip on. D (or "Read") undoes it.
- **Refresh details.** More → "Refresh details" → "Refreshing details. The summary and tags update shortly."; R right after → "This card is already refreshing. Try again in a minute."
- **More like this.** More → "More like this": Home shows "Similar to <title>". `bunx convex logs --history 400 --success` shows `search/semanticActions:requestSemanticResults` for free-word and `like:` searches, not for a filter-only one like `is:archived`.
- **Card links.** `tag:scenario-card-links` → "Cycle A → Cycle B": a pill with B's current title and "Linked from" listing Cycle C. "Broken links": three "Missing card" pills and "Renamed after linking". Edit shows the raw `[[…|teak:…]]`; Close without saving.
- **Card link picker.** Open a note → Edit → type `%s[[garden` in the note field: "Link a card" shows "Garden plan", never the note itself, with the keyboard up. Tap it: the field reads `[[Garden plan|teak:<id>]] ` and the dropdown closes. Save: a "Garden plan" pill, and "Garden plan" lists the note under "Linked from". Back after `[[` (`adb shell input keyevent KEYCODE_BACK`) closes it until the `[[` is deleted. `[[trash` offers nothing from Trash.
- **Detail rows.** `tag:scenario-omnibox-titles` → "Omnibox note: 1 < 2 > 0, saved from a page": Info "From · example.com". `tag:scenario-card-details` → "Author, a distinct publisher and a date": "Published · Nov 20, 2023". On the second account, upload a JPEG with EXIF through Add → "Photos & Videos": the camera rows appear once processing finishes.
- **Empty Trash.** Three cards → "Move to Trash" ("Moved 3 cards to Trash" with "Undo"). On the "Trash" chip, "Empty Trash" is enabled; tap it: "Empty Trash?" with "This permanently deletes 3 cards and their files. This can't be undone." → "Empty Trash": "Deleting 3 cards forever", the empty state, a greyed button, and the web's Trash empties live.
- **Link title and address.** Open a link, type "Field notes" in the top bar's title field, press Done: the preview and the web read "Field notes". Cleared and left, the old title returns. More → "Edit link…" → `example.org` → Save: "URL must use the http or https scheme", nothing saved. `https://example.org/` → Save: "Link saved. The preview refreshes shortly." and the preview updates.
- **Suggested by Teak.** On any processed link, Tags shows "Suggested by Teak". A chip tap (TalkBack: "Keep <tag>") makes it a card tag; `×` ("Remove <tag>") on another removes it; the web matches both. In Trash, no Keep or Remove.
- **Settings.** The account card shows the run's email and "Free · N of 200 cards". Appearance → "Dark" darkens the app and the row reads "Dark"; reset to "Auto". Language's dialog marks English. Help and docs and Privacy policy open teakvault.com in a Custom Tab; Sign out and Delete account ask first (cancel). On a tablet (`wm size 2560x1600`, density 320): one centered column.
- **Folder errors.** Set Language to French, Folders tab → "More for Design links" → "Edit search…" → search `trash` → Save: the error is French, not "Smart folders don't show Trash…". Switch back.
- **Hidden.** `tag:secret` shows "Shows hidden cards"; a hidden card's Tags read "Hidden. Only a search for #secret or #hidden shows this card.". Edit a visible note → add tag "Secret" → "Hidden. Search #secret to see it." → Close → "Discard".
- **Revisit.** Clear the search, "Revisit": "1 of 20". Swipe right (keep), ← ("Archived 1 card" with "Undo"), Z (back one, unarchived), → until "All caught up" with "N archived · N kept", then "Done".
- **Tags.** Settings → Tags → "Find a tag" → your run's tag → "More for …" → "Rename…" (card "Renamed “…” to “…” · N cards changed"), "Merge into…" (pick a tag → "Merge into “…”"), "Delete…" → "Delete tag" ("Deleted “…”"; "No tags match …"). This cleans up **Bulk**'s tags.
- **Search operators.** Seed `search-operators` and search the rows of [search-operators](./search-operators.md)'s table (each in parentheses after `#scenario-search-operators`): counts match the web's, with no chips. Type `design -`: under the field, "Exclude" lists `-tag:` "Leave out cards with a tag" … `-is:`; `-tag:` gives `design -tag:`. `design o`: "Combine" with "OR" "Either side matches", which gives `design OR `. `#scenario-search-operators figma last week` lists only last week's "figma" cards.
- **Share.** Second account: `adb shell am start -a android.intent.action.SEND -t text/plain --es android.intent.extra.TEXT "https://example.com/verify-<run-id>" -n com.praveenjuge.teak.debug/com.praveenjuge.teak.feature.capture.share.ShareActivity` → "Saved" with "Add tags and a note"; within 6 seconds tap it, add tags and a note, "Save": the card's detail shows both. Untouched, the sheet closes itself.

- **Widgets.** Add Quick Capture and Favorites (long-press the home screen → Widgets → Teak Dev). Each Quick Capture tile (TalkBack: "New note", "Voice memo", "Photos & videos", "Files", "Camera" when wide) opens Add on that action; without a launcher, `adb shell am start -a com.praveenjuge.teak.action.PHOTOS -n com.praveenjuge.teak.debug/com.praveenjuge.teak.MainActivity`. Favorite three cards: the widget shows them newest first within seconds; unfavorite one: the next fills in. A tile opens its card (`adb shell am start -a com.praveenjuge.teak.action.OPEN_CARD --es com.praveenjuge.teak.extra.CARD_ID <id> -n …/com.praveenjuge.teak.MainActivity`). Sign out: Favorites shows "No favorites yet". `adb shell run-as com.praveenjuge.teak.debug ls files/widgets` lists `favorites.json` and thumbnails only while signed in.

- **Tabs.** Search `palette` on Home, tap Folders, then Home: "palette" and its cards remain. Add → "Voice memo" → Record, tap Home, Add, Add again: still "Recording", time counting; stop it. Type `is` in Search: "Filters" suggestions; Back hides the keyboard, Back again closes the suggestions, and the app stays open.
- **Library loading.** Scroll Home, type a search: the old cards stay under a thin bar until the new ones arrive. Pull to refresh: the cards don't blink.
- **Rail.** A phone has 4 bottom tabs and the view chips. Make a tablet: `adb shell wm size 2560x1600 && adb shell wm density 320` (1280 dp): the rail lists Home, Favorites, Archive, Trash, Folders, Add, Settings, and Home lacks those chips. "Trash" opens a "Trash" page with no view chips or "Clear"; the "Notes" chip adds "Clear", which keeps Trash. Same for "Favorites" and "Archive"; a card opens in the detail pane beside the list. With "Trash" selected, `adb shell wm size 1200x1920` (600 dp, Medium): 4 tabs again, on Home. Restore with `adb shell wm size reset && adb shell wm density reset`.
- **Wallpaper colors.** "Wallpaper colors" is off; the selected tab and buttons are Teak red. On: they take the wallpaper's colors at once, in dark too (`adb shell cmd uimode night yes`). A new wallpaper (long-press the home screen → Wallpaper & style) recolors the app. Force-stop and reopen: still on. Off: red again.
- **PDF viewer.** On the second account, `adb push <any multi-page>.pdf /sdcard/Download/`, then Add → "Files" → pick it. "View document": "Opening the PDF…", then the pages; scrolled, the pill reads "Page 2 of N". "Open in another app" opens the file in a PDF app (or the browser). Reopening it skips the download. In airplane mode an unopened PDF shows "Couldn't open this PDF." with "Try again", which loads it once online. A Markdown or code document opens in the browser.
- **Palette colors.** Open the seeded "Citrus" palette (five colors): an "Add color" tile ends the swatches; tap it (the field has focus). Type `#ff55`: "Enter a hex code, such as #FF5500" and Add is off; `fb5607`: "#FB5607 is already in this palette"; `2a9d8f` (or a preset): the preview fills and Add turns on. Add: the swatch appears with "Added #2A9D8F", and the grid tile updates. Long-press `#FB5607` → "Remove color": "Removed #FB5607" and "Undo"; tap Undo at once (by position): it's back at the end. At 12 colors the tile is gone. A palette down to one color has no long-press menu (it copies, like a tap). In Trash: no tile, no menu; Restore it. Check from `packages/convex` with `env -u CONVEX_DEPLOY_KEY CONVEX_AGENT_MODE=anonymous bunx convex data cards --format jsonl` (`colors` and `colorHexes` match). For dark, use Settings → Appearance → "Dark" (`cmd uimode night yes` doesn't reach it), then "Auto".
- **Audio seek.** On the second account, Add → "Voice memo", record about 10 seconds, save and open it: "0:10". Tap the waveform's middle: "0:05 / 0:10", half the bars fill, and play starts from 0:05. Dragging while playing jumps on release. TalkBack on the bar: "Seek", "0:05 of 0:10"; volume keys move it.
- **Offline.** `adb reverse` survives airplane mode, so run a TCP forwarder from host port `p+1000` to `p` for the convex, convexSite, emulator, coreWorker and web ports, `adb reverse tcp:<p> tcp:<p+1000>`; "offline" is airplane mode plus stopping the forwarder. Online, a PDF card → More → "Keep offline": "Kept offline", and `adb shell run-as com.praveenjuge.teak.debug ls files/offline/<id>` lists `card.json` and `file`. Offline, Home shows the offline line after 3 seconds. Long-press a tile → Archive: it leaves at once with "Archived 1 card" and "Undo"; `run-as … cat no_backup/outbox.json` holds the edit. A never-opened tile says "You're offline"; the kept PDF opens, "View document" reads it, and Favorite fills. A new note (Add → "Note or link" → Save) closes the composer. Home, `adb shell am kill com.praveenjuge.teak.debug`, go online without opening the app: within a minute WorkManager runs, `outbox.json` is gone, and `bunx convex data cards` shows the archive, favorite and note once. For cache keys, clear `cache/coil3_disk_cache`, reopen, and check file names start with the SHA-256 of an image link minus `?exp=…&sig=…`.

## Gotchas

- `adb shell wm size` and `wm density` survive the app and reboots; reset both after **Rail**.
- Test sign-in is Debug-only; Release ignores the launch file and has no cleartext exception.
- `uiautomator dump` takes seconds and hangs while an animation (a loader, a snackbar) runs; tap Undo by position when timing matters.
- Sharing a MediaStore image from `adb shell am start` fails with "Teak couldn't read it" (the shell can't grant that URI); upload photos through Add → "Photos & Videos".
- No seed pack sets a link price; the "Price" row and the tile's price pill need a real product page the local Core Worker can reach.
- An emulator restart keeps the session but drops `adb reverse`: no cards show until it's redone.
