# Apple app (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

The native Teak app on iPhone, iPad and Mac shows the library with search chips, a "More" menu, archive, folders and smart folders, tags, Revisit and a card view. The share extension saves first, then takes tags and a note. It shares the web app's backend, so a change made here shows on the web and back.

## Sub-features

- `apple-archive` archives and unarchives a card (context menu, swipe, Card menu or More menu) with Undo; Archive lists them (sidebar on Mac and iPad, More menu on iPhone).
- `apple-note-tables` shows a note's GFM tables read-only (bold header row, wide ones scroll sideways, `[[` pills work in cells); aligned, ragged and listed tables stay literal monospaced text. Steps in [note-tables](./note-tables.md), **Native apps**.
- `apple-markdown` shows a note's Markdown in Allo, with inline code (on a light tint) and sideways-scrolling code blocks in SF Mono, and table header shading spanning every column. Paragraphs over 40,000 characters show in pieces, so a 500 KB note isn't blank on iPhone. Tiles drop nested markers (`***both***` reads "both").
- `apple-tasks` shows `- [ ]` and `- [x]` items as checkboxes (done ones muted and struck through); tapping the box or the gap up to the text ticks that item, moves it below the open ones and saves; the web shows it live. A failed save puts it back with "Couldn't update the checklist. Try again." Steps in [note-tables](./note-tables.md), **Native apps**.
- `apple-bulk` selects cards (More › Select) and archives, unarchives, tags, files, favorites, trashes or restores them, with a count message and Undo. **Add Tags** takes at most 20 tags: with 21 the button is off and the alert reads "Choose at most 20 tags at a time."
- `apple-sort` sorts by Newest first, Oldest first or Shuffle (More menu), with Reshuffle.
- `apple-folders` lists folders and smart folders, opens, creates, renames and deletes them with Undo, files a card from its Folders row or the selection bar, and saves the search as a smart folder.
- `apple-done` toggles "Mark as read/watched/bought…" or "Mark as done" on a link, checks done tiles, and filters with `is:done` and `is:todo`.
- `apple-search` turns filters with their own argument (a type, favorites, `is:archived`, Trash, a color, a style, a date) into chips on Return and keeps other words in one word chip as typed; picked suggestions become chips; a month name or number beside other words, a year outside 1900 to ten years ahead and fullwidth digits stay words; it searches by meaning and shows "Shows hidden cards" for a `#secret` or `#hidden` search.
- `apple-shared-rows` runs the rows in `packages/convex/__tests__/shared/fixtures` shared with the web and Android: a lone month name ("march") stays a word, `site:例え.jp` searches `xn--r8jz45g.jp` while its chip shows `例え.jp`, `#3b82f680` and `#fff.` search as hex colors, and the grid shows 5 columns from 1300 points. `swift test --package-path apps/apple/Packages/TeakKit` runs them.
- `apple-more-like-this` opens the library with a "Similar to" chip from a card's More or context menu.
- `apple-refresh` runs Refresh details: the queued message, or "This card is already refreshing…".
- `apple-hidden` shows "Hidden. Search #secret to see it." in Edit on adding a hidden tag.
- `apple-revisit` deals up to 20 shuffled cards from the view: Archive (swipe left, ←), Keep (swipe right, →), Undo (Z), "All caught up" at the end.
- `apple-card-links` shows `[[…]]` links as pills (live titles or "Missing card") that open the target, and lists "Linked from".
- `apple-card-details` shows Price, Published, EXIF rows (Camera, Taken, Exposure, Aperture, Focal length, ISO) and the "From <host>" row in the card's Info group.
- `apple-tags` lists tags with counts and renames, merges and deletes them with progress.
- `apple-share-details` adds tags and a note after saving one shared item.
- `apple-share-sheet` shows the item being saved (iPhone and iPad: a large thumbnail, up to three fanned out, or a symbol and the host or first line for links and text; Mac: a compact row) with a corner badge: spinning, then a green check, an orange "!" for sign-in or nothing to save, or a shaking red ✕ for a failure. A band drains across **Done** during the 3-second auto-close, stopping once you type or open folders.
- `apple-safari-save` saves the Safari tab with `createCard`, no duplicate check: a page already in Teak moves to the top and out of Archive, and the popup reads "Saved to Teak" (`createCard` returns only the id).
- `apple-commands` (Mac and iPad Card menu): Archive ⌃⌘A, Mark as Done ⇧⌘D, More Like This ⇧⌘L, Refresh Details ⌥⌘R, and View › Revisit ⌃⌘R.
- `apple-card-link-picker` opens "Link a card" under a note's Edit field on `[[`, searches as you type (`cards:searchCardLinkSuggestions`: no Trash, not the note itself), and inserts `[[Title|teak:<id>]]` on a tap or ↑/↓ and Return; Escape or deleting the `[[` closes it.
- `apple-empty-trash` offers "Empty Trash" in Trash's toolbar and More menu, off when `cards:trashCount` is 0 (hidden cards count); the confirmation reads "This permanently deletes 3 cards and their files. This can't be undone." ("at least 500 cards" at the cap) and calls `cards:emptyTrash`, then "Deleting 3 cards forever", and the cards leave Trash on every client. Emptying too often within a minute shows "Trash was emptied a few times just now. Try again in a minute."
- `apple-trash-undo` trashes one card (context menu, swipe or card view) without asking: "Card moved to Trash" with **Undo** (`updateCardField restore`); Delete forever still asks.
- `apple-link-edit` renames a link from its title (**Rename**; on Mac the toolbar title) with `updateCardField metadataTitle` (blank restores the old title), and changes its address from More › "Edit link…" with `updateCardField url`.
- `apple-suggested-tags` shows "Suggested by Teak" in an open card's Tags: a dashed chip per tag Teak added that the card lacks; a tap offers Keep (adds it to the card's tags), Search for it and Remove (`removeAiTag`); in Trash a tap only searches.
- `apple-smart-folder-search` edits a smart folder's name and search from its menu's "Edit search…" (`folders:updateFolder` with `query`); the page shows the new results, and a bad search the server's error in the app's language.
- `apple-search-operators` sends `-`, `OR`, parentheses and `site:` as typed (one word chip), suggests "-tag: · Leave out cards with a tag"… after `-` and "OR · Either side matches" after a word, and searches words beside a date ("figma last week"). See [search-operators](./search-operators.md).
- `apple-image-viewer` opens a photo at full size: on iPhone and iPad full screen over black (pinch and double-tap zoom; swipe down, ✕ or Escape closes); on Mac its own window titled with the file name (pinch, double-click, **Zoom In** ⌘=, **Zoom Out** ⌘-, **Zoom to Fit** ⌘0). Animated GIFs play inline and don't open it.
- `apple-audio-seek` makes a voice memo's waveform a seek bar (tap or drag, playing or paused); the time reads the length, then "0:05 / 0:10"; VoiceOver swipes up or down move it.
- `apple-intents` lists Save to Teak, Search Teak, Open Card, Add to Folder, New Note, Record Voice Memo, Add Photos, Take Photo (iPhone and iPad) and Upload Files in Shortcuts, each with an App Shortcut phrase. Search Teak sends `searchQuery` to `cards:searchMobileCardSummariesPaginated` (up to 20; archived only with **Include archived cards**; nothing for `#secret`/`#hidden`); Add to Folder offers manual folders and calls `addCardsToFolder`; New Note, Record Voice Memo and Open Card open the app on `teak://new-note`, `teak://voice-memo` and `teak://card/<id>`. Signed out: "Open Teak and sign in, then run the shortcut again."
- `apple-spotlight` indexes the cards the library loads plus the newest 500 when the app becomes active (at most every 5 minutes; a refresh reaching the library's end also drops deleted cards), with title, preview, host, type and thumbnail; trashed, archived and hidden cards are never indexed, and leave it when the library sees them change. A result opens the card. Sign-out and account deletion delete the domain and the `Spotlight.json` ledger in Application Support.
- `apple-controls` adds New Note, Voice Memo, Add Photos, Take Photo (iPhone and iPad) and Upload Files controls (TeakWidgets) to Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action button; each runs its Shortcuts intent, opening the app on that screen (`teak://photos`, `teak://camera`, `teak://files` for the last three).
- `apple-quick-capture-widget` (small, medium, Lock Screen circular) has buttons opening `teak://new-note`, `teak://voice-memo`, `teak://photos`, `teak://camera` (not on Mac) and `teak://files`; it reads nothing.
- `apple-favorites-widget` shows the newest favorites (small 1, medium 4, large 6, Lock Screen rectangular and inline 1) from `Widgets/Favorites.json` and thumbnails in the app group. The app writes them while Convex is signed in from a live `cards:searchMobileCardSummariesPaginated` with `favoritesOnly` (no archived or hidden cards), and deletes the folder on sign-out. A card opens `teak://card/<id>`; empty, it reads "No favorites yet".
- `apple-share-folders` shows **Add to folders** after a share-extension save: a checklist of manual folders (search above 8) from `folders:listFolders` that, on close, saves `addCardsToFolder`/`removeCardsFromFolder` for only the folders it changed (a re-shared link keeps its other folders), and pauses the 3-second auto-close while open.
- `apple-iphone-duo` runs full screen on iPhone Duo (built with Xcode 27.2; 27.0 builds run letterboxed): closed, the tab bar and toolbar sit on the side; open, the sidebar layout; partly open, the grid keeps every column off the fold without adding the composer tile, and a card beside the sidebar shows Info under it, not in a covering inspector.
- `apple-palette-colors` adds a color from the **+** tile after the swatches (iPhone: a sheet; iPad and Mac: a popover) with a linked color picker and hex field, a preview and **Add** (`updateCardField addColor`): "Added #RRGGBB". A bad code shows "Enter a hex code, such as #FF5500", one already there "#RRGGBB is already in this palette", and Add stays off. Each swatch's context menu has **Remove color** (`removeColor`): "Removed #RRGGBB" with **Undo**, which re-adds it at the end. **+** hides at 12 colors, Remove at one, both in Trash; a failed save reads "Couldn't update the palette. Try again." Tapping a swatch still copies its hex.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- iPhone: Home → the circled-ellipsis **More** (Select, Revisit, Show, Sort, Save as smart folder…); the **Folders** tab (folders, smart folders, New folder); Settings › Tags.
- iPad and Mac: the sidebar (Home, Add, Favorites, Archive, Tags, Trash, then Folders and Types; iPad also lists Settings and has **+** on its Folders header; Mac's last Folders row is **New folder**); the library toolbar's **More**; on Mac, **+** adds, every **New note** (⌘N, **+**, Add, `teak://new-note`) opens the Quick Capture window, every **Voice Memo** (⇧⌘R, **+**, Add, the menu bar menu, `teak://voice-memo`) opens a floating recorder whose close button is off while there's audio to lose, a drop on the menu bar icon (**Settings › General › Show Teak in the menu bar**) shows an upload arrow, then a check or "!", and Settings opens its own window from the app menu or ⌘,. Mac has one main window; reopening the library brings it forward.
- Settings on iPhone and iPad: an account card (email, "Free · 8 of 200 cards"); Appearance, Language and Tags (iPhone), footnoted by the Safari extension how-to (and the language's Undo or error); Version and Sign out above "Made with care by @praveenjuge"; then Delete account. On Mac, General keeps the Appearance segmented control and Account holds Profile and both buttons; the plan row's **Upgrade…** (Free) or **Manage…** (Pro) opens `<web>/settings/plan` (see **Plan link**).
- Any card: press and hold or right-click a tile; open it for the done toggle and **More** (Archive, Folders…, More like this, Refresh details). On iPhone a link's **Edit** and **Share** lead its More menu, so the bar keeps heart, the done toggle and **More** out of iOS's "•••" (a too-long title still folds them); other cards keep Share and the pencil in the bar. Copy and Copy link to card show "Copied" / "Link copied".
- Card sections: Notes, Tags, Summary, Transcript, Media, Linked from, then **Info**: Type and Folders (tappable), "From <host>" when saved from a page, a link's Price/Site/Author/Published, file facts, Created and Updated. A link's site and description are only in its header.
- Share sheet from any app → Teak → "Saved" → tags and note → Save.

## Driving it with the iOS Simulator and xcodebuild

Preconditions:

- The local E2E stack is up (`.agents/.state/stack.json`: `mode: "e2e"`, `ready: true`).
- `bun --no-env-file run apps/apple/scripts/e2e-session.ts` prints the launch environment; the user id is `user.id` in the base64 `TEAK_UI_TEST_SESSION` JSON.
- Build: `xcodebuild build -project apps/apple/Teak.xcodeproj -scheme Teak -destination "platform=iOS Simulator,name=<iPhone>" -derivedDataPath apps/apple/build/DerivedData`, then `xcrun simctl install` and launch with each variable prefixed `SIMCTL_CHILD_`. On Mac, build with `-allowProvisioningUpdates` and `open -n --env K=V … Teak.app --args -teak.appearance light|dark`.
- Launch once to link the account, then seed it from `packages/convex`: `env -u CONVEX_DEPLOY_KEY CONVEX_AGENT_MODE=anonymous bunx convex run devSeed:seed '{"workosUserId":"<id>"}'` and `devSeed:seedScenario` with `archive-mix`, `smart-folders`, `done-states`, `hidden-mix`, `card-links`, `backlinks`, `card-details`, `save-details`, `revisit-sizes`, `search-qualifiers`.
- Screenshots: `xcrun simctl io <device> screenshot`; on Mac only Teak's window (`screencapture -l <window id>`), never the whole screen.

Steps:

- **Archive.** Press and hold a Home tile → **Archive**: "Archived 1 card" with **Undo**, the tile leaves Home. More › Archive (sidebar on iPad and Mac) lists it; press and hold → **Unarchive**: "Returned 1 card to the library"; **Undo** re-archives.
- **Bulk.** More › **Select**, tap two tiles ("2 selected"), tap the bar's folder button, pick "Design refs": "Added 2 cards to Design refs". The heart favorites them ("Added 2 cards to favorites"), or unfavorites them if all are; the trash button trashes them.
- **Sort.** More › **Shuffle**: a new order and **Reshuffle**; **Newest first** restores it.
- **Folders.** iPhone **Folders** tab (iPad and Mac sidebar) → "Design refs": a page with its 12 cards and its own More menu; a card opened there stays in the Folders tab; "Design links" is a smart folder (`tag:design type:link`). Search `type:link is:fav` → More › **Save as smart folder…** → "Saved “…”". Press and hold a folder → **Rename…** / **Delete** (Undo restores it).
- **Done.** Search `#scenario-done-states`: done links show a green check. Open "A tv link" → **Mark as watched**: "Marked as watched", the toggle reads "Watched"; tap again to undo. `is:todo` + Return: only undone actionable links.
- **Search.** `is:to`, pick "To do": a "To do" chip. End each search below with Return. `type:image price:<100 text:"Total due"`: an "Image" type chip and one word chip `price:<100 text:"Total due"`. `#secret`: the "Shows hidden cards" header and hidden cards. `things i may need`: one word chip, no date chip; `march` alone: a "Mar <year>" date chip. `top 100 bad sites`: one word chip, no color chip. `1234`: a word chip, not a year. `２０２４` doesn't crash and stays a word.
- **Search operators.** Seed `search-operators` and search each row of [search-operators](./search-operators.md)'s table as `#scenario-search-operators (<row>)`: counts match the web's. `design -` suggests `-tag:` … `-is:` with "Leave out …"; picking "-tag:" gives `design -tag:`. `design o` suggests "OR · Either side matches"; picking it gives `design OR `. `#scenario-search-operators figma last week` lists only cards saved last week that match "figma".
- **More like this.** Card → **More** → **More like this**: Home with a "Similar to: <title>" chip.
- **Refresh.** Card → **More** → **Refresh details**: "Refreshing details. The summary and tags update shortly." Again: "This card is already refreshing. Try again in a minute."
- **Hidden hint.** Edit a note, add tag `Secret`: "Hidden. Search #secret to see it." under Tags. Cancel → Discard changes.
- **Plan link (Mac).** Seed `card-limit-199`, save one note. Settings › Account, the library status bar and a capture at the limit each have **Upgrade…**, which opens the default browser at `<web>/settings/plan`, not `/settings`.
- **Revisit.** More › **Revisit**: "1 of 20" with a card, its type and "Added <date>". Swipe right (Keep) → "2 of 20"; left (Archive) → "3 of 20" and **Undo**; Undo → "2 of 20". On a smaller view (`#rv1`) the deck ends with "All caught up" and "<n> archived · <n> kept". Archive the first card, keep the rest, tap **Undo last archive**: the card is back in the library and the deck returns to it ("1 of <n>"), not the last kept card. Revisit is off in Archive and Trash.
- **Markdown.** Save and open a note with ``A *italic*, ***both***, `code` and ~~gone~~ line``, a fenced code block and a table: italic slants, code and the code block are monospaced (code on a tint), and the header shading reaches the last column. Its tile reads "A italic, both, code and gone line". With `note-tables` seeded, "A long note with a table" shows its text and final table on iPhone, not a blank page.
- **Card links.** Search `#scenario-backlinks hub`, open "Source 60": the pill "Hub with 60 backlinks"; tap it → the hub opens with **Linked from** rows. Open `"Broken links"`: three "Missing card" pills, the "Renamed after linking" live title, RTL, CJK and emoji titles.
- **Card link picker.** Edit any note and type " [[garden": the "Link a card" list shows "Garden plan" (seeded by `card-links`) and never the note itself. Tap it (Mac: ↓, Return): the field reads `[[Garden plan|teak:<id>]] ` and the list closes. **Save**: a "Garden plan" pill, and "Garden plan" lists the note under **Linked from**. "[[" then Escape (hardware keyboard): the list stays closed until the `[[` is deleted. `[[trash` offers no trashed card.
- **Card details.** Search `#scenario-save-details`, open "A selection from a page whose title runs to 300 characters.": Info has a "From example.org" row with the page title under it. Product links show **Price**, dated links **Published**, photos with EXIF the camera rows.
- **Trash and Undo.** Press and hold a note → **Move to Trash**: no dialog, "Card moved to Trash" with **Undo**; Undo brings it back. From a card's More → **Move to Trash**: the card closes, same note with Undo.
- **Empty Trash.** Move three cards to Trash and open it (More › Trash, or the sidebar): "Empty Trash" is enabled. Tap it: "Empty Trash?" with the confirmation above. Confirm: "Deleting 3 cards forever", Trash shows its empty state and the button greys out. The web's Trash empties live.
- **Link title and address.** Open a link → tap its title → **Rename** → "Field notes" → Return: title and preview read "Field notes", on the web too. Blank brings the old title back. More → "Edit link…" → `example.org` → Save: "URL must use the http or https scheme" under the field, nothing saved. `https://example.org/` → Save: the sheet closes, "Link saved. The preview refreshes shortly.", and the preview updates.
- **Suggested by Teak.** Open any processed link: Tags shows "Suggested by Teak" with each tag Teak added. Tap one → **Keep**: it becomes a card tag, on the web too. Tap another → **Remove**: it's gone, here and on the web.
- **Smart folder search.** Press and hold (right-click on Mac) "Design links" → "Edit search…": its name and `tag:design type:link`. `tag:design` → Save: "Saved “Design links”", and the page lists every card tagged design. `trash` → Save: "Smart folders don't show Trash. ## Gotchas

- Renaming a seeded tag empties smart folders naming it ("Design links", "🎨 Moodboard ✨"); rename it back.
- The **Undo** toast lasts about 6 seconds.
- On iPad and iPhone a search hides the library toolbar; open More before typing.
- Debug builds read the stack's URLs and session only from launch environment variables; a home-screen relaunch keeps the last session.
- Extensions (share, Safari, controls) read the backend from Info.plist build settings, not launch variables, so a default Debug build's extension talks to the dev deployment and says "Sign in required".
- Simulator taps lag a few seconds, longer than the share extension's 3-second auto-close; to reach **Add to folders**, tap it the moment "Saved" shows, or use a throwaway build with a longer wait. Screenshots lag too: to see the sheet's states, record with `xcrun simctl io <device> recordVideo --codec h264 out.mp4` (stop with Ctrl-C) and tile frames with `ffmpeg -i out.mp4 -vf "fps=3,scale=300:-1,tile=8x3" -frames:v 1 sheet.png`.
- iOS shows share extensions in its own sheet whatever the `modalPresentationStyle`, so the card can't float over the sharing app.
- The Mac Debug app shares an installed Teak's bundle id; pass `-teak.appearance` instead of writing its defaults.
