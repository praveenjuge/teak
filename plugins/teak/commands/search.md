Call `teak_v1_search_cards` with `q` set to the search as written, keeping qualifiers such as `tag:`, `type:`, `site:`, `folder:` and `is:fav` unchanged. Summarize the best matches in a short list: each card's title, its type, and why it matches. If nothing matches, suggest one broader search.
