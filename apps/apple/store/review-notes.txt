APP OVERVIEW
Teak is a personal library for saving and finding inspiration: notes, links, images, videos, voice memos, documents, color palettes and quotes. This version is a new native SwiftUI app for iPhone and iPad that replaces our earlier build on the same listing.

HOW TO TEST
1. Tap "Continue with Apple", "Continue with Google" or "Register with Email" to create an account. No demo account is needed.
2. The library opens with a few starter cards. Tap a card to see its details.
3. Tap Add (the + tab) to write a note or paste a link, record a voice memo, or upload photos, videos and files.
4. Use Search: typed words like "image", "favorites" or "last week" become filters.
5. Long-press a card for actions (favorite, share, copy, delete). On iOS 27, swipe a card to favorite or delete it.
6. In Safari, tap Share and choose Teak to save the current page, or open the Teak Safari extension.
7. Settings shows the account, theme, and Delete Account.

SIGN-IN
Sign-in uses WorkOS AuthKit in a system web authentication session (Sign in with Apple, Google, or email and password). The session is stored in the device keychain.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > Delete Account permanently deletes the account, its cards and uploaded files, in the app.

EXTENSIONS
• Share extension: saves text, links, images, videos and files from other apps.
• Safari web extension: saves the current page.
• Shortcuts: a "Save to Teak" action saves text or a link.

PERMISSIONS (requested only when the feature is used)
• Camera: taking a photo or video for a card.
• Microphone: recording a voice memo.
• Photo Library: choosing photos and videos to add.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
• Convex: stores and syncs the user's library.
• WorkOS: sign-in.
• PostHog: app usage events and crash reports from the app (not the extensions), linked to the account ID and email.

PRIVACY
Each library is private to its account. No advertising and no selling or sharing of personal data. Privacy policy: https://teakvault.com/docs/privacy-policy
