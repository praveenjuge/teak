Teak is your personal vault for design inspiration and creative ideas. Save, organize, and find your ideas again on your phone, tablet, and the web.

CAPTURE ANYTHING
• Save links, images, videos, voice memos, and documents
• Share straight into Teak from any app with the Android share sheet
• Select text in any app and choose "Save to Teak"
• Jump to a new note or voice memo from the app icon

ORGANIZE YOUR WAY
• Add notes in Markdown and tags to keep everything searchable
• Mark favorites for quick access to your best ideas
• Filter by type, color, favorites, or Trash
• Search by words or dates, like "last week" or "June 2024"

MADE FOR ANDROID
• Material 3 design with light and dark themes
• Tablets, foldables, and desktop windows show your library and a card side by side
• Uploads keep going in the background with a progress notification

SYNC EVERYWHERE
• Your vault stays in sync across Android, iPhone, Mac, and the web
• Save from your browser with the Teak extension

YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
• Private by design: your content is never used for advertising
• Encrypted in transit
• Export or delete your data anytime
• Deleted cards stay in Trash for 30 days

TEAK IS FOR
• Designers collecting inspiration
• Creators organizing ideas and references
• Researchers saving articles and resources
• Anyone building a personal knowledge base
