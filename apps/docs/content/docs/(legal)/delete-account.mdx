---
title: Delete Your Account
description: How to delete your Teak account and everything in it
---

You can delete your Teak account and its data from any Teak app or by email. Deleting your account can't be undone.

## Before you delete

- **Pro ends right away.** Deleting your account cancels your Pro subscription immediately, without a refund for unused time. If you're within 30 days of your first payment, email us for a refund before you delete.
- **Export anything you want to keep.** Download your cards and original files as a ZIP from Settings in the web app.

## What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your sign-in, sessions, API keys, connected apps, cards, tags, folders, notes and uploaded files, along with the app usage events, crash reports and session recordings linked to your account in our analytics service, PostHog. It starts as soon as you confirm, and the [Privacy Policy](/docs/privacy-policy) commits to finishing it within 30 days.

We keep a minimal record of the deletion (your account ID, email and deletion date) to prove it happened and prevent abuse. Your billing details with our payment provider are anonymized; it keeps past orders as tax law requires.

If you only want to remove some cards, delete them instead. Deleted cards stay in Trash for 30 days, then are deleted for good.

## On Android

1. Open Teak and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete Account**, then **Delete Account** again.
3. Type "delete account" and tap **Delete Forever**.

## On iPhone, iPad or Mac

1. Open Teak and go to **Settings**.
2. Choose **Delete Account**, then confirm.
3. Type "delete account" to finish.

## On the web

1. Sign in at [app.teakvault.com](https://app.teakvault.com) and open **Settings**.
2. Choose **Delete account**.
3. Type "delete account" and confirm.

## By email

If you can't sign in, email hello@praveenjuge.com from the address on your Teak account and ask for your account to be deleted. We'll confirm when it's done, within 30 days.
