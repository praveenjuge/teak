---
title: Delete Your Account
description: How to delete your Teak account and everything in it
---

You can delete your Teak account and its data from any Teak app or by email. Deleting your account can't be undone.

## Before you delete

- **Cancel Pro first.** Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it in Settings → Plan on the web, then delete your account.
- **Export anything you want to keep.** Download your cards and original files as a ZIP from Settings in the web app.

## What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your sign-in, sessions, API keys, connected apps, cards, tags, folders, notes and uploaded files. It starts as soon as you confirm, and the [Privacy Policy](/docs/privacy-policy) commits to finishing it within 30 days.

We keep a minimal record of the deletion (your account ID, email and deletion date) to prove it happened and prevent abuse. Our payment provider keeps billing records as tax law requires.

App usage events and crash reports aren't removed automatically. To have those deleted too, email us after you delete your account.

If you only want to remove some cards, delete them instead. Deleted cards stay in Trash for 30 days, then are deleted for good.

## On Android

1. Open Teak and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete Account**, then **Delete Account** again.
3. Type "delete account" and tap **Delete Forever**.

## On iPhone, iPad or Mac

1. Open Teak and go to **Settings**.
2. Choose **Delete Account**, then confirm.
3. Type "delete account" to finish.

## On the web

1. Sign in at [app.teakvault.com](https://app.teakvault.com) and open **Settings**.
2. Choose **Delete account**.
3. Type "delete account" and confirm.

## By email

If you can't sign in, or the app says account changes are paused, email hello@praveenjuge.com from the address on your Teak account and ask for your account to be deleted. We'll confirm when it's done, within 30 days.
