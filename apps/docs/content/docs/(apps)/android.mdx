The Teak app for Android lets you save links, notes, voice memos, photos and files from your phone or tablet, share into Teak from any app, and browse your whole library, in sync with your other devices.

## Requirements

- An Android phone or tablet running Android 12 or newer
- A Teak account

## Install

Get Teak from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.praveenjuge.teak) and sign in with your Teak account.

## Sign In

On first launch, choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**, or **Sign in with email** or **Register with email**. Sign-in finishes on the Teak sign-in page in a browser tab, then brings you back to the app.

## Saving Content

Open the **Add** tab to save something new:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Note or link** | Type or paste any text or link |
| **Voice memo** | Record a voice note; Teak transcribes it |
| **Photos & videos** | Pick up to 20 photos or videos at once |
| **Camera** | Take a photo |
| **Files** | Pick up to 20 documents or other files at once |

Uploads keep going in the background, with a **Saving to Teak** notification that shows the progress, so you can leave the app while a large file uploads. Teak asks to send notifications before your first upload. **Uploads** on the Add tab lists what's waiting, uploading and saved.

If you reach the Free plan's card limit, Teak tells you before saving.

Press and hold the Teak icon on your home screen for **New note** and **Voice memo** shortcuts.

## Share Sheet

Save from another app without opening Teak:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share from any app">
    In any app (Chrome, Photos, Files, and so on), tap **Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Teak">
    Pick **Teak** from the share sheet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    A small sheet shows **Saving to Teak…** while it saves, then closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To save text from a web page or another app, select it and choose **Save to Teak** from the selection menu.

You can share links, text, images, videos and files, up to 20 at a time, with the same format support as the web app (see [Features](/docs/features)). If the sheet asks you to sign in, open Teak, sign in, then share again.

If you also installed Teak's web app from Chrome, the share sheet lists Teak twice. The Android app saves without opening anything, so keep that one. See [Install the web app](/docs/features#install-the-web-app).

## Your Library

Your library works like the iPhone app: search, filters, cards, links between notes, editing, folders and smart folders, tags, Revisit, Archive and Trash all behave the same way. See [Your Library](/docs/mobile#your-library) on the iPhone and iPad page.

A few things are different on Android:

- **Tablets:** on a wide screen, such as a tablet in landscape, the navigation rail lists **Home**, **Favorites**, **Archive**, **Trash**, **Folders**, **Add** and **Settings**. Favorites, Archive and Trash open as their own pages, so the library leaves out their filter chips. Phones and narrower windows keep the bottom bar, with the **Favorites**, **Archive** and **Trash** chips above your cards.
- **PDFs:** **View document** opens a PDF in the app, page by page. Tap the open-in icon at the top to read it in another app instead.
- **Palette colors:** **+ Add color** opens a sheet with a hex code field and a row of colors to pick from, instead of the system color picker.
- **Photos and voice memos:** tap a photo to see it full screen and pinch or double-tap to zoom. Tap or drag along a voice memo's waveform to jump to that point.
- **Offline:** when you lose your connection, Home says so and keeps the cards it already loaded. Favorite, archive, mark done, edit, move to Trash or restore a card, or save a new note or link, and the change shows right away and syncs when you're back online, even if you close the app. Images you've already seen stay available. To keep a card and its file for later, open the card and choose **More → Keep offline**; it then opens, plays and shows its PDF without a connection. **Remove offline copy** in the same menu frees the space. Opening the app with no connection doesn't load your library yet, and search, More like this, Refresh details and adding tags or folders still need a connection.

## Settings

The **Settings** tab lets you:

- Open **Tags** to rename, merge, or delete your tags
- Choose a **Theme**: **Auto** (follows your phone), **Light**, or **Dark**
- Turn on **Wallpaper colors** to color the app from your wallpaper (Material You), in light and dark. It's off until you turn it on, so Teak's red stays the default.
- Choose Teak's **Language**; it's saved to your account, so the web and your other devices follow it. See [Language](/docs/features#language)
- See your account email, card usage, and plan
- **Sign out**
- **Delete account**, after typing the confirmation phrase Teak shows. See [Delete Your Account](/docs/delete-account)
- Open **Help and docs** or the **Privacy policy**

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="Teak isn't in the share sheet">
    - Scroll the share sheet's app list; Android puts apps you use often first.
    - Make sure Teak is installed from Google Play and you've opened it once.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="A shared item or upload is missing">
    - Open the **Add** tab and check **Uploads**. A failed upload says why.
    - Check that you were signed in when you shared the item.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="A video won't play">
    Some phones can't play every video format. Tap **Open in another app** on the card.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Still stuck?">
    Reach out via [Support](/docs/support).
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
