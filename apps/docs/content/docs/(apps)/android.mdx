Save links, notes, voice memos, photos and files from your Android phone or tablet, share into Teak from any app, and browse your whole library, in sync with your other devices.

## Requirements

- An Android phone or tablet running Android 12 or newer
- A Teak account

## Install and sign in

Get Teak from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.praveenjuge.teak). On first launch, choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**, or **Sign in with email** or **Register with email**. Sign-in finishes on the Teak sign-in page in a browser tab, then brings you back to the app.

## Saving content

Open the **Add** tab:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Note or link** | Type or paste any text or link |
| **Voice memo** | Record a voice note; Teak transcribes it |
| **Photos & videos** | Pick up to 20 photos or videos at once |
| **Camera** | Take a photo |
| **Files** | Pick up to 20 documents or other files at once |

Uploads keep going in the background with a **Saving to Teak** notification, so you can leave the app while a large file uploads. **Uploads** on the Add tab lists what's waiting, uploading and saved. On the Free plan, Teak tells you before saving past the card limit.

Press and hold the Teak icon on your home screen for **New note** and **Voice memo** shortcuts.

## Share sheet

Save from another app without opening Teak:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share from any app">
    In any app (Chrome, Photos, Files and so on), tap **Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Teak">
    Pick **Teak** from the share sheet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    A small sheet shows **Saving to Teak…** while it saves, then closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can share links, text, images, videos and files, up to 20 at a time, in any [format the web app takes](/docs/features#card-types). To save selected text, choose **Save to Teak** from the selection menu. If the sheet asks you to sign in, open Teak, sign in, then share again.

If you also [installed Teak's web app](/docs/features#install-the-web-app) from Chrome, the share sheet lists Teak twice. Keep the Android app's: it saves without opening anything.

## Your library

Search, filters, folders, tags, Revisit, Archive and Trash work as everywhere else (see [Features](/docs/features)), with the same controls as the [iPhone app](/docs/mobile#your-library). On Android:

- **Tablets:** on a wide screen, a navigation rail lists **Home**, **Favorites**, **Archive**, **Trash**, **Folders**, **Add** and **Settings**. Phones keep the bottom bar, with **Favorites**, **Archive** and **Trash** chips above your cards.
- **PDFs:** **View document** opens a PDF in the app, page by page; the open-in icon at the top reads it in another app.
- **Palette colors:** **+ Add color** opens a sheet with a hex code field and a row of colors.
- **Photos and voice memos:** tap a photo to see it full screen and pinch or double-tap to zoom. Tap or drag along a voice memo's waveform to jump to that point.

### Offline

When you lose your connection, Home says so and keeps the cards it already loaded. Favoriting, archiving, marking done, editing, moving to Trash, restoring, and saving a new note or link show right away and sync when you're back online, even if you close the app. Images you've seen stay available.

To keep a card and its file for later, open it and choose **More → Keep offline**; it then opens, plays and shows its PDF without a connection. **Remove offline copy** frees the space. Opening the app with no connection doesn't load your library, and search, More like this, Refresh details and adding tags or folders need a connection.

## Settings

The **Settings** tab has:

- **Tags**, to rename, merge or delete tags
- **Theme**: **Auto**, **Light** or **Dark**
- **Wallpaper colors**, to color the app from your wallpaper (Material You); off by default
- [**Language**](/docs/features#language)
- Your account email, card usage and plan
- **Sign out**, and **Delete account** after typing the confirmation phrase (see [Delete your account](/docs/delete-account))
- **Help and docs** and the **Privacy policy**

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="Teak isn't in the share sheet">
    - Scroll the share sheet's app list; Android puts apps you use often first.
    - Make sure Teak is installed from Google Play and you've opened it once.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="A shared item or upload is missing">
    - Open the **Add** tab and check **Uploads**. A failed upload says why.
    - Check that you were signed in when you shared the item.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="A video won't play">
    Some phones can't play every video format. Tap **Open in another app** on the card.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Still stuck?">
    Reach out via [Support](/docs/support).
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
