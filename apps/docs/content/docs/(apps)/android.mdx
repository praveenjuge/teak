Save links, notes, voice memos, photos and files on Android phones and tablets, share from any app, and browse your library, synced across devices.

## Requirements

- An Android phone or tablet running Android 12 or newer
- A Teak account

## Install and sign in

Get Teak from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.praveenjuge.teak). Then choose **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple**, **Sign in with email** or **Register with email**; sign-in finishes on the Teak sign-in page in a browser tab and returns you to the app.

## Saving content

Open the **Add** tab:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Note or link** | Type or paste text or a link |
| **Voice memo** | Record a voice note; Teak transcribes it |
| **Photos & videos** | Pick up to 20 at once |
| **Camera** | Take a photo |
| **Files** | Pick up to 20 files at once |

Uploads continue in the background, with a **Saving to Teak** notification. **Uploads** on the Add tab lists what's waiting, uploading and saved. On the Free plan, Teak warns you before saving past the card limit.

Press and hold the Teak icon for **New note** and **Voice memo** shortcuts.

## Widgets

Touch and hold your home screen, tap **Widgets**, and find Teak.

- **Quick Capture** starts a note, voice memo, photos or files; widen it for a large **New note** button and the camera.
- **Favorites** shows your newest favorites: one when small, four when wide, six when also tall. Tap to open. It updates while Teak runs, skips archived cards, and empties on sign-out.

## Share sheet

Save from another app without opening Teak:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share from any app">
    Tap **Share** in Chrome, Photos, Files or any other app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Teak">
    Pick **Teak**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    A small sheet shows **Saving to Teak…**, then closes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Share links, text, images, videos and files, up to 20 at a time, in any [format the web app takes](/docs/features#card-types). For selected text, choose **Save to Teak** in the selection menu. If asked to sign in, sign in in Teak and share again.

If you also [installed Teak's web app](/docs/features#install-the-web-app) from Chrome, Teak appears twice; pick the Android app, which saves without opening anything.

## Your library

Search, filters, folders, tags, Revisit, Archive and Trash work as in [Features](/docs/features), with the [iPhone app's](/docs/mobile#your-library) controls. On Android:

- **Tablets:** a navigation rail lists **Home**, **Favorites**, **Archive**, **Trash**, **Folders**, **Add** and **Settings**. Phones use the bottom bar, with **Favorites**, **Archive** and **Trash** chips above your cards.
- **PDFs:** **View document** opens a PDF in the app; the open-in icon at the top opens it in another app.
- **Palette colors:** **+ Add color** opens a sheet with a hex code field and a row of colors.
- **Photos and voice memos:** tap a photo for full screen; pinch or double-tap to zoom. Tap or drag a voice memo's waveform to jump there.

### Offline

Offline, Home says so and keeps the cards it loaded. Favoriting, archiving, marking done, editing, trashing, restoring, and saving a note or link show at once and sync when you're back online, even if you close the app. Images you've seen stay available.

**More → Keep offline** keeps a card and its file so it opens, plays and shows its PDF offline; **Remove offline copy** frees the space. Opening the app offline doesn't load your library, and search, More like this, Refresh details and adding tags or folders need a connection.

## Settings

The **Settings** tab has:

- **Tags**, to rename, merge or delete tags
- **Theme**: **Auto**, **Light** or **Dark**
- **Wallpaper colors** (Material You), off by default
- [**Language**](/docs/features#language)
- Your email, card usage and plan
- **Sign out**, and **Delete account** after typing the confirmation phrase (see [Delete your account](/docs/delete-account))
- **Help and docs** and the **Privacy policy**

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="Teak isn't in the share sheet">
    - Scroll the list; Android puts frequent apps first.
    - Install Teak from Google Play and open it once.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="A shared item or upload is missing">
    - Check **Uploads** on the **Add** tab; a failed upload says why.
    - Check you were signed in when you shared it.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="A video won't play">
    Some phones can't play every format; tap **Open in another app** on the card.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Still stuck?">
    See [Support](/docs/support).
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
