Teak for Mac brings your library and Safari saving together in a native Mac app. An internet connection is required to sign in, browse, and save.

## Install and sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install">
    Get [Teak for Mac](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teak-for-mac/id6770003409?mt=12) from the Mac App Store, then open it from Applications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Choose **Sign in**, complete sign-in in your browser, and approve **Teak for Mac**. Return to the app to open your library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Safari saving">
    Open **Safari → Settings → Extensions** and enable **Teak**. Click its toolbar icon to save the current page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Mac app and Safari extension share your connection. With WorkOS, a confirmed Safari disconnect signs it out across all its installations; your browser session stays separate. Reconnecting can take about five minutes. If this Mac still has a connection from before the October 7 sign-in update, or WorkOS already rejects your refresh credential, sign-out clears only this Mac’s connection and tells you that other installations may remain connected. Manage those in **Settings → Security → Connected apps**.

## Your library

Search your cards by text, tags, dates, type, color, or visual style. Combine filters, show Favorites, or browse Trash. Open a card to read its content, notes, summary, and tags, play media, or preview a PDF.

Edit text and quotes directly, then choose **Save changes**. Use **Edit Notes** and **Manage Tags** to organize a card. Add favorites, move cards to Trash, restore them, or confirm **Delete Forever** to remove them permanently. Changes sync to your other Teak apps.

## Capture

Write or paste into **Write a note…**, then save. Links and other supported content are recognized automatically, just as on the web. Expand the editor for longer notes.

Use **Upload files** or drag files onto the library to save images, videos, audio, and documents. Choose **Record audio**, allow microphone access when prompted, then **Stop and Save**. Click the Safari toolbar icon to save the current page.

New cards appear after saving. If a save fails, check your connection and try again.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Action | Shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| Settings | ⌘, |
| New note | ⌘N |
| Find in library | ⌘F |
| Upload files | ⇧⌘U |
| Refresh library | ⌘R or ⌥⌘R |
| Full screen | ⌃⌘F |
| Close window | ⌘W |

Refresh shows progress and a short confirmation in the library. Standard Mac editing shortcuts are available.

## Settings and menu bar

Open **Settings** to view your account and usage, change appearance, manage the Safari extension, or sign out. Turn on the optional menu bar item for quick access to Teak.

API keys, import, export, billing, and connection revocation are available in [the web app](https://app.teakvault.com).

## Updates and limitations

Updates come through the Mac App Store. Enable automatic updates there or check its **Updates** section.

The app requires a connection and does not cache your library for offline use. The Mac app does not send crash reports or usage telemetry.

For help, see [Support](/docs/support).
