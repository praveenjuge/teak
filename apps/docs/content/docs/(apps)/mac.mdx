The native Mac app has your whole library, quick saving from any app, and a Safari extension. It's the same App Store app as on iPhone and iPad.

## Requirements

- A Mac running macOS 26 or newer
- A Teak account

## Install and sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install">
    Get [Teak](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teak-save-inspirations/id6756574989?platform=mac&mt=12) from the Mac App Store and open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Choose **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with Google**, **Login with Email** or **Register with Email** (email finishes on the Teak sign-in page).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your library

The sidebar has **Home**, **Add**, **Favorites**, **Archive**, **Tags**, **Trash** and **Settings**, then your **Folders** and a **Types** section per card type. Search takes the web's filters ([Smart search](/docs/features#smart-search)); a word like `image`, `favorites`, a color or `#tag` becomes a filter when you press Return.

- **More** (the circled ellipsis in the toolbar): **Select** cards, start **Revisit**, show **Favorites**, **Archive** or **Trash**, narrow by type or color, sort by **Newest First**, **Oldest First** or **Shuffle**, **Save as Smart Folder…**, or **Clear Filters**.
- **Right-click a card** to open, copy, share, tag, favorite, archive, select or trash it, for **More Like This** or **Refresh Details**, or to copy its link or open it on the web.
- **Folders:** **New folder** ends the sidebar's **Folders**. Right-click a folder to rename or delete it, or a smart folder to **Edit search…**.
- **Tags:** right-click a tag to **Rename…**, **Merge Into…** or **Delete…** it.
- **Revisit** (⌃⌘R): ← or **Archive**, → or **Keep**, Z to undo.
- **Trash:** restore cards or delete them forever; **Empty Trash** is in the toolbar and **More**.
- On macOS 27 and later, swipe a card to favorite, archive or delete.

In an open card:

- The toolbar has a link's done toggle (**Mark as read**, **Mark as watched** and so on). Click its title to **Rename** it; **Edit link…** in **More** changes its address.
- While editing a note, type `[[` to link a card; pick it with the arrow keys and Return.
- Click a photo to open its own window: pinch or double-click to zoom, or use **Zoom In** (⌘=), **Zoom Out** (⌘-) and **Zoom to Fit** (⌘0).
- Click or drag a voice memo's waveform to jump there.
- In a palette, click **+** to add a color; right-click one for **Remove color**.

See [Features](/docs/features) for how these work.

## Saving

Use the toolbar's **+**, the **Add** page, or the **File** menu:

- **New Note** (⌘N): text or a link, in the Quick Capture window.
- **Voice Memo** (⇧⌘R): records in a small window above other apps.
- **Upload Files…** (⇧⌘U): images, videos, audio and documents.

Or drag files, images, links or text onto the library window, or save the clipboard with ⌘V there (or **Edit › Paste as New Card**, ⌃⌘V).

### From any app

- **Quick Capture** (**File › Quick Capture…**) opens a small window over other apps for a note or link; ⌘Return saves. Turn on **Quick Capture shortcut** in **Settings › General** to open it anywhere with ⌃⌥⌘T.
- **Save to Teak** in **Services** (the menu or right-click submenu) saves selected text, links, images or files.
- **Menu bar item:** turn on **Show Teak in the menu bar** in **Settings › General**. Drop files, images, links or text on its icon, or use its menu for **Quick Capture…**, **Voice Memo** and **Paste as New Card**. Teak then keeps running after you close its window.
- **Safari:** turn on the Teak extension and click it in the toolbar to save the page ([Safari](/docs/extension#safari)).

### Shortcuts, Spotlight, widgets and Control Center

- Shortcuts actions ([Shortcuts and Siri](/docs/mobile#shortcuts-and-siri)): **Save to Teak**, **Search Teak**, **Open Card**, **Add to Folder**, **New Note**, **Record Voice Memo**, **Add Photos** and **Upload Files**.
- Spotlight finds cards by title and opens them in Teak. Archived, hidden and trashed cards are left out; signing out removes them.
- For the **Quick Capture** or **Favorites** [widget](/docs/mobile#widgets) on the desktop or in Notification Center, right-click the desktop, choose **Edit Widgets…** and search for Teak.
- Add **New Note**, **Voice Memo**, **Add Photos** or **Upload Files** to Control Center or the menu bar in **System Settings → Control Center**.

## Settings

Open **Settings** (⌘,) from the **Teak** menu or the sidebar:

- **General**: appearance (Auto, Light or Dark), [language](/docs/features#language), menu bar item, Quick Capture shortcut and Safari extension.
- **Account**: your email, usage and plan, **Upgrade…** or **Manage…** (opens **Settings → Plan** on the web), **Sign out** and **Delete account**.
- **About**: the app version.

API keys, import and export are in [the web app](https://app.teakvault.com).

## Updates

Updates come through the Mac App Store: turn on automatic updates or check **Updates**.
