The Teak app runs natively on your Mac, with your whole library, quick ways to save from any app, and a Safari extension. It's the same app as on iPhone and iPad, from the same App Store listing.

## Requirements

- A Mac running macOS 26 or newer
- A Teak account

## Install and sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install">
    Get [Teak](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teak-save-inspirations/id6756574989?platform=mac&mt=12) from the Mac App Store, then open it from Applications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Choose **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with Google**, or use **Login with Email** or **Register with Email** to finish on the Teak sign-in page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your library

The sidebar has **Home**, **Add**, **Favorites**, **Archive**, **Tags**, **Trash** and **Settings**, then your **Folders** and a **Types** section for each card type. Search works as on the web, filters included (see [Smart search](/docs/features#smart-search)); a word like `image`, `favorites`, a color or `#tag` becomes a filter when you press Return.

- **More** (the circled ellipsis in the toolbar): **Select** cards, start **Revisit**, show **Favorites**, **Archive** or **Trash**, narrow by type or color, sort by **Newest First**, **Oldest First** or **Shuffle**, **Save as Smart Folder…**, or **Clear Filters**.
- **Right-click a card** to open, copy or share it, add tags, favorite or archive it, see **More Like This**, **Refresh Details**, copy a link to the card, open it on the web, select it, or move it to Trash.
- **Folders:** **New folder** is the last row of **Folders** in the sidebar. Right-click a folder to rename or delete it, or a smart folder to **Edit search…**.
- **Tags:** right-click a tag in **Tags** to **Rename…**, **Merge Into…** or **Delete…** it.
- **Revisit** (⌃⌘R): press ← or **Archive**, → or **Keep**, and Z to undo the last one.
- **Trash:** restore cards or delete them forever; **Empty Trash** is in the toolbar and the **More** button.
- On macOS 27 and later, swipe a card to favorite, archive or delete it.

In an open card:

- The toolbar has the link's done toggle (**Mark as read**, **Mark as watched** and so on). Click a link's title to **Rename** it, and use **Edit link…** in **More** to change its address.
- Type `[[` while editing a note to link another card; pick one with the arrow keys and Return.
- Click a photo to open it in its own window: pinch or double-click to zoom, or use **Zoom In** (⌘=), **Zoom Out** (⌘-) and **Zoom to Fit** (⌘0).
- Click or drag along a voice memo's waveform to jump to that point.
- In a palette, click **+** after its colors to add one, and right-click a color for **Remove color**.

How these features work is the same everywhere: see [Features](/docs/features).

## Saving

Use the **+** button in the toolbar, the **Add** page, or the **File** menu:

- **New Note** (⌘N) for text or a link, in the Quick Capture window.
- **Voice Memo** (⇧⌘R) to record in a small window that stays above other apps.
- **Upload Files…** (⇧⌘U) for images, videos, audio and documents.

You can also drag files, images, links or text onto the library window, or press ⌘V in the library (or **Edit › Paste as New Card**, ⌃⌘V) to save what's on the clipboard.

### From any app

- **Quick Capture** (**File › Quick Capture…**) opens a small window for a note or link over other apps; press ⌘Return to save. Turn on **Quick Capture shortcut** in **Settings › General** to open it from anywhere with ⌃⌥⌘T.
- **Save to Teak** in the **Services** menu (or a right-click's **Services** submenu) saves selected text, links, images or files.
- **Menu bar item:** turn on **Show Teak in the menu bar** in **Settings › General**. Drop files, images, links or text on its icon, or open its menu for **Quick Capture…**, **Voice Memo** and **Paste as New Card**. While it's on, Teak keeps running after you close its window.
- **Safari:** turn on the Teak extension and click it in Safari's toolbar to save the page. See [Safari](/docs/extension#safari).

### Shortcuts, Spotlight and Control Center

- The Shortcuts app has Teak's actions: **Save to Teak**, **Search Teak**, **Open Card**, **Add to Folder**, **New Note** and **Record Voice Memo**. See [Shortcuts and Siri](/docs/mobile#shortcuts-and-siri).
- Spotlight finds your cards by title and opens them in Teak. Archived, hidden and trashed cards aren't included, and signing out removes them.
- Add **New Note** or **Voice Memo** to Control Center or the menu bar from **System Settings → Control Center**.

## Settings

Open **Settings** (⌘,) from the **Teak** menu or the sidebar:

- **General**: appearance (Auto, Light or Dark), [language](/docs/features#language), the menu bar item, the Quick Capture shortcut, and whether the Safari extension is on.
- **Account**: your email, usage and plan, with **Upgrade…** or **Manage…** (opens **Settings → Plan** on the web), **Sign out** and **Delete account**.
- **About**: the app version.

API keys, import and export are in [the web app](https://app.teakvault.com).

## Updates

Updates come through the Mac App Store. Turn on automatic updates there, or check its **Updates** section.
