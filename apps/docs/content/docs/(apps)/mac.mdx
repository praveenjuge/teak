The Teak app runs natively on your Mac with your whole library, quick ways to save from any app, and a Safari extension. It's the same app as on iPhone and iPad, from the same App Store listing.

## Requirements

- A Mac running macOS 26 or newer
- A Teak account

## Install and sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install">
    Get [Teak](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teak-save-inspirations/id6756574989?mt=12) from the Mac App Store, then open it from Applications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Choose **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with Google**, or use **Login with Email** or **Register with Email** to finish on the Teak sign-in page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your library

The sidebar has **Home**, **Add**, **Favorites**, **Archive**, **Tags**, **Trash** and **Settings**, then your **Folders** and a **Types** section for notes, links, images, and the other card types.

Search by words or by when you saved something, such as "last week" or "March 2026". Teak also searches by meaning, so "cozy reading corner" can find a photo that never says those words. Words like "image", "favorites", "trash", a color, a hex code, or `#tag` turn into filters in the search field; press Return to add one. You can also type `is:archived`, `is:done`, `is:todo`, `price:<100`, `text:"total due"` or `mood:calm`, the same filters the web app understands. Put quotes around words that belong together.

The **More** button (the circled ellipsis) in the toolbar lets you **Select** cards, start **Revisit**, show **Favorites**, **Archive** or **Trash**, narrow by type or color, and sort by **Newest First**, **Oldest First** or **Shuffle** (**Reshuffle** draws a new order). **Save as Smart Folder…** saves the current search, and **Clear Filters** goes back to everything.

Click a card to open it. Right-click a card to open its link, copy or share it, add tags, favorite or archive it, see **More Like This**, **Refresh Details**, copy a link to the card, open it on the web, select it, or delete it. Deleted cards move to Trash, where you can restore them or delete them forever.

In an open card:

- Links you mean to read, watch or buy have a toggle in the toolbar: **Mark as read**, **Mark as watched**, **Mark as bought**, or **Mark as done**. Done links show a check on their tile.
- Links between notes show as pills with the linked card's current title; click one to open it. A link to a card in Trash, deleted or not yours shows **Missing card**. **Linked from** lists the notes that link to the card.
- The details show a product's price, when a page was published, camera details for photos, and **From** with the site for text saved from a web page.
- **Folders** in the details files the card in folders.
- Adding the tag `secret` or `hidden` while editing hides the card everywhere except a search for that tag.

### Archive

Archive cards you're done with to keep them out of your library without deleting them: right-click a card, use the **Card** menu, or the card's **More** menu. A message lets you **Undo**. **Archive** in the sidebar shows them, where you can unarchive them.

### Folders and smart folders

Your folders sit in the sidebar. Click **+** next to **Folders** to make one, and right-click a folder to rename or delete it (**Undo** brings it back; its cards stay in your library). A smart folder is a saved search that shows every card that matches, including cards you save later.

### Tags

**Tags** in the sidebar lists every tag with its card count. Right-click a tag to **Rename…**, **Merge Into…** another tag, or **Delete…** it from every card. Teak shows the progress while it works.

### Revisit

Choose **Revisit** from the **More** button or **View › Revisit** (⌃⌘R) to go through up to 20 shuffled cards from the current view, one at a time. Press ← or click **Archive** for cards you're done with, → or **Keep** for the rest, and Z to undo the last one.

### Selecting cards

To work with several cards, choose **Select** from the **More** button or **View › Select Cards**, click the cards you want, then archive them, add tags, add them to a folder, favorite them, or move them to Trash. In Archive, unarchive them; in Trash, restore them or delete them forever.

## Saving

Use the **+** button in the toolbar, the **Add** page, or the **File** menu:

- **New Note** (⌘N) for text or a link.
- **Voice Memo** (⇧⌘R) to record audio.
- **Upload Files…** (⇧⌘U) for images, videos, audio, and documents.

You can also drag files, images, links, or text onto the library window, or press ⌘V in the library to save what's on the clipboard. **Edit › Paste as New Card** (⌃⌘V) saves the clipboard as a card.

### From any app

- **Quick Capture** (**File › Quick Capture…**) opens a small window for a note or a link, even over other apps. Write or paste, then press ⌘Return. To open it from anywhere with ⌃⌥⌘T, turn on **Quick Capture shortcut** in **Settings › General**.
- **Save to Teak** in the **Services** menu, or in a right-click's **Services** submenu, saves selected text, links, images, or files.
- **Menu bar item.** Turn on **Show Teak in the menu bar** in **Settings › General**. Drop files, images, links, or text on its icon, or open its menu for **Quick Capture…** and **Paste as New Card**. While it's on, Teak keeps running after you close its window.

A short confirmation appears near the top of the screen after each save.

## Safari extension

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Open **Settings › General**. The **Safari Extension** section shows whether it's on. Choose **Open Safari Settings…**, then turn on **Teak** under **Extensions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a page">
    Click **Teak** in Safari's toolbar to save the page you're on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

Open **Settings** (⌘,) from the **Teak** menu or the sidebar:

- **General**: appearance (Auto, Light, or Dark), Teak's language (saved to your account; see [Language](/docs/features#language)), the menu bar item, the Quick Capture shortcut, and the Safari extension's status.
- **Account**: your email, usage, and plan. On the Free plan, **Upgrade…** opens billing on the web; on Pro, **Manage…** does. **Sign out** and **Delete account** are here too.
- **About**: the app version.

API keys, import, export, and billing are available in [the web app](https://app.teakvault.com).
