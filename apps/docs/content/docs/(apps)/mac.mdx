Teak for Mac brings your library and Safari saving together in a native Mac app. An internet connection is required to sign in, browse, and save.

## Install and sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install">
    Get [Teak for Mac](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teak-for-mac/id6770003409?mt=12) from the Mac App Store, then open it from Applications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Choose **Sign in**, complete sign-in in your browser, and approve **Teak for Mac**. Return to the app to open your library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Safari saving">
    Open **Safari → Settings → Extensions** and enable **Teak**. Click its toolbar icon to save the current page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The Mac app and Safari extension share your connection; your browser session stays separate. Signing out disconnects Teak for Mac across all its installations, and reconnecting can take about five minutes. If the saved connection has already expired, sign-out clears only this Mac and says so — disconnect other installations in **Settings → Security → Connected apps**.

## Your library

Search your cards by text, tags, dates, type, color, or visual style. Combine filters, show Favorites, or browse Trash. Click a tag on a card to show every card with exactly that tag. Open a card to read its content, notes, summary, and tags, play media, or preview a PDF. Notes and Markdown files show formatted, and source files show with line numbers.

Text cards open formatted. Switch to **Edit** to change the Markdown, then choose **Save changes**; quotes are edited directly. Use **Edit Notes** and **Manage Tags** to organize a card, including removing tags Teak added. Add favorites, move cards to Trash, restore them, or confirm **Delete Forever** to remove them permanently. **Copy Link** and **Open on Web** share or open the card on the web. Changes sync to your other Teak apps.

To work with several cards, ⌘-click them or choose **Select** from a card's menu. The bar at the bottom favorites, unfavorites, or moves them to Trash together; in Trash it restores them or deletes them forever. Press Esc to stop selecting.

## Capture

Write or paste into **Write a note…**, then save. Links and other supported content are recognized automatically, just as on the web. Expand the editor for longer notes.

Use **Upload files** or drag files onto the library to save images, videos, audio, and documents. Press ⌘V on the library, outside the search field, to save what's on the clipboard: copied files, an image, a link, or text. Choose **Record audio**, allow microphone access when prompted, then **Stop and Save**. Click the Safari toolbar icon to save the current page.

You can also save without opening the library:

- **Quick Capture** (⌃⌥⌘T from any app, or **File → Quick Capture…**) opens a small note window. Write or paste, then press ⌘Return. Turn the shortcut off in **Settings**.
- **Save to Teak** in the **Services** menu, or a right-click's **Services** submenu, saves selected text or files from any app.
- With the menu bar item on, drop files, images, links, or text onto its icon, or choose **Paste as New Card**.

A short confirmation appears near the top of the screen after each save.

New cards appear after saving. If a save fails, check your connection and try again.

| --- | --- |
| Settings | ⌘, |

| Full screen | ⌃⌘F |

Refresh shows progress and a short confirmation in the library. Standard Mac editing shortcuts are available.

## Settings and menu bar

Open **Settings** to view your account, usage, and plan, change appearance, manage the Safari extension, or sign out. On the Free plan, **Upgrade…** opens billing on the web; on Pro, **Manage…** does. If you reach the Free plan's card limit while saving, Teak offers the same upgrade. Turn on the optional menu bar item for quick access to Teak.

API keys, import, export, billing, and connection revocation are available in [the web app](https://app.teakvault.com).

## Updates and limitations

Updates come through the Mac App Store. Enable automatic updates there or check its **Updates** section.

:::note[Online only]
The app requires a connection and does not cache your library for offline use.
:::

The Mac app does not send crash reports or usage telemetry.
