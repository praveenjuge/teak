Save, record voice notes and browse your library on iPhone and iPad, synced across devices.

## Requirements

- iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or newer
- A Teak account

## Install and sign in

Download Teak from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teak-save-inspirations/id6756574989). Then choose **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with Google**, **Login with Email** or **Register with Email** (email finishes on the Teak sign-in page).

## Saving content

Open the **Add** tab:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Note or Link** | Type or paste text or a link |
| **Voice Memo** | Record a voice note; Teak transcribes it |
| **Photos & Videos** | Pick up to 20 at once |
| **Camera** | Take a photo or video |
| **Files** | Pick up to 20 files at once |

Or use the [share sheet](#share-sheet), [Safari extension](/docs/extension#safari) or [Shortcuts and Siri](#shortcuts-and-siri). On the Free plan, Teak warns you before saving past the card limit.

## Your library

**Home** shows your cards, newest first, with more columns on iPad. Search takes the web's filters ([Smart search](/docs/features#smart-search)); each one you type, such as `#design`, `is:todo` or `price:<100`, becomes a chip.

- **More** (the circled ellipsis): **Select** cards, start **Revisit**, show **Favorites**, **Archive** or **Trash**, narrow by **Type** or **Color**, sort by **Newest First**, **Oldest First** or **Shuffle**, **Save as Smart Folder…**, or **Clear Filters**.
- **Press and hold a card** to favorite, archive, select or delete it, or for **More Like This** and **Refresh Details**. On iOS 27 and later, swipe to favorite, archive or delete.
- **Folders:** the **Folders** tab (iPhone) or sidebar (iPad) lists folders and smart folders, with **New Folder**. Press and hold a folder to rename or delete it, or a smart folder to **Edit search…**.
- **Tags:** **Settings › Tags** on iPhone, or **Tags** in the iPad sidebar. Press and hold a tag to **Rename…**, **Merge Into…** or **Delete…** it.
- **Revisit:** swipe left or tap **Archive**, swipe right or tap **Keep**; **Undo** restores the last archived card.
- **Trash:** tap a card to **Restore** it or **Delete Forever**; **Empty Trash** is in the toolbar and **More**.

Tap a card to open it; on iPad, its notes, tags, summary and details sit alongside.

- Tap a photo for full screen; pinch or double-tap to zoom, swipe down to close.
- **View Document** opens a PDF or other file; Markdown and code show inline.
- Tap or drag a voice memo's waveform to jump there.
- Tap a tag to search it.
- The toolbar has a link's done toggle (**Mark as read**, **Mark as watched** and so on). Tap its title to **Rename** it; **Edit link…** in **More** changes its address.
- The pencil edits the card's text, notes and tags; tap **Save** when done.
- In a palette, tap **+** to add a color, or press and hold one for **Remove color**.

See [Features](/docs/features) for how these work.

### Links between notes

While editing a note, type `[[` and tap a card to link it. Links show as pills that open the card. See [Link cards](/docs/features#link-cards).

## Share sheet

Save from any app without opening Teak:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share from any app">
    Tap **Share** in Safari, Photos, Files or any other app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Teak">
    Pick **Teak**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    Teak saves it and shows the status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a single item, add comma-separated tags and a note before tapping **Save**. **Add to folders** files everything you shared. Untouched, the sheet closes itself. If asked to sign in, sign in in Teak and share again.

Share links, text, images and files up to 100 MB in any [format the web app takes](/docs/features#card-types); Markdown becomes a text card, up to 512 KiB.

## Shortcuts and Siri

Shortcuts lists Teak's actions under **Teak**; Siri runs each by name, such as "Search Teak" or "New note in Teak":

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Save to Teak** | Saves text or a link without opening Teak |
| **Search Teak** | Finds up to 20 cards with the app's search, filters included; turn on **Include archived cards** to search Archive too |
| **Open Card** | Opens a card in Teak |
| **Add to Folder** | Adds cards, such as Search Teak's results, to a folder |
| **New Note** | Opens a new note |
| **Record Voice Memo** | Opens the voice memo recorder |
| **Add Photos** | Opens the photo picker |
| **Take Photo** | Opens the camera (iPhone and iPad) |
| **Upload Files** | Opens the file picker |

Trashed and hidden cards never appear. If asked to sign in, sign in in Teak and run the action again.

## Spotlight

Spotlight finds cards by title and opens them in Teak. Cards are added as you browse, so one saved elsewhere appears after you next open Teak. Archived, hidden and trashed cards are left out; signing out removes all.

## Widgets

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**, and search for Teak.

- **Quick Capture** starts a note, voice memo, photos, camera or files; on the Lock Screen, a note.
- **Favorites** shows your newest favorites: one in small, four in medium, six in large, one on the Lock Screen. Tap to open. It updates while Teak is open, skips archived and hidden cards, and empties on sign-out.

## Control Center and the Action button

Add **New Note**, **Voice Memo**, **Add Photos**, **Take Photo** or **Upload Files** to Control Center or the Lock Screen: open Control Center, tap **+**, then **Add a Control**, and search for Teak. For the Action button, choose **Controls** or **Shortcut** in **Settings → Action Button** and pick one.

## Settings

The **Settings** tab has **Tags** (iPhone), **Appearance** (Auto, Light or Dark), [**Language**](/docs/features#language), your email, card usage and plan, **Sign out** and **Delete account**.
