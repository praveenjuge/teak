The Teak app lets you save inspiration on the go, capture voice notes, upload files, and browse your full library on iPhone and iPad, synced in real time with your other devices.

## Requirements

- iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or newer
- A Teak account

## Install

Download the app from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teak-save-inspirations/id6756574989) and sign in with your Teak account.

## Sign In

On first launch, choose **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with Google**, or use **Login with Email** or **Register with Email** to finish on the Teak sign-in page.

## Saving Content

Open the **Add** tab to save new content manually:

| Action              | What it does                                                    |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Note or Link**    | Type or paste any text or link                                  |
| **Voice Memo**      | Record a voice note; Teak auto-transcribes it                   |
| **Photos & Videos** | Pick up to 20 photos or videos at once                          |
| **Camera**          | Take a photo or video                                           |
| **Files**           | Pick up to 20 documents or other files at once                  |

If you reach the Free plan's card limit, Teak tells you before saving.

## Your Library

**Home** shows your cards, newest first, with more columns on iPad. Search by words or by when you saved something, such as "last week" or "March 2026". Teak also searches by meaning, so "cozy reading corner" can find a photo that never says those words. Words like "image", "favorites", or a color turn into filters in the search bar; press Return to add one.

You can also type filters the way the web app does, and each becomes a chip:

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| `#tag` or `tag:design` | Cards with that tag |
| `is:archived` | Archived cards |
| `is:done` / `is:todo` | Links you've marked done, or ones still to read, watch or buy |
| `price:<100` | Product links under (or `>` over) a price |
| `text:"total due"` | Words read from images and scans |
| `mood:calm` | Images by mood |

Put quotes around words that belong together, like `text:"total due"` or `"design system"`.

Tap the **More** button (the circled ellipsis) to **Select** cards, start **Revisit**, show **Favorites**, **Archive** or **Trash**, narrow by **Type** or **Color**, or sort by **Newest First**, **Oldest First** or **Shuffle** (choose **Reshuffle** for a new order). **Save as Smart Folder…** saves the current search. The title shows what you're looking at; choose **Clear Filters** to go back to everything.

Tap a card to open it. Notes show their formatting, tables and checklists (tap a checkbox to tick it), links show their details (like price, rating or when they were published) and any photos from the post, photos show camera details, and documents show inline when they're Markdown or code. Tap **View Document** to read a PDF or other file. Tap a photo to see it full screen: pinch or double-tap to zoom, and swipe down to close it. Tap or drag along a voice memo's waveform to jump to that point; it shows how far in you are and how long the recording is. Tap a tag to search for it. Text saved from a web page shows **From** and the site at the bottom; tap it to open the page.

On iPad, a card's notes, tags, summary, and details sit in a panel beside it.

### Links between notes

A note can link to other cards. To add one, edit the note and type `[[`: a list of your cards appears under the field and narrows as you keep typing. Tap a card to put in the link. Cards in Trash aren't offered; press Escape on a keyboard, or delete the `[[`, to close the list. Links show as tappable pills with the linked card's current title; tap one to open that card. A link to a card that's in Trash, deleted or not yours shows **Missing card**. Notes that link to the card you're viewing are listed under **Linked from**.

### Mark links done

Links you mean to read, watch or buy have a toggle in the toolbar: **Mark as read**, **Mark as watched**, **Mark as bought** and so on, or **Mark as done** for other links. Done links show a check on their tile. Search `is:todo` for what's left.

### More like this and Refresh Details

In a card's **More** menu (or when you press and hold a card), **More Like This** shows cards similar to it, and **Refresh Details** asks Teak to read the card again and update its summary and tags.

### Edit a card

Tap the pencil in a card's toolbar to edit it in one place: rewrite a note or quote, change its notes and tags, or remove tags Teak added. Tap **Save** when you're done. Adding the tag `secret` or `hidden` hides the card everywhere except a search for that tag; the Edit sheet says so when you add one.

In an open palette, tap the **+** after its colors to add one: pick it, or type or paste a hex code such as `#FF5500`. Press and hold a color and choose **Remove color** to take it out; the message that follows has **Undo**. Tap a color to copy its hex code. A palette holds up to 12 colors and keeps at least one.

Tags Teak added also show under **Suggested by Teak** in an open card's tags: **Keep** makes one your own tag, and **Remove** takes it off the card. To rename a link, tap its title at the top and choose **Rename**. **Edit link…** in the card's **More** menu changes its address, and Teak then refreshes its preview, summary and tags.

### Archive

Archive cards you're done with to keep them out of your library without deleting them. Press and hold a card and choose **Archive**, or use **Archive** in a card's **More** menu. A message lets you **Undo**. Open **Archive** from the **More** button (on iPad, from the sidebar) to see archived cards and **Unarchive** them.

### Folders and smart folders

On iPhone, the **Folders** tab lists your folders and smart folders, with each folder's card count; tap one to see its cards, with its own search and **More** menu, or tap **New Folder** to make one. On iPad, folders sit in the sidebar. Press and hold a folder to rename or delete it; a deleted folder can be brought back with **Undo**, and its cards stay in your library. To file a card, open it and tap **Folders** in its details, or select cards and choose **Add to Folder**. A smart folder is a saved search: it shows every card that matches, including cards you save later. Press and hold one and choose **Edit search…** to change its name or search.

### Tags

Open **Settings › Tags** on iPhone, or **Tags** in the iPad sidebar, to see every tag with its card count. Press and hold a tag to **Rename…**, **Merge Into…** another tag, or **Delete…** it from every card. Teak shows the progress while it works. Tap a tag to see its cards.

### Revisit

Choose **Revisit** from the **More** button to go through up to 20 shuffled cards from the current view, one at a time. Swipe left or tap **Archive** for cards you're done with, swipe right or tap **Keep** for the rest. **Undo** brings back the last archived card. When the deck ends, Teak shows how many you archived and kept.

### Trash and selecting cards

Deleting a card moves it to **Trash**, where it stays for 30 days, and shows **Undo**. Open Trash from the **More** button, then tap a card to **Restore** it or **Delete Forever**. You can also press and hold a card for the same actions. On iOS 27, swipe a card to favorite, archive or delete it. **Empty Trash**, in Trash's toolbar and its **More** button, deletes every card in Trash after you confirm. That can't be undone.

To act on several cards, choose **Select** from the **More** button (or press and hold a card and choose **Select**), tap the cards you want, then archive them, add tags, add them to a folder, favorite them, or move them to Trash. In Archive, unarchive them; in Trash, restore them or delete them forever. Tap **Done** to stop selecting.

## Share Sheet (Import from Other Apps)

Use the iOS Share Sheet to save content to Teak without leaving another app:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share from any app">
    In any app (Safari, Photos, Files, etc.), tap the **Share** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Teak">
    Select **Teak** from the share destinations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save automatically">
    The Teak share extension opens and saves the item automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The share extension shows live status while saving. When a single item is saved, you can add tags (separated by commas) and a note before tapping **Save**. Tap **Add to folders** to file what you saved into your folders; with several items, the folders you pick apply to all of them. If you don't touch anything, it closes by itself. If it asks you to sign in, open Teak, sign in, then retry.

Supported share sources include links, text, images, and files, with the same format support as the web app (see [Features](/docs/features)), up to 100 MB — except Markdown shares, which become text cards limited to 512 KiB.

## Safari Extension

Save the page you're reading from Safari:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the extension">
    In Safari, tap the extensions button in the address bar, choose **Manage Extensions**, and turn on **Teak**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a page">
    Open the extensions menu and tap **Teak**. The page saves right away. A page that's already in your library moves to the top instead of being saved twice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the extension asks you to sign in, tap **Sign In** to open Teak, sign in, then try again.

## Shortcuts and Siri

Teak's actions are in the Shortcuts app under **Teak**, and Siri runs each by name, such as "Search Teak" or "New note in Teak":

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Save to Teak** | Saves text or a link without opening Teak |
| **Search Teak** | Finds up to 20 cards with the same search as the app, so filters like `type:image` work; turn on **Include archived cards** to search Archive too |
| **Open Card** | Opens a card in Teak |
| **Add to Folder** | Adds cards, such as Search Teak's results, to one of your folders |
| **New Note** | Opens Teak on a new note |
| **Record Voice Memo** | Opens Teak on the voice memo recorder |

Cards in Trash and hidden cards never show up in Shortcuts. If an action asks you to sign in, open Teak, sign in, then run it again.

## Spotlight

Search for a card's title in Spotlight to find it, with its thumbnail; tap it to open the card in Teak. Teak adds cards as you browse your library and when you open the app, so a card saved on the web shows up after you next open Teak. Cards in Trash, archived cards and hidden cards aren't in Spotlight, and signing out removes everything Teak added.

## Control Center and the Action button

Add **New Note** or **Voice Memo** to Control Center or the Lock Screen: open Control Center, tap **+**, then **Add a Control**, and search for Teak. Each opens Teak on that screen. On an iPhone with an Action button, choose **Controls** or **Shortcut** in **Settings → Action Button** and pick either one.

## Settings

The **Settings** tab lets you:

- Open **Tags** to rename, merge, or delete tags (iPhone)
- Switch appearance between **Auto** (follows iOS system setting), **Light**, and **Dark** via **Settings → Appearance**
- Choose Teak's language in **Settings → Language**; it's saved to your account, so the web and your other devices follow it. See [Language](/docs/features#language)
- View your account email, card usage, and plan
- Sign out
- Delete your account

## Android

The Android app works like the iPhone app, with these differences:

- **Share sheet:** if you also installed Teak's web app from Chrome, the share sheet lists Teak twice. The Android app saves without opening anything, so keep that one. See [Install the web app](/docs/features#install-the-web-app).

- **Tablets:** on a wide screen, such as a tablet in landscape, the navigation rail lists **Home**, **Favorites**, **Archive**, **Trash**, **Folders**, **Add** and **Settings**. Favorites, Archive and Trash open as their own pages, so the library leaves out their filter chips. Phones and narrower windows keep the bottom bar, with the **Favorites**, **Archive** and **Trash** chips above your cards.
- **Wallpaper colors:** turn on **Settings → Appearance → Wallpaper colors** to color the app from your wallpaper (Material You), in light and dark. It's off until you turn it on, so Teak's red stays the default.
- **PDFs:** **View document** opens a PDF in the app, page by page. Tap the open-in icon at the top to read it in another app instead.
- **Palette colors:** **+ Add color** opens a sheet with a hex code field and a row of colors to pick from, instead of the system color picker.
- **Photos and voice memos:** tap a photo to see it full screen and pinch or double-tap to zoom. Tap or drag along a voice memo's waveform to jump to that point.
- **Offline:** when you lose your connection, Home says so and keeps the cards it already loaded. Favorite, archive, mark done, edit, move to Trash or restore a card, or save a new note or link, and the change shows right away and syncs when you're back online, even if you close the app. Images you've already seen stay available. To keep a card and its file for later, open the card and choose **More → Keep offline**; it then opens, plays and shows its PDF without a connection. **Remove offline copy** in the same menu frees the space. Opening the app with no connection doesn't load your library yet, and search, More like this, Refresh details and adding tags or folders still need a connection.

## Troubleshooting

  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Content saved through the share sheet is missing">
    - Open Teak and pull down to refresh.
    - Check that you were signed in when you shared the item.
