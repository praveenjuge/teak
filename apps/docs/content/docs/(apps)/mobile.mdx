Save on the go, record voice notes, upload files, and browse your whole library on iPhone and iPad, in sync with your other devices.

## Requirements

- iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or newer
- A Teak account

## Install and sign in

Download Teak from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teak-save-inspirations/id6756574989). On first launch, choose **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with Google**, or use **Login with Email** or **Register with Email** to finish on the Teak sign-in page.

## Saving content

Open the **Add** tab:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Note or Link** | Type or paste any text or link |
| **Voice Memo** | Record a voice note; Teak transcribes it |
| **Photos & Videos** | Pick up to 20 photos or videos at once |
| **Camera** | Take a photo or video |
| **Files** | Pick up to 20 documents or other files at once |

You can also save from other apps with the [share sheet](#share-sheet), from Safari with the [Safari extension](/docs/extension#safari), and with [Shortcuts and Siri](#shortcuts-and-siri). On the Free plan, Teak tells you before saving past the card limit.

## Your library

**Home** shows your cards, newest first, with more columns on iPad. Search works as on the web, filters included (see [Smart search](/docs/features#smart-search)); each filter you type, such as `#design`, `is:todo` or `price:<100`, becomes a chip.

- **More** (the circled ellipsis): **Select** cards, start **Revisit**, show **Favorites**, **Archive** or **Trash**, narrow by **Type** or **Color**, sort by **Newest First**, **Oldest First** or **Shuffle**, **Save as Smart Folder…**, or **Clear Filters**.
- **Press and hold a card** to favorite, archive, select or delete it, or for **More Like This** and **Refresh Details**. On iOS 27 and later, swipe a card to favorite, archive or delete it.
- **Folders:** on iPhone, the **Folders** tab lists folders and smart folders, with **New Folder**; on iPad they're in the sidebar. Press and hold a folder to rename or delete it, or a smart folder to **Edit search…**.
- **Tags:** **Settings › Tags** on iPhone, or **Tags** in the iPad sidebar. Press and hold a tag to **Rename…**, **Merge Into…** or **Delete…** it.
- **Revisit:** swipe left or tap **Archive**, swipe right or tap **Keep**; **Undo** brings back the last archived card.
- **Trash:** tap a card to **Restore** it or **Delete Forever**; **Empty Trash** is in the toolbar and the **More** button.

Tap a card to open it. On iPad, its notes, tags, summary and details sit in a panel beside it.

- Tap a photo to see it full screen; pinch or double-tap to zoom, and swipe down to close.
- **View Document** opens a PDF or other file. Markdown and code show inline.
- Tap or drag along a voice memo's waveform to jump to that point.
- Tap a tag to search for it.
- The toolbar has the link's done toggle (**Mark as read**, **Mark as watched** and so on). Tap a link's title to **Rename** it, and use **Edit link…** in **More** to change its address.
- The pencil edits the card: rewrite a note or quote, and change its notes and tags. Tap **Save** when you're done.
- In a palette, tap **+** after its colors to add one, or press and hold a color for **Remove color**.

How these features work is the same everywhere: see [Features](/docs/features).

### Links between notes

While editing a note, type `[[` and tap a card from the list to link it. Links show as pills; tap one to open that card. See [Link cards](/docs/features#link-cards).

## Share sheet

Save from any app without opening Teak:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share from any app">
    In any app (Safari, Photos, Files and so on), tap **Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Teak">
    Pick **Teak** from the share destinations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    Teak saves the item and shows the status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a single item, you can add tags (separated by commas) and a note before tapping **Save**. **Add to folders** files what you saved, all items at once when you share several. Untouched, the sheet closes by itself. If it asks you to sign in, open Teak, sign in, then share again.

You can share links, text, images and files up to 100 MB, in any [format the web app takes](/docs/features#card-types). Markdown becomes a text card, up to 512 KiB.

## Shortcuts and Siri

Teak's actions are in the Shortcuts app under **Teak**, and Siri runs each by name, such as "Search Teak" or "New note in Teak":

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Save to Teak** | Saves text or a link without opening Teak |
| **Search Teak** | Finds up to 20 cards with the same search as the app, filters included; turn on **Include archived cards** to search Archive too |
| **Open Card** | Opens a card in Teak |
| **Add to Folder** | Adds cards, such as Search Teak's results, to one of your folders |
| **New Note** | Opens Teak on a new note |
| **Record Voice Memo** | Opens Teak on the voice memo recorder |

Cards in Trash and hidden cards never show up in Shortcuts. If an action asks you to sign in, open Teak, sign in, then run it again.

## Spotlight

Search for a card's title in Spotlight to find it, and tap it to open the card in Teak. Teak adds cards as you browse your library, so a card saved elsewhere shows up after you next open Teak. Archived, hidden and trashed cards aren't included, and signing out removes them.

## Control Center and the Action button

Add **New Note** or **Voice Memo** to Control Center or the Lock Screen: open Control Center, tap **+**, then **Add a Control**, and search for Teak. On an iPhone with an Action button, choose **Controls** or **Shortcut** in **Settings → Action Button** and pick either one.

## Settings

The **Settings** tab has **Tags** (iPhone), **Appearance** (Auto, Light or Dark), [**Language**](/docs/features#language), your email, card usage and plan, **Sign out** and **Delete account**.

  <AccordionItem title="Something shared is missing">
    - Open Teak and pull down to refresh.
    - Check that you were signed in when you shared it.
    - If the status showed a partial failure, only some items saved.
