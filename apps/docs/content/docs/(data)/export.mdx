---
title: Export Your Data
description: Download a portable archive of your Teak cards and original files
---

Teak can prepare a portable ZIP archive of your active cards and the original files attached to them. Exports are available from Settings on the web app and desktop app.

:::note[Exports are read-only]
Starting an export does not change, delete, or move anything in your Teak library.
:::

## Start an Export

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Export">
    Open Teak on web, or the desktop app via **Teak → Settings…**, then find **Import/Export Data** and choose **Manage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start export">
    Switch to the **Export** tab and choose **Start export**, then keep working while Teak prepares the archive in the background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    When the export is ready, choose **Download archive**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can cancel an export while it is still preparing. Canceled and failed exports do not count toward your weekly export limit.

## What the Archive Includes

Each export is a ZIP with your active cards (`cards.json`), their original files (`files/`), and a manifest. Importing the archive adds the exported cards and included files that satisfy import limits; cards with missing or oversized (>20 MiB) files fail to import.

## What Is Not Included

Exports focus on portable library content: trash, AI enrichment, generated previews, and account data are not included. If an original file cannot be read, its card is still included in the archive, but the card cannot be restored on re-import.

## Limits and Availability

| Limit | Details |
|---|---|
| Frequency | One successful export every 7 days. |
| Active jobs | One export can prepare at a time. |
| Download window | Ready archives stay available for 7 days. |
| Library size | Exports support up to 10,000 active cards or an estimated 5 GB archive. |

If your latest export is still available, download it from **Settings -> Export Data -> Manage**. After it expires, you can start a new export when your weekly quota allows it.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title='I see "Available in..."'>
    You recently completed an export. Wait for the shown time, then start another one.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="My export failed">
    Open **Export Data** and try again. Failed exports do not count toward the weekly limit.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="Some files are unavailable">
    The archive still includes those cards in `cards.json`, but their original files were unavailable when the export was built.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="The archive is too large">
    If your library is over the export limit, delete unneeded cards or permanently remove old trash items, then try again.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
