- **Export** your library from Settings on the web as a ZIP of your cards and original files. Follow its progress, cancel it, or download it when it's ready. See [Export](/docs/export).
- **Import** browser bookmarks, Raindrop.io and Teak archives from Settings. Links you already have are skipped, and Raindrop links keep their notes, tags, folder, favorite and saved date. See [Import](/docs/import).
- API keys have their own window in Settings to copy, disable, revoke or regenerate them.
