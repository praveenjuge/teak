  category: Product updates
---

- Delete your account from Settings, and change your avatar more reliably.
- Signing in, signing up and resetting your password give clearer feedback, and work the same on web and mobile.
- Theme selection moved into Settings.
- PDF and video cards show better previews in the grid, and dark mode dialogs are easier on the eyes.
- AI summaries and transcripts arrive faster, and link previews show richer images.
- When something goes wrong, the app helps you recover instead of leaving you on a broken screen.
- Accessibility improvements across the app.
