- Sign in with Apple on mobile.
- Free accounts can hold up to 200 cards.
- Images you save get a color palette automatically.
- Uploads explain what went wrong when they fail.
- Link and file cards look better on mobile, even when a page shares little.
- Manage tags, and copy images to reuse them elsewhere.
- Large libraries scroll smoothly as more cards load.
- Thumbnails work for SVGs, rotated photos and videos.
