- Teak has a public API and an MCP server, so your own tools and AI clients can save, search and manage cards.
- Raycast adds **Save Clipboard** and **Save Current Browser Tab**, a refreshed card view, and AI that can save and search your cards.
- The browser extension adds a save button to posts on Instagram, Pinterest, X, Hacker News and several design news sites.
- Saved Instagram and X posts keep their text, photos and videos.
- Very tall images fill the preview's width and scroll.
- A broken card link shows a clear error, and links into cards on mobile open more reliably.
