title: "August 2025"
description: Product updates and improvements released in Teak during August 2025.
type: changelog
date: "2025-08-31"
changelog:
  category: Product updates
---

- Teak launched: save cards, search them, and browse your ideas in a masonry grid.
- Pro plans arrived for people who need more room than the free plan.
- Search and filters stay fast in large libraries, and a command palette gets you to common actions.
- The browser extension can save the page you're viewing automatically, and right-click saves links, text and images without opening Teak.
- Link cards show better titles, images and source details.
- Drag files into Teak, see their upload progress, and download the original file from its card.
- Filter your library by card type.
- Quote and palette cards arrived, with rich previews.
- Select several cards and clean them up in one go.
- On mobile, Home and Settings got their own screens, with a page to manage your subscription.
