title: "September 2025"
description: Product updates and improvements released in Teak during September 2025.
type: changelog
date: "2025-09-30"
changelog:
  category: Product updates
---

- Recording audio on mobile is easier, with microphone permission handled for you.
- Image-heavy libraries load faster, with right-sized thumbnails instead of full originals.
- Manage your subscription from a billing page, and when you reach your card limit Teak explains what to do next.
- The card grid scrolls more smoothly.
- Editing cards on mobile is easier, with a separate view for each kind of change.
- Link cards get better previews and are sorted by kind automatically after you save them.
- The "analyzing" indicator now goes away as soon as a card is ready.
