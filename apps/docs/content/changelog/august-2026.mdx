  category: Product updates

- Image and video cards load noticeably faster, and new images get their previews and colors sooner.
- Uploaded PDFs show their first page in the grid again.
- The iPhone and iPad apps no longer close on launch, and load faster.
- Signing in from the CLI, Raycast or a browser extension asks you to approve the device first.
- Broken image files are refused with a clear message instead of becoming a card without a preview.
- Large uploads pick up where they stopped after a dropped connection.
- Audio recorded in Chrome and Safari saves as an audio card with a waveform player.
- Exports of large libraries finish faster and more reliably.
- You can have up to 10 API keys, and revoke them all at once in Settings.
