## Sign in again after the sign-in update

Teak moved to a new sign-in system on October 7. Your library, cards and plan are unchanged.

- **Everyone:** sign in again on the web, iPhone, iPad, Mac, the browser extension, Raycast and the CLI.
- **If you signed in with a password:** reset it from the sign-in page first.
- **If you use Google or Apple:** continue with the same option.
- **Raycast:** update the extension from the Raycast Store, then sign in.
- **MCP clients that signed in with the browser:** remove Teak from the client, add it again, and sign in. API keys keep working. See [MCP](/docs/mcp).

## A new Teak for iPhone, iPad and Mac

- Teak on iPhone, iPad and Mac is now one native app from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teak-save-inspirations/id6756574989) and [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teak-save-inspirations/id6756574989?platform=mac&mt=12). It needs iOS, iPadOS or macOS 26. See [iPhone and iPad](/docs/mobile) and [Mac](/docs/mac).
- Save from any app with the share sheet, from Safari with the Teak Safari extension, or on the Mac with **Quick Capture**, the Services menu and the menu bar icon.
- Search by meaning, archive, tag, add to folders and smart folders, mark links done, use **Revisit** and **More like this**, and act on several cards at once.
- Shortcuts and Siri can save, search, open cards and add them to folders; Spotlight finds your cards; and Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action button can start a note or voice memo.
- Photos open full screen with zoom, notes show their tables and checklists, and `[[` links one card from another.

## Android

- PDFs open in the app, tablets have a side rail, and **Wallpaper colors** matches your phone.
- Edits and new notes made offline show at once and sync when you're back, and **Keep offline** saves a card for the road.

## Read and highlight saved articles

- A saved article opens in a clean **Read** view on the web, with its text and images in one calm layout. Press `R` to switch between **Read** and **Preview**. See [Read](/docs/features#read).
- Select a passage and choose **Highlight**, or press `H`, then add a note to it. The card lists its highlights, and clicking one jumps to it. See [Highlights](/docs/features#highlights).
- Highlights from Instapaper, Omnivore and Raindrop.io imports arrive as highlights.

## A saved copy of every page

- Teak keeps a private copy of every link you save, on every plan, and shows it when the original page is gone. See [Saved copies of pages](/docs/features#saved-copies-of-pages).
- Saving from the Chrome or Firefox extension keeps the page as you saw it, including pages you're signed in to, without passwords or payment details.

## Search that understands you

- Search finds cards by meaning as well as words: `cozy reading nook` finds a photo of an armchair by a window, typos still match, and a search in another language finds cards saved in English. See [Smart search](/docs/features#smart-search).
- Search finds words inside PDFs, Office and code files, and what's said in audio, video, Vimeo videos and podcast episodes.
- Teak reads the words in images, screenshots and scanned PDFs, so search finds a receipt by its invoice number. `text:` searches only those words.
- Leave things out with `-`, combine with `OR`, and group with parentheses: `(site:github.com OR site:gitlab.com) -is:archived`.
- Narrow a search with `tag:`, `type:`, `is:fav`, `site:`, `folder:`, `price:`, `mood:` and quoted phrases. The search box suggests filters, tags, folders and sites as you type.
- Searches start in **Best match**, and **More like this** on any card finds the cards closest to it in meaning.

## Organize your library

- A new sidebar holds **New**, **Everything**, **Favorites**, **Tags**, your folders, **Archive**, **Trash** and **Settings**. Every page has the same header, with search in the middle and a **View** menu for card types, order and saving a smart folder. See [Sidebar](/docs/features#sidebar).
- **Folders:** group cards in colored folders. A card can be in several, and removing it from one never deletes it. See [Folders](/docs/features#folders).
- **Smart folders:** save a search, and it fills itself with every card that matches, including ones you save later. See [Smart folders](/docs/features#smart-folders).
- **Tags:** see every tag with its count, and rename, merge or delete one across your whole library. See [Tags and filters](/docs/features#tags-and-filters).
- **Archive:** keep a card out of your library without deleting it.
- **Select many cards** to archive, favorite, tag, add to a folder or move to Trash at once, with **Undo**.
- **Empty Trash** deletes everything in Trash for good, after asking.
- **Hidden cards:** tag a card `secret` or `hidden` to keep it out of sight until you search for that tag. See [Hidden cards](/docs/features#hidden-cards).

## Rediscover what you saved

- **Shuffle** shows your cards in a random order, and **Revisit** walks you through 20 random cards so you can keep or archive each. See [Sort and share a search](/docs/features#sort-and-share-a-search).
- **Oldest first** starts from the beginning. Imported bookmarks keep their original dates.
- **Mark a link done** as read, watched, listened, bought, cooked or played, then search `is:todo` for what's left. See [Read, watch and buy](/docs/features#read-watch-and-buy).

## Saving

- **Save again, move to top:** saving a link you already have, from anywhere, moves that card to the top instead of making a second one, even when the address has tracking parameters. See [Saving something again](/docs/features#saving-something-again).
- **Paste a list of links** to save each as its own card.
- **Save by email:** forward anything to `save@in.teakvault.com`. See [Save by email](/docs/features#save-by-email).
- **Install Teak** from Chrome, Edge or Safari to use it in its own window, and share to it on Android and ChromeOS. See [Install the web app](/docs/features#install-the-web-app).
- New accounts start with a **Start here** folder of example cards, one of each type, with tips to try.

## Richer cards

- Summaries and AI tags are written in the language of what you save. See [AI enrichment](/docs/features#ai-enrichment).
- Wikipedia, arXiv, YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music and book links show richer details, and **Save PDF** saves an arXiv paper's PDF as its own card. See [Card details](/docs/features#card-details).
- Teak recognizes places, typefaces, apps, games and code repositories, and shows their facts, like a restaurant's address or a repository's stars.
- Product links show their price, and `price:<100` finds them by it.
- Posts from Bluesky, Mastodon, Reddit, TikTok, Threads, LinkedIn and Substack Notes keep their text, author and photos.
- Photos show their camera details, never their location, and links show who wrote them and when.
- Videos and GIFs get colors, video cards preview as you move across them, and audio shows its real waveform.
- **Refresh details** gets a fresh summary, tags and preview, and you can choose your own image for any link.

## Notes

- Write notes with headings, lists, tasks, quotes, code and links; type `/` for formatting. Notes stay in Markdown.
- Type `[[` to link another card, and see which notes link to a card under **Linked from**. See [Link cards](/docs/features#link-cards).
- Type `/table` to add a table, and ticked checklist items move to the bottom.
- **Focus** (`⌘E`) shows only your note, for any note.

## A calmer card grid and card view

- Cards are easier to scan, with site icons, note headings and audio length. Rest the pointer on a card for its main actions.
- An open card keeps its notes, tags, folders, summary and details beside it, all editable in place. Step to the next card with the arrows or `←` and `→`.
- Deleting a card offers **Undo**.

## Keyboard and command palette

- Use your library without a mouse: move between cards with the arrow keys, open with `Enter`, and press `?` to see every shortcut. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/features#keyboard-shortcuts).
- `⌘K` opens the command palette to go anywhere, find a card, or run an action.

## A redesigned Settings

- Settings has a page for each area: **Account**, **Plan**, **Appearance**, **Integrations**, **Security** and **Import & export**.
- Choose monthly or yearly Pro right on the **Plan** page; checkout opens in a new tab.

## Teak in seven more languages

- The web app, browser extension, and iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android apps are now in Español, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français, 日本語, 한국어 and 简体中文. Your choice follows you to every device. See [Language](/docs/features#language).

## Imports and exports

- Import from Pocket, Instapaper, Readwise Reader, Omnivore, Linkwarden, mymind, any CSV, or a text file of links, as well as browser bookmarks, Raindrop.io and Teak archives. See [Import](/docs/import).
- **Changed:** folders in bookmark files and Raindrop exports now become Teak folders instead of tags.
- Exports include a bookmarks file your browser can import and a spreadsheet of every card. See [Export](/docs/export).

## Browser extension

- **Firefox:** Teak is on [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/teak/), with everything the Chrome extension does. See [Browser extension](/docs/extension).
- Press **⌥⇧S** (**Alt+Shift+S**) to save the page, and the Teak icon shows a check when the page is already in your library.
- Type `teak` in the address bar to search your library.
- Add tags and a note right after saving, and saved text remembers the page it came from.

## Teak in Claude and Raycast

- Claude shows your cards as Teak cards right in the conversation when it searches or saves. See [MCP](/docs/mcp#card-views).
- In Claude Code, install the Teak plugin for `/teak:save`, `/teak:search` and `/teak:recent`. See [Connect your client](/docs/mcp#connect-your-client).
- Raycast can browse folders, find similar cards, show random cards, archive, mark done and manage tags. See [Raycast](/docs/raycast).

## Fixes

- Deleting your account also deletes its usage and crash data. See [Delete your account](/docs/delete-account).
- Editing a card again right after saving it no longer fails.
- Searching for a color or style finds your images and palettes.
- A dropped connection no longer signs you out.
- A link pasted on its own saves as a link card, and a pasted screenshot as an image.
- Downloads keep the file's original name, and imported bookmarks keep their names.
- Leaving a card right after typing keeps what you typed.
- Recordings with no speech no longer get made-up transcripts or tags, and PDF summaries are written from the document's text.

## For developers

- Everything above that works with cards, tags, folders, highlights and search is also in the [API](/docs/api), [MCP](/docs/mcp), [SDK](/docs/sdk) and [CLI](/docs/cli).
- The SDK and CLI report each API error by its own code, and retry safely when Teak is briefly busy.
- **Breaking:** deleting a card forever through the API needs it to be in Trash first.

## For self-hosters

- **Breaking:** the old setting for closing sign-ups no longer works. After you update, pause sign-ups again as described in [Production basics](/docs/self-hosting#production-basics).
