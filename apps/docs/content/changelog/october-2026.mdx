## A redesigned Settings

- Settings on the web now has a page for each area: **Account**, **Plan**, **Appearance**, **Integrations**, **Security** and **Import & export**, listed beside the page on wide screens. Each page has its own address you can bookmark, and the command palette can open any of them (type "Settings").
- **Account** shows who you're signed in as and a summary of every page, such as your plan and card count, whether Save by email is on, how many devices are signed in and your last export.
- **Plan** shows how many of the free plan's 200 cards you've used, and you choose monthly or yearly Pro right on the page. On Pro, **Manage subscription** opens your billing portal.
- **Appearance** shows a preview of each theme next to the language.
- **Integrations** brings together Save by email, the browser extensions and apps, and API keys. **Security** lists signed-in devices and connected apps, and is where you delete your account.
- **Import & export**: pick a source and choose a file, or drop it on the import area.
- Press `G` then `S` from anywhere to open Settings.

## Highlight what you read

- In a link's **Read** view on the web, select a passage and choose **Highlight**, or press `H`. Click a highlight to add a note or remove it. See [Highlights](/docs/features#highlights).
- The card's details list its highlights after Notes; click one to jump to it in the article.
- Highlights from Instapaper, Omnivore and Raindrop.io imports now arrive as highlights instead of note text, and Teak archives keep them.
- A highlighted link keeps its saved copy: **Refresh details** and saving it again no longer replace the page your highlights were made in.
- **API, MCP, SDK and CLI:** list, add by quote, note and remove highlights (`teak highlights`), and cards include `highlightCount`. See [Highlights](/docs/api#highlights).

## Read saved articles in Teak

- A saved article now has a **Read** view in its card on the web: the article's text and images in one calm layout, in light and dark, with the site, author and reading time above it and **Open original ↗** below. Articles, news and research open on **Read**; press `R` to switch to **Preview** and back. See [Read](/docs/features#read).
- Search finds links by words in their article, and a long article's AI summary is written from the article itself.
- **API, MCP, SDK and CLI:** cards include `article` (`wordCount`, `readingMinutes`), and `GET /v1/cards/:cardId/content?kind=article` (or `teak content <card-id> --kind article`) returns the article as Markdown. See [A link's article](/docs/api#a-links-article).

## Teak keeps a copy of every page you save, and opens it when the original is gone

- Every link you save now gets a private copy of its page, on every plan: its text, styles and images in one file, up to 20 MB, without video, audio or scripts. A page too large to keep whole is kept as text only. Links you saved before get one too. See [Saved copies of pages](/docs/features#saved-copies-of-pages).
- Saving from the Chrome or Firefox extension keeps the page as you saw it, including pages you're signed in to. Passwords, payment card numbers and one-time codes are left out.
- A link's **Details** show **Archived copy** with its size, or why a copy couldn't be saved.
- **API, SDK and CLI:** cards include `archive` (`kind`, `bytes`, `capturedAt`). See [Saved copies of pages](/docs/api#saved-copies-of-pages).

## Import links from any CSV

- **Settings → Import** has two new sources. **CSV file** reads any comma-separated CSV with a header row: Teak finds the link, title, tags, folder, note and date columns by name, including common names such as `link`, `labels`, `collection`, `description` and `date added`. See [CSV columns](/docs/import#csv-columns).
- **Text file of links** imports a `.txt` file with one link per line, optionally followed by ` | Title`. OneTab's **Export URLs** text works as it is, so OneTab is no longer listed on its own.
- Rows without a usable link are listed in the import report with their row number, and a CSV without a link column stops with a message saying which column to add. Links you already have, and repeats in the file, are skipped.

## Saving a link again moves it to the top

- Save a link you already have, from anywhere you save, and Teak moves that card to the top of your library instead of making a second one. An archived card comes back from Archive. See [Saving something again](/docs/features#saving-something-again).
- Lists, folders, tags and search sorted by date now follow when you last saved a card, and date words such as `last week` find a card you saved again then. The card's **Details** keep **Added**, the day you first saved it, and show **Saved again** with the last time.
- The browser extension and Raycast say **Already in Teak** or **Already saved**, and the card moves. Tags and a note sent with a link you already have aren't added to it.
- **API, MCP, SDK and CLI:** saving a link you already have answers with that card and `resaved: true`, and every card has `savedAt`. See [Create behavior](/docs/api#create-behavior).

## Paste a list of links to save them all

- On the web, paste a list of links, one per line, and Teak saves each as its own card, up to 100 at a time. Links already in Teak move to the top. See [Saving links](/docs/features#saving-links).

## AI summaries and tags in your content's language

- Teak now writes a card's summary and AI tags in the language of what you saved, on every app: a Japanese article gets a Japanese summary and tags like `デザイン`, a Spanish note gets Spanish ones. Items with no text, like a photo without words, still get English tags. See [AI enrichment](/docs/features#ai-enrichment).
- Your own tags are never changed, and search by meaning finds cards across languages. Tags in different languages are different tags (`design` and `diseño`); merge them on the **Tags** page if you like.
- Cards you saved before keep their English summary and tags; use **Refresh details** on a card to redo them in its language.

## Teak in seven more languages

- The web app, the Chrome and Firefox extension, and the iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android apps are now available in Español, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français, 日本語, 한국어 and 简体中文, as well as English. Choose one in **Settings → Language**; it switches right away and is saved to your account. Teak stays in English until you choose. See [Language](/docs/features#language).
- Dates, numbers and counts follow the language too. Search words such as `is:fav` and `type:image` stay in English in every language, and so do some details Teak adds to cards, like link facts and bylines.
- **iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android:** the apps speak the same eight languages and follow the language saved to your account, so changing it on the web changes it in the apps too. Choose one in the app's **Settings → Language**, or in your device's settings for Teak (iOS, macOS, and Android 13 and newer); either way it's saved to your account. Signed out, the apps use your device's language.
- One word for each action everywhere: **Move to Trash**, **Delete forever**, **More like this**, **Favorite** and **Unfavorite**, and **Sign out** on every app.
- **API, SDK, and CLI:** `/v1/me` and `me()` include `locale`, and `teak whoami` shows your language.

## Install Teak from your browser

- Choose **Install Teak** in Chrome or Edge to open Teak in its own window, or add it to your Dock or Home Screen from Safari. See [Install the web app](/docs/features#install-the-web-app).
- Installed in Chrome on Android or ChromeOS, Teak shows up in the share sheet: shared pages and text open the composer filled in, and shared photos and files upload. Sharing while signed out picks up again after you sign in.

## Teak in Shortcuts, Spotlight and Control Center

- **iPhone, iPad and Mac:** the Shortcuts app has new Teak actions: **Search Teak**, **Open Card**, **Add to Folder**, **New Note** and **Record Voice Memo**, beside **Save to Teak**. Siri runs them by name, and Search Teak's results can go straight into a folder. See [Shortcuts and Siri](/docs/mobile#shortcuts-and-siri).
- Spotlight finds your cards by title and opens them in Teak. Trashed, archived and hidden cards stay out, and signing out removes them all.
- **New Note** and **Voice Memo** controls for Control Center, the Lock Screen and the iPhone Action button open Teak on that screen.
- The share sheet has **Add to folders**, so what you save lands in the right folders right away.

## Empty Trash, and more ways to fix up a card

- **Empty Trash** on the web, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android deletes everything in Trash for good, after a confirmation that says how many cards it removes. See [Trash](/docs/features#trash).
- **Edit link…** in a link card's menu changes its address, and the preview refreshes. On iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android you can rename a link's title from the card view too.
- In the card view, **Suggested by Teak** lists the tags Teak picked: keep one to add it to your tags, or remove it. Now on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android as well as the web.
- On iPhone, iPad and Mac, Move to Trash no longer asks first and offers **Undo** instead, and a smart folder has **Edit search…**.
- Photos open full screen with pinch and double-tap zoom on iPhone and iPad, and in a zoomable window on Mac. Voice memos have a seek bar on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.
- **Android:** PDFs open in the app, Android tablets list Favorites, Archive and Trash in the side rail, and Settings → Appearance has **Wallpaper colors**. See the [mobile guide](/docs/mobile).

## Notes and search on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android

- Notes show their tables as tables, scrolling sideways when they're wide, and their checklists with checkboxes you can tick. A ticked item moves below the open ones, as on the web. Edit a table's cells on the web.
- Type `[[` while editing a note to link another card: a list of your cards appears under the field and narrows as you type. See [Links between notes](/docs/mobile#links-between-notes).
- Search takes `-`, `OR`, parentheses and `site:` as typed, and typing `-` or a word suggests what to leave out or combine, as on the web.

## Exclude words and filters with -, and combine them with OR

- Put `-` before a word or a filter to leave those cards out: `design -figma`, `#inspo -type:image`, `-is:archived`. Uppercase `OR` finds cards that match either side, such as `tag:fonts OR tag:type`, and parentheses group them: `(site:github.com OR site:gitlab.com) -is:archived`. See [Smart search](/docs/features#smart-search).
- Typing `-` in the search box suggests what to leave out, and `OR` is suggested after a word. An excluded filter shows as a pill with a minus before its name, and the **?** shortcuts sheet has a new **Search** section.
- Smart folders take operators too, and a search typed inside one narrows it without mixing the two.
- **API, MCP, SDK, and CLI:** `q` takes the same operators. In the CLI, quote the search, or put a search that starts with `-` after `--`. See [Search qualifiers](/docs/api#search-qualifiers).

## Your library's new tools on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Raycast

- **Organize:** archive cards and open **Archive**, put cards in folders or save a search as a smart folder, and rename, merge or delete a tag everywhere from **Tags**.
- **Find:** sort Newest First, Oldest First or Shuffle, and open **More like this** (Find Similar in Raycast) on any card. `is:done`, `is:todo`, `is:archived`, `text:`, `price:` and `mood:` work in search, and searching `#secret` or `#hidden` shows hidden cards.
- **Revisit:** go through 20 random cards one at a time and keep or archive each, with Undo. On iPhone, iPad and Mac it's in the **More** menu; in Raycast it's **Random Cards**.
- **Card details:** mark a link as read, watched or bought, see `[[` links as their titles and the notes that link to a card under **Linked from**, and see prices, publish dates and the page a saved text came from. **Refresh Details** fetches a card's details again.
- **iPhone, iPad and Mac:** search finds cards by meaning as well as words, photos show their camera details, and selecting several cards lets you archive, tag, favorite or add them to a folder together. The library's **More** menu holds Select, Revisit, filters and sorting, iPhone has a **Folders** tab for your folders and smart folders, and on Mac **Settings** sits in the sidebar under **Trash**. After saving from the share sheet, add tags and a note. See the [mobile guide](/docs/mobile) and the [Mac app guide](/docs/mac).
- **Raycast:** new **Browse Folders**, **Random Cards** and **Find Similar Cards** commands, archive, done, folder and refresh actions on every card, tag management in **Browse Tags**, tags and a note in **Quick Save** and **Save to Teak**, and AI tools that understand folders, done states and similar cards. See the [Raycast guide](/docs/raycast).
- **Raycast, tidied up:** a type menu beside the search bar, image thumbnails and palette colors in the list, every tag behind a **+N**, and the date a card was last saved. Card actions are grouped the same way everywhere, empty lists keep **Clear Filters**, **Leave Archive** and **Sign Out** within reach, and a signed-out command opens **Sign in with Teak** right away.

## Link cards together with [[

- In a note on the web, type `[[` to link another card: pick one of your cards from the list, and it shows as a pill with that card's current title, so renaming the card updates every link to it. Click a pill to open the card. A link to a card in Trash or deleted shows **Missing card** until you restore it. See [Link cards](/docs/features#link-cards).
- Open a card to see which notes link to it under **Linked from**, and click one to open it.
- Card tiles show a link as its title, and search finds a note by a linked card's title.
- **API, MCP, SDK, and CLI:** links are written `[[Title|teak:<card-id>]]` in a note's content. A single card's response includes `links` and `backlinks`, and `teak_v1_get_card_links`, `cards.links` and `teak links <card-id>` list both. See [Links between cards](/docs/api#links-between-cards).

## Save to Teak by email

- Turn on **Settings → Save by email**, then send or forward anything to `save@in.teakvault.com` from the email you sign in with. A link becomes a link card, anything else a note, and every card is tagged `email`. See [Save by email](/docs/features#save-by-email).
- Attachments are saved as their own cards, with the same formats as uploads.
- Only mail from your sign-in email that passes its domain's sender checks is saved; anything else is refused.

## Search what's said in Vimeo videos and podcast episodes

- A Vimeo link's captions become the card's transcript, so search finds the video by a line someone says in it, and its summary and tags describe what's said.
- A podcast episode whose page links to its audio, or that Apple Podcasts or the show's feed lists, is transcribed after it's saved, up to 100 MB of audio. The transcript arrives a few minutes later, and the episode's summary and tags are rewritten from it. Spotify episodes and longer episodes get no transcript. See [Smart search](/docs/features#smart-search).
- YouTube links show their channel, length and upload date when the video's page has them. YouTube doesn't let Teak read its captions, so YouTube videos get no transcript.
- The transcript is in `aiTranscript` in the API, and in `teak content <card-id>` and the MCP `teak_v1_get_card_content` tool. Links you saved before get transcripts when you use **Refresh details**.

## Richer details for Wikipedia, arXiv, music and books

- Wikipedia articles show their short description and when they were last edited, and a disambiguation page says so. arXiv papers show their authors, publish date, subject and DOI. Spotify and Apple Music links show the artist and whether it's a track, album or playlist. Book links that name an ISBN get their author, page count, publisher and publish date from Open Library, and a cover when the page has none. See [Card details](/docs/features#card-details).
- Teak's summary of a Wikipedia article or an arXiv paper now reads its opening paragraph or abstract, not just the title.
- **Save PDF** on an arXiv paper's card saves its PDF as its own document card, so its text is searchable in Teak. Saving it again opens the one you have.
- **API, MCP, SDK, and CLI:** `POST /v1/cards/:cardId/save-pdf`, `teak_v1_save_card_pdf`, `cards.savePdf`, and `teak cards save-pdf <card-id>`. See [Save a paper's PDF](/docs/api#save-a-papers-pdf).
- Links you saved before get these details when you use **Refresh details**.

## Mark links as read, watched or bought

- Open a link and click **Mark as read** at the top of its details, or press `D`, to mark it done. It says **Mark as read** for an article, **Mark as watched** for a film, **Mark as bought** for a product, **Mark as played** for a game, and so on, and a check appears on the link's tile. A toast lets you undo it.
- Search `is:todo` for what's left to read, watch, listen to or buy, and `is:done` for what you've finished. Both work in smart folders, such as `is:todo folder:"Reading list"`. See [Read, watch and buy](/docs/features#read-watch-and-buy).
- **API, MCP, SDK, and CLI:** `POST /v1/cards/:cardId/done` and `DELETE` to undo, `teak_v1_set_card_done`, `cards.setDone`, and `teak done <card-id>` (`--undo` to clear it). Cards include `completedAt` and `linkCategory`. See [Mark a link done](/docs/api#mark-a-link-done).

## Find screenshots and scanned PDFs by the words in them

- Teak now reads the words in the images and videos you save: receipts, screenshots, signs, slides and code. Search finds an image by any word in it, and `text:` searches only those words, so `text:4411` finds the receipt and not the note that mentions it. See [Smart search](/docs/features#smart-search).
- Scanned PDFs are searchable too. Teak reads the first 3 pages of a PDF that has no text of its own, so search finds a scanned lease by a phrase on page 2, and its summary and tags describe what the scan says instead of only its file name.
- Links count as well: Teak reads the words in a link's preview image and in its screenshot, such as a talk's slide title.
- **API, MCP, SDK, and CLI:** `q` takes `text:"…"` too. See [Search qualifiers](/docs/api#search-qualifiers).

## Teak recognises a link you've already saved, even with tracking parameters

- The browser extension, Raycast, the API, MCP and the CLI now know a link is already in Teak when the address differs only by `http` or `https`, `www.`, a trailing slash, a `#fragment` (but not a `#/` or `#!` route), or `utm_*`, `fbclid` and `gclid` tracking parameters. See [Check for a saved URL](/docs/api#check-for-a-saved-url).
- Imports skip those variants too, both against your library and within the file. See [Duplicate handling](/docs/import#duplicate-handling).

## Save a page with ⌥⇧S, and see when it's already in Teak

- In the Chrome and Firefox extensions, press **Alt+Shift+S** (**⌥⇧S** on Mac) to save the current page without opening the popup. The Teak icon shows **…** while it saves, then **✓**, or **!** with the reason when it can't. Change the shortcut in your browser's extension shortcut settings. See [Save with a shortcut](/docs/extension#save-with-a-shortcut).
- The Teak icon also shows **✓** whenever the page in your current tab is already in your library. Private and incognito windows are never checked. See [Already in Teak](/docs/extension#already-in-teak).

## Teak for Firefox

- The browser extension is now on [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/teak/), with everything the Chrome extension does: save the page when you open the popup, add tags and a note, the context menu, file upload, inline save buttons, pending saves, and address bar search. It needs Firefox 140 or later. See [Install for Firefox](/docs/extension#install-for-firefox).
- In private windows, Teak runs once you allow it. If Firefox always uses private browsing, Teak forgets your sign-in when Firefox closes, and the popup tells you.

## Search Teak from your browser's address bar

- With the Chrome or Firefox extension, type `teak`, then Space or Tab, in the address bar to search your library. Your top five matching cards appear as you type; choose one to open it, or press Enter to open the full search in Teak. Search qualifiers such as `#design` and `type:image` work too. See [Address bar search](/docs/extension#address-bar-search).

## Tags and a note right after saving, and text that remembers its page

- In the Chrome and Firefox extensions, once the popup saves a page, add tags and a note right there. Your most used tags and the ones you used last are suggested, and changes save when you press Enter, leave the field, or close the popup. For a page you'd already saved, the popup shows its tags and note so you can change them. See [Add tags and a note after saving](/docs/extension#add-tags-and-a-note-after-saving).
- **Save Text to Teak** now keeps the page the text came from. The card shows **From** and the site in its details, the site appears under the text in your library, and `site:` searches find it.
- **API, MCP, SDK, and CLI:** send `sourceUrl` and `sourceTitle` when creating a card, or use `teak add --source-url <url> --source-title <title>`. Cards and exports include them. See the [API guide](/docs/api#create-behavior).

## Revisit: 20 random cards, keep or archive

- On the web, choose **Revisit** from the **View** menu to go through 20 random cards from what's on screen, one at a time: your library, Favorites, a folder, a smart folder, a tag, or a search. Press `←` to archive a card (with **Undo**) or `→` to keep it, or swipe on a touch screen.
- `Space` opens the card's details and `Z` takes back the last archive. At the end, **Revisit 20 more** continues with cards you haven't seen. See [Sort and share a search](/docs/features#sort-and-share-a-search).

## Jump anywhere with ⌘K

- On the web, press `⌘K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows and Linux) to open the command palette. Go to any view, folder, or smart folder by name, find a card as you type (search qualifiers work too), or run an action such as New note, Upload files, Import, Export, Toggle theme, Shuffle, or Sign out.
- While you write a note, `⌘K` still adds a link. See [Command palette](/docs/features#command-palette).

## Focus mode for every note, new or existing

- On the web, press `⌘E` (`Ctrl+E` on Windows and Linux), or click the expand button, to write any note with nothing else on screen, not only a new one. Focus replaces the full-screen note.
- The **Close** and **Save** bar fades away while you type, and a word count appears when you pause, counting Chinese and Japanese words correctly. `Esc` returns to the card with your edits, after first closing the slash menu or link editor if one is open. See [Focus mode](/docs/features#focus-mode).

## Done items sink to the bottom of checklists

- On the web, ticking an item in a note's checklist slides it below the open items in its list, so what's left to do stays at the top. Checkboxes are redrawn too, with a check that pops in. Unticking it moves it back up, and `⌘Z` (`Ctrl+Z` on Windows and Linux) undoes both. Items in a nested list move only within their own list.

## Tables in notes

- On the web, type `/table` in a note to add a table. `Tab` moves between cells and adds a row at the end, and the **⋯** menu at the table's corner adds or deletes rows and columns. Tables are saved as Markdown, so the API, exports and the apps keep the same text, and card tiles show the cells separated by `·`. Markdown tables you already have open as tables too, unless their columns are aligned. See [Tables in notes](/docs/features#tables-in-notes).

## Hide cards with a secret or hidden tag

- Tag a card `secret` or `hidden` to keep it out of your library, Favorites, Archive, Trash, folders, search, and Shuffle, on the web, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Raycast, and the API. Cards you had already tagged this way are now hidden too.
- To see them, search for the tag: `#secret`, `tag:hidden`, or the tag from the **Tags** page. Searching the word `secret` doesn't show them, and tags Teak adds never hide a card. Remove the tag and the card is back.
- On the web, adding the tag shows **Undo**, and the card's tags show **Hidden**. Hidden cards still count toward your card limit. See [Hidden cards](/docs/features#hidden-cards).
- **API, MCP, SDK, and CLI:** lists and searches leave hidden cards out unless the search names one of those tags. Cards include `isHidden`, and `teak ls` marks them `hidden`. See the [API guide](/docs/api#hidden-cards).

## Find similar cards

- Open a card and choose **More like this** from its **⋯** menu to see the cards closest to it in meaning, from notes and links to images about the same thing. Narrow the list with any filter and save it as a smart folder. See [Find similar](/docs/features#find-similar).
- `mood:"moody brutalist"` finds images by their look and feel.
- **API, CLI, and MCP:** `GET /v1/cards?similarTo=<id>`, `teak similar <id>`, and the `teak_v1_find_similar_cards` tool return the same cards.

## Choose your own image for any link

- On the web, give a link the image you want: drop an image on its preview, paste one while the card is open, or choose **Replace image** from its **⋯** menu. A link without an image shows **Add an image**.
- Your image shows in the grid on the web and in the iPhone, iPad, and Mac apps, and stays when you refresh the card's details. **Undo** puts back the previous image, and **Reset to original image** goes back to the one Teak found. See [Replace a link's image](/docs/features#replace-a-links-image).
- **API, MCP, SDK, and CLI:** upload with `POST /v1/cards/:cardId/thumbnail/upload`, apply with `PUT /v1/cards/:cardId/thumbnail`, and reset with `DELETE`; `teak cards thumbnail <card-id> <file>` (or `--reset`) does it from the command line. See [Replace a link's image](/docs/api#replace-a-links-image).

## Search understands what you mean

- Search now finds cards by meaning as well as by their words. `cozy reading nook` also finds a photo of an armchair by a window with books, a typo such as `typograhpy` still finds your type notes, and a search in another language finds cards saved in English.
- Cards that match your words appear as fast as before, and related cards join them a moment later. Filters, qualifiers, folders, Archive, Trash, and your chosen order all still apply. See [Smart search](/docs/features#smart-search).
- **API, CLI, and MCP:** searches blend by default. `mode=keyword` (`--mode keyword` in the CLI) gives word matches only and `mode=semantic` meaning matches only, and every search response says which ran in `searchMode`. See the [API guide](/docs/api#search-and-favorites).

## Prices on product cards and `price:` search

- Product links, such as an Amazon page or a shop that describes its products, now show their price as a tag on the card in the grid, like **$24.99**, **€1,299.00**, or **Free**. Links without an image show it beside the title. Teak reads it when you save the link, and again from the page when you choose **Refresh details**.
- Search by price with `price:<100`, `price:>20`, `price:<=50`, `price:>=10`, or `price:=0`. Two bounds make a range, so `lamp price:>20 price:<100` finds lamps between 20 and 100, and you can save it as a smart folder. The amount is compared in any currency, and links without a price don't match.
- Product links you saved earlier get their price as Teak catches up on them.
- **API, CLI, and MCP:** `q` and `teak search` read `price:` too, and cards include `linkPrice` with `amount` and `currency`. `teak cards get` shows the price. See [Smart search](/docs/features#smart-search) and the [API guide](/docs/api#search-qualifiers).

## Save posts from Bluesky, Mastodon, Reddit, TikTok and more

- Saving a post from Bluesky, Mastodon, or Reddit keeps its text, author, and date, and up to 10 of its photos, the way X posts already were. Videos come along when the site offers them as a file, and their cover image otherwise.
- A TikTok keeps its caption, author, and cover image. Threads, LinkedIn, and Substack Notes posts keep the text and image their page shares.
- Mastodon posts work from any server, not just the big ones.
- A private, deleted, or login-only post is saved like any other page, so saving never fails because of it. For a post saved earlier, use **Refresh details** to fetch it again. See [Card details](/docs/features#card-details).

## Teak now recognizes places, fonts, apps, games, code repositories and social posts

- Links to Google Maps, Apple Maps, Yelp, Tripadvisor, OpenTable, Resy, Airbnb, Booking.com, Zillow, Redfin, Rightmove, and Realtor.com are places; Google Fonts, Adobe Fonts, MyFonts, Fontshare, and Future Fonts are typefaces; the App Store, Google Play, the Chrome Web Store, and the Microsoft Store are apps; Steam, itch.io, the Epic Games Store, GOG, and BoardGameGeek are games; and GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, Codeberg, and sourcehut projects are code repositories.
- Places, apps, and games show more in an open card's **Details** when the page lists it: a restaurant's address, cuisine, price range, and rating, an app's platform and rating, or a game's platform, genre, and release date. Repositories keep their GitHub stars and forks. See [Card details](/docs/features#card-details).

## Best match, and cards that know what they are

## Fixes across the web, API, CLI, and MCP

**Everywhere**

- Deleting your account now also deletes the app usage events, crash reports and session recordings linked to it, so you no longer need to email us to have them removed. See [Delete Your Account](/docs/delete-account).
- Editing a card again right after saving it no longer fails with "This card is already queued for reprocessing", on any app or the API. Every edit saves right away, and Teak refreshes the card's summary and tags within a minute.
- Editing a dozen or more notes in a minute no longer fails with "Too many cards created". Every edit saves; past a minute's worth of summary and tag refreshes, Teak keeps a card's current ones instead.

**Web**

- Searching for a color, like `orange` or `#FFA050`, or a style, like `vintage`, now finds your matching images and palettes instead of showing nothing.
- A dropped connection, like when your laptop wakes up or switches Wi-Fi, no longer asks you to sign in again. Teak reconnects on its own once you're back online.
- A link pasted on its own into **Write a note…** saves as a link card with its preview, instead of as a text note.
- Notes with underscores or asterisks, like `file_name_v2` or `2*3`, show correctly in the grid instead of with stray backslashes.
- A card deleted on another device or through the API now disappears from search results right away.
- **Delete Forever** and deleting several cards at once ask you to confirm first. The delete message no longer covers the bar for selected cards.
- Downloading a file starts right away and saves it under its original name.
- Imported bookmarks and Raindrop links keep the names you gave them.
- Adding or removing tags quickly no longer drops one of the changes, and the save shortcut (⌘↩ or Ctrl+Enter) saves a new note even while the previous one is still saving.
- Scrolling a search or a filtered view keeps loading older matches, instead of stopping when a page brought only cards already on screen.
- Opening and closing cards no longer fills your browser history: one Back after closing a card leaves the page.
- A search sorted by **Newest first** stays that way when you reload the page or share its link.
- In **Revisit**, pressing Space on a focused button presses that button instead of opening the card's details.
- Copying an SVG image copies the image, not its link.
- "Added yesterday" is right on the days the clocks change.
- Removing a tag from a card while you're viewing that tag, or adding `secret`, no longer makes the next tag you add replace all the card's others.
- Favoriting or tagging a card in search results no longer hides, for a moment, the results Teak found by a file's text or by meaning.
- A new note shows at the top of your library right away, and the "Drop files to upload" outline stays up after you drag files across a text box.
- Closing the recording window while your browser asks for the microphone no longer leaves a recording running in the background.
- Typing a search and scrolling a big library are faster: cards that didn't change no longer redraw, and link images below the fold wait until you scroll to them.
- Pasting a screenshot into **Write a note…** saves it as an image card, as pasting it anywhere else on the page does.
- An upload that stops responding is retried and then reported, instead of showing "Uploading…" forever. Once you reach the free plan's card limit, the rest of the files you dropped are skipped rather than uploaded first, and errors on large files say what went wrong.
- Leaving a card right after typing in it, by clicking a tag or its type, or with Back, keeps what you typed. Closing or reloading the tab while a change is still saving asks first.
- In **Favorites** or a tag's view, the previous and next card arrows keep working after you unfavorite or untag the open card.

**Summaries and tags**

- Recordings with no speech no longer get made-up transcripts, summaries, or tags.
- A card no longer shows the same AI tag twice.
- PDF summaries are written from the document's text instead of only its file name.
- Links that no longer exist fail quickly instead of retrying.
- A link saved while its site's address briefly couldn't be looked up is tried again, instead of being marked as a blocked address.
- A photo whose time zone is stored wrongly shows the time the camera recorded, instead of a shifted time or none.
- A podcast episode whose transcript has to be retried uses one of your hourly transcripts, not one per try.
- A link saved while Teak was briefly unreachable is tried again instead of showing a failed preview, and pages served as XHTML get their preview too.
- A large export that has to retry picks up where it stopped instead of starting over, and an import retries a file it briefly couldn't unpack instead of marking it failed.

**API**

- `PATCH /v1/cards/:id` can change a card's text and URL in one request.
- `GET /v1/cards/changes` now includes cards deleted forever, so synced apps remove them too.
- Paging through cards and search results no longer repeats or skips cards when new ones are saved in between.
- `GET /v1/tags` lists every tag you can filter by, including tags Teak added, in lowercase, with an `isComplete` flag for very large libraries.
- Deleting a card forever needs it to be in Trash first; otherwise the API answers `409 NOT_IN_TRASH`. Busy moments return `503` with `Retry-After` instead of an error, and `RateLimit-Reset` is now in seconds. See the [API guide](/docs/api).
- Bulk requests to `/v1/cards/bulk` follow the same rules as single-card requests: an empty `url` or empty content no longer wipes a card, cards in Trash can't be edited until they're restored, and an image, video, audio or document card still needs an uploaded file when it has a `url`.
- Renaming, merging or deleting a tag works for older tags longer than 64 characters, through the API and MCP.
- A request body over 20 MB is refused with `413` even when it's sent without a `Content-Length`.
- `removeAiTags` matches tags ignoring case, as `GET /v1/tags` lists them, and changes nothing when no tag matches.
- Bulk `create` trims notes and drops a blank one, as `POST /v1/cards` does. Bulk `create` and `update` items with a field they don't take, or an `update` item with nothing to change, fail with `INVALID_INPUT` instead of succeeding without it.
- Adding cards to a folder, or moving or removing them, skips cards in Trash as not found, and a folder you just deleted no longer counts toward the 500-folder limit.

**CLI**

- `teak add "text" --url …` keeps your text alongside the link.
- `teak rm` and `teak restore` with several cards keep going past a card that fails, and say which ones did. `teak rm --permanent` works on cards that aren't in Trash yet.
- `teak export download -o` saves where you asked, and `teak open` prints the link instead of crashing on machines without a browser. See the [CLI guide](/docs/cli).
- `teak cards changes` lists the cards deleted since then and prints the next page's cursor, without needing `--json`.
- The CLI and SDK give up with a timeout error when a response stops partway, instead of waiting forever.
- `teak export -o` and `teak export --wait` exit with an error when the export fails, so a backup script notices.

**MCP**

- Unknown tools and out-of-range limits return clear errors, and a bulk action that only partly succeeds is reported as an error with the cards that failed. See the [MCP guide](/docs/mcp).
- A batch of only notifications gets `202 Accepted` with no body, as the protocol expects.

**Raycast**

- Text that starts with a link saves the link, not the whole text as one broken address, and a "quoted phrase" in search stays a phrase.

**Chrome extension**

- Browser pages Teak can't save, such as other browsers' settings pages and extension stores, show a clear message instead of an error.
- **Save Text to Teak** keeps a selection's line breaks, and a selection that starts with a link saves as text instead of as a broken link.
- **Save Asset to Teak** on an ordinary link saves the link, and still saves the file when the link points to one.

## A cleaner Chrome extension popup

- The popup now matches the Teak web app and shows what it saved, with the site name.
- Opening it on a page you already saved says **Already in your Teak** and stays open, instead of briefly showing that the page was saved.
- The pending saves notice only appears for saves that didn't finish, not while a save is still in progress.
- Inline save buttons no longer appear on Web Designer News, which changed its layout. Right-click **Save Page to Teak** or the popup still saves any of its pages.
