## Sign in again after the sign-in update

Teak moved to a new sign-in system on October 7. Your library, cards, Pro plan, and account ownership stay exactly as they were.

- **Everyone:** sign in again on the web, mobile, Teak for Mac (including Safari saving), Chrome, Raycast, and the CLI.
- **If you signed in with a password:** reset your password from the sign-in page before you sign in. Sign-in emails come from Teak.
- **If you use Google or Apple:** continue with the same option as before.
- **MCP clients connected with browser sign-in:** remove Teak from the client, add it again, and sign in. API keys keep working without changes. See the [MCP guide](/docs/mcp).

New account sign-ups are paused for now while the move settles.

## Write notes on the web

- Notes support headings, lists, tasks, quotes, code, and links while you write. Type `/` for formatting commands or select text for quick formatting.
- Your notes stay in Markdown. Existing tables, image references, and other unsupported formatting remain editable as text, with no changes until you edit.

## Capture and organize on Mac

- Save notes, quotes, files, and recorded audio from the Mac library alongside links and Safari pages.
- Browse a refreshed native library, search by tags, dates, colors, and style, and use familiar Mac keyboard shortcuts.
- Edit text and quotes, organize notes and tags, toggle favorites, and manage Trash without switching to the web app. Changes sync across your library.

See the [Mac app guide](/docs/mac) to get started.
