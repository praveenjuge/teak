## Sign in again after the sign-in update

Teak moved to a new sign-in system on October 7. Your library, cards, Pro plan, and account ownership stay exactly as they were.

- **Everyone:** sign in again on the web, mobile, Teak for Mac (including Safari saving), Chrome, Raycast, and the CLI.
- **If you signed in with a password:** reset your password from the sign-in page before you sign in. Sign-in emails come from Teak.
- **If you use Google or Apple:** continue with the same option as before.
- **Raycast:** update the extension to the latest version from the Raycast Store, then sign in again. See the [Raycast guide](/docs/raycast).
- **MCP clients connected with browser sign-in:** remove Teak from the client, add it again, and sign in. API keys keep working without changes. See the [MCP guide](/docs/mcp).

New accounts can sign up again on the new sign-in system. On the web, signing in, creating an account, and resetting your password now open the sign-in page directly, with no extra step. The mobile app works the same way: **Login with Email** and **Register with Email** go straight to the sign-in page, and logging out takes you back to the app's welcome screen without opening a browser. **Continue with Apple** on iPhone and iPad works again, and new accounts open their library right away instead of sometimes showing a sign-in error.

## Upgrade to Pro in a new tab

On the web, **Continue to checkout** in Settings now opens checkout in a new tab instead of a window over Teak, and Settings stays open behind it.

## Write notes on the web

- Notes support headings, lists, tasks, quotes, code, and links while you write. Type `/` for formatting commands or select text for quick formatting.
- Your notes stay in Markdown. Existing tables, image references, and other unsupported formatting remain editable as text, with no changes until you edit.

## Capture and organize on Mac

- Save notes, quotes, files, and recorded audio from the Mac library alongside links and Safari pages.
- Browse a refreshed native library, search by tags, dates, colors, and style, and use familiar Mac keyboard shortcuts.
- Edit text and quotes, organize notes and tags, toggle favorites, and manage Trash without switching to the web app. Changes sync across your library.

- Paste with ⌘V to save the clipboard, and save from anywhere with **Quick Capture** (⌃⌥⌘T), **Save to Teak** in the Services menu, or by dropping onto the menu bar icon.
- Select several cards to favorite them or move them to Trash together. Click a tag to see every card with exactly that tag.
- Notes and Markdown files show formatted, source files show with line numbers, and you can remove tags Teak added. **Copy Link** and **Open on Web** share a card.
- Settings shows your plan, with **Upgrade…** or **Manage…** on the web.

See the [Mac app guide](/docs/mac) to get started.

## A new Teak for iPhone and iPad

Teak on iPhone and iPad is now a native app, rebuilt to feel at home on both. It needs iOS or iPadOS 26.

- A faster grid with more columns and a details panel on iPad.
- Save from any app with the share sheet, or from Safari with the new **Teak** Safari extension.
- Edit a card's text, notes, and tags in one place, and swipe a card to favorite or delete it on iOS 27.
- Type words like "image", "favorites", or "last week" in search to turn them into filters.
- **Save to Teak** in Siri and Shortcuts now saves without opening the app.

See the [mobile guide](/docs/mobile).

## More of your library on iPhone

- Edit a card's notes and tags, rewrite notes and quotes, and remove tags Teak added.
- Filter Home by favorites, type, or color, and open **Trash** to restore cards or delete them forever.
- Select several cards to delete or restore them together, and pick up to five photos or files at once.
- Save text or links from any shortcut or Siri with the **Save to Teak** action.
- Notes show their formatting, links show their details and post photos, and documents open in a viewer. Tap a tag to search for it.
- Settings shows your plan and how many cards you've used.

See the [mobile guide](/docs/mobile).

## Video, audio, and PDF previews

- **Video hover previews:** on the web, move your mouse across a video card to skim through the video.
- **Real audio waveforms:** audio cards on the web show the shape of the recording, and the player shows your progress through it.
- **More thumbnails:** HEVC, MKV, and AVI videos now get thumbnails, and WebM videos and large PDFs get them more reliably.
- **More transcripts:** recordings the transcriber can't read directly, like some Ogg and Opus files, are converted first, so more of them get transcripts.
- **Instagram posts:** saving an Instagram post keeps its photos and videos when Instagram shows them, and its cover image otherwise.

These apply to videos, recordings, and links you save from now on.

## Raycast, CLI, API, and MCP

- **Raycast:** save files from Finder or the clipboard, see palette colors and transcripts, scroll through all results, filter by color, date, and Trash, restore cards, and remove tags Teak added. Saving a page you already have says so. See the [Raycast guide](/docs/raycast).
- **CLI:** `teak trash`, `teak restore`, `teak open`, `teak download`, `teak export`, `teak whoami`, and `teak tags add` / `rm`, plus color, style, and plain-English date filters. Text you add becomes a quote or palette when it is one, like on the web. See the [CLI guide](/docs/cli).
- **API:** `GET /v1/me` includes your plan and card usage, `PATCH /v1/cards/:id` can remove tags Teak added, and the new export endpoints start a ZIP export and return its download link. Cards include `audioWaveform` and `videoScrub` for building your own previews. See the [API guide](/docs/api#export-your-cards).
- **MCP:** the list and search tools take the same filters as the API, and new tools restore cards from Trash, check whether a URL is saved, and show your plan. See the [MCP guide](/docs/mcp).

## Fixes across the web, API, CLI, and MCP

**Web**

- A dropped connection, like when your laptop wakes up or switches Wi-Fi, no longer asks you to sign in again. Teak reconnects on its own once you're back online.
- A link pasted on its own into **Write a note…** saves as a link card with its preview, instead of as a text note.
- Notes with underscores or asterisks, like `file_name_v2` or `2*3`, show correctly in the grid instead of with stray backslashes.
- A card deleted on another device or through the API now disappears from search results right away.
- Cards can be reached with the keyboard: Tab to a card and press Enter to open it.
- **Delete Forever** and deleting several cards at once ask you to confirm first. The delete message no longer covers the bar for selected cards.
- Downloading a file starts right away and saves it under its original name.
- Imported bookmarks and Raindrop links keep the names you gave them.
- Adding or removing tags quickly no longer drops one of the changes, and the save shortcut (⌘↩ or Ctrl+Enter) saves a new note even while the previous one is still saving.

**Summaries and tags**

- Recordings with no speech no longer get made-up transcripts, summaries, or tags.
- A card no longer shows the same AI tag twice.
- PDF summaries are written from the document's text instead of only its file name.
- Links that no longer exist fail quickly instead of retrying.

**API**

- `PATCH /v1/cards/:id` can change a card's text and URL in one request.
- `GET /v1/cards/changes` now includes cards deleted forever, so synced apps remove them too.
- Paging through cards and search results no longer repeats or skips cards when new ones are saved in between.
- `GET /v1/tags` lists every tag you can filter by, including tags Teak added, in lowercase, with an `isComplete` flag for very large libraries.
- Deleting a card forever needs it to be in Trash first; otherwise the API answers `409 NOT_IN_TRASH`. Busy moments return `503` with `Retry-After` instead of an error, and `RateLimit-Reset` is now in seconds. See the [API guide](/docs/api).

**CLI**

- `teak add "text" --url …` keeps your text alongside the link.
- `teak rm` and `teak restore` with several cards keep going past a card that fails, and say which ones did. `teak rm --permanent` works on cards that aren't in Trash yet.
- `teak export download -o` saves where you asked, and `teak open` prints the link instead of crashing on machines without a browser. See the [CLI guide](/docs/cli).

**MCP**

- Unknown tools and out-of-range limits return clear errors, and a bulk action that only partly succeeds is reported as an error with the cards that failed. See the [MCP guide](/docs/mcp).

**Chrome extension**

- Browser pages Teak can't save, such as other browsers' settings pages and extension stores, show a clear message instead of an error.

## A cleaner Chrome extension popup

- The popup now matches the Teak web app and shows what it saved, with the site name.
- Opening it on a page you already saved says **Already in your Teak** and stays open, instead of briefly showing that the page was saved.
- The pending saves notice only appears for saves that didn't finish, not while a save is still in progress.
- Inline save buttons no longer appear on Web Designer News, which changed its layout. Right-click **Save Page to Teak** or the popup still saves any of its pages.
