## A calmer card grid and card view

- On the web, cards in the grid are easier to scan: links show their site's icon beside the title, notes show their first line as a heading and more of what follows, quotes and palettes read more cleanly, documents show their file type, and audio shows its length.
- Rest the pointer on a card to open its link, favorite it, select it, or open **More** for every other action, without right-clicking.
- An open card has a header with its title, site, and when you added it, plus **Open** or **Download**, favorite, and **More**. Notes, tags, folders, colors, and details sit beside it and edit in place, so there are no extra windows; notes, a note's text, and a link's renamed title save on their own as you type.
- Step through cards without closing them, with the arrows at the top or `←` and `→`. `F` favorites, `T` adds a tag, `N` writes a note, `E` archives, and `Delete` moves the card to Trash. On a phone the card opens as a sheet from the bottom. See [Grid](/docs/features#grid).
- Selecting cards no longer moves them around the grid, an open card closes the moment you dismiss it, and messages such as **Copied** appear as a small centered pill instead of a bar along the bottom, so they read fully even over an open card.

## Shuffle your library

- On the web, choose **Shuffle** from the **View** menu beside the search bar to see your cards in a random order and rediscover what you saved long ago. The order holds while you scroll, so no card repeats or goes missing.
- **Reshuffle** (the dice beside the **View** menu) picks a new order, and Back returns to the previous one. The order is part of the address, so a reload or a shared link shows the same one.
- Shuffle works in search, tags, folders, Favorites, Archive, and Trash too. See [Sort and share a search](/docs/features#sort-and-share-a-search).
- **API, CLI, and MCP:** `GET /v1/cards?sort=shuffle&shuffleSeed=42` returns the same order for the same seed, `teak ls --sort shuffle --seed 42` does the same, and the MCP list and search tools take `sort: "shuffle"` with `shuffleSeed`. See the [API guide](/docs/api).

## Import from more apps

- Settings → Import/Export Data now brings in your saves from Pocket, Instapaper, Readwise Reader, Omnivore, Linkwarden, mymind, and OneTab, as well as browser bookmarks, Raindrop.io, and Teak archives. Pick the source and Teak tells you which file to upload.
- Titles, tags, notes, highlights, favorites, archived items, and saved dates come along wherever the source has them.
- **Changed:** folders in bookmark files and Raindrop exports now become Teak folders instead of tags. A nested folder becomes one folder named after its path, like **Work / Design**.
- A file that isn't from the source you picked now says so, instead of showing an error code. See [Import Your Data](/docs/import).

## Richer exports

- An export now includes `bookmarks.html`, which Chrome, Firefox, and Safari import with your folders, and `cards.csv`, a spreadsheet of every card that opens safely in Excel, Numbers, and Google Sheets.
- `cards.json` adds each card's title, AI tags and summary, folder names, and whether it's archived.
- Importing a new Teak archive brings back your folders, archived cards, and titles. Older archives import as before. See [Export Your Data](/docs/export).

## Keyboard shortcuts on the web

- Move through your library without a mouse: the arrow keys go to the nearest card, `Enter` opens it, `F` favorites, `E` archives, `Delete` moves it to Trash, and `X` selects it; with cards selected (outside Trash), the same keys act on all of them. Closing a card brings you back to it.
- `/` jumps to search, `N` starts a new note, `U` uploads files, and `G` then `F`, `A`, `T`, or `E` opens Favorites, Archive, Trash, or Everything.
- Press `?` to see them all. Shortcuts never fire while you type. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/features#keyboard-shortcuts).
- **Delete** on a card now offers **Undo**, like moving several cards to Trash.

## Smart folders

- On the web, save any search as a smart folder: search, open the **View** menu at the end of the search box, and choose **Save as smart folder…**. It shows every card that matches, including cards you save later, so a `tag:design type:link` folder fills itself.
- Smart folders sit with your folders and have a search icon. The search shows at the top of the folder with **Edit search**, and typing narrows it further. A smart folder can look inside a regular folder with `folder:`, and follows it if you rename it.
- **API, CLI, and MCP:** create one with `POST /v1/folders` and `{ "kind": "smart", "query": "..." }`, change its search with `PATCH`, and `GET /v1/cards?folder=` lists what it matches. The CLI adds `teak folders create --query`, and the MCP folder tools take `kind` and `query`. See [Smart folders](/docs/features#smart-folders) and the [API guide](/docs/api#folders).

## Search with qualifiers

- On the web, narrow a search with `tag:design` (or `#design`), `type:link`, `is:fav`, `is:archived`, `site:github.com`, `folder:"Reading list"`, and `"quoted phrases"`, and mix them with words, colors, and dates.
- As you type, the search box suggests filters, your tags and folders, and the sites you save most. Each one you pick becomes a removable pill, and the address bar keeps the whole search for reloads and shared links.
- `site:` treats `www.github.com` and `GitHub.com` as the same site, and international addresses work in either form.
- **API, CLI, and MCP:** `GET /v1/cards?q=`, `teak search`, and the MCP search tool read the same qualifiers, together with their other filters. See [Smart search](/docs/features#smart-search) and the [API guide](/docs/api#search-qualifiers).

## Manage tags across your library

- On the web, **Tags** in the sidebar opens a page with every tag and how many cards carry each. Find one, sort by name, and click a tag to see only its cards; it shows as a pill under the search bar.
- Rename, merge, or delete a tag from its menu on the Tags page, and Teak changes it on every card at once: your tags and AI tags, in your library, Archive, and Trash. A message at the bottom of the page shows the progress.
- **API, CLI, and MCP:** `POST /v1/tags/rename`, `/v1/tags/merge`, and `/v1/tags/delete` start a change and `GET /v1/tags/operations/:id` reports its progress. The CLI adds `teak tags rename`, `merge`, and `delete` (with `--wait`), and MCP adds four tag tools. See [Tags and filters](/docs/features#tags-and-filters) and the [API guide](/docs/api#rename-merge-and-delete-tags).
- `GET /v1/tags` no longer fails for a library with thousands of distinct tags: past 500, it lists the 3,000 most-used from a summary that catches up within seconds.

## Organize cards into folders

- On the web, create folders from the sidebar, give them a color, and add cards from a card's **Folders…** menu or by selecting many at once. A card can be in several folders, and removing it from one never deletes it.
- Open a folder to search, filter, and sort just its cards. Rename, recolor, or delete folders from the sidebar; deleting keeps the cards, and **Undo** brings the folder back.
- Up to 500 folders, with a card in up to 50 of them. Archived cards and cards in Trash stay out of a folder until you bring them back.
- **API, CLI, and MCP:** `/v1/folders` creates, renames, and deletes folders, `POST` and `DELETE /v1/folders/:id/cards` add and remove cards, `GET /v1/cards?folder=` lists a folder's cards, and cards include `folderIds`. The CLI adds `teak folders` and `teak ls --folder`, and MCP adds six folder tools. See [Folders](/docs/features#folders) and the [API guide](/docs/api#folders).

## A sidebar for your library

- On the web, a compact sidebar holds **Everything**, **Favorites**, **Tags**, **Archive**, **Trash**, and **Settings**, each its own page you can bookmark, share, and reach with Back and Forward, with your folders below them.
- A slim header on every page shows the page's name, a compact search box with your filters as pills inside it, and one **View** menu for card types, order, and saving a smart folder. It stays at the top as you scroll, and the address bar keeps your filters for reloads and shared links.
- **+** beside the Teak logo starts a note or link, uploads files, or records audio. Drag a card onto a folder to add it, or name a new folder right in the list.
- Drag the sidebar's edge to resize it. Collapse it to icons and rest the pointer on them to see it in full; Teak remembers the width and your choice. On a phone it opens as a drawer.
- Typing `fav`, `archive`, or `trash` in search now opens that page. See [Sidebar](/docs/features#sidebar).

## Archive cards and act on many at once

- **Archive:** right-click a card or open it and choose **Archive** to keep it out of your library without deleting it. Open **Archive** in the sidebar to find it again, and **Unarchive** to bring it back. Archived cards still count toward your card limit.
- **Bulk actions:** select cards and use the bar across the top to archive them, add them to favorites, add or remove tags, copy their links, or move them to Trash, hundreds at a time. Archive and Move to Trash offer **Undo**, and cards that couldn't be changed stay selected so you can retry.
- **API, CLI, and MCP:** `PATCH /v1/cards/:id` takes `isArchived`, `GET /v1/cards?archived=only` lists archived cards, and `POST /v1/cards/bulk` adds `archive`, `unarchive`, `addTags`, `removeTags`, and `restore` and returns `failedIds`. The CLI adds `teak archive`, `teak unarchive`, and `teak ls --archived`; MCP adds `teak_v1_set_card_archived`. See the [API guide](/docs/api#archive-cards).

## Sort your library oldest first

- On the web, the **View** menu beside the search bar switches between **Newest first** and **Oldest first**.
- Cards are now ordered by the date you saved them everywhere, so imported bookmarks appear where they were first saved instead of at the top. This also applies to the API, CLI, MCP, Raycast, and the iPhone and Android apps.
- Your search and sort stay in the address bar, so reloading, Back and Forward, and shared links keep the same view.
- **API:** `GET /v1/cards?sort=` orders by `createdAt` on every listing. A `cursor` from before this update returns `400 INVALID_INPUT`; start again from the first page. See the [API guide](/docs/api).

## Refresh a card, and richer card details

- **Refresh details:** open a card and choose **Refresh details**, or right-click it in the grid, to get a fresh summary, tags, transcript, and link preview. Your own tags and notes stay. It's also available as `POST /v1/cards/:id/refresh` in the [API](/docs/api#refresh-a-cards-details), `teak refresh` in the [CLI](/docs/cli), and `teak_v1_refresh_card` in [MCP](/docs/mcp).
- **Who wrote it, and when:** link cards on the web show the author and publish date next to the site name when the page includes them.
- **Photo details:** **Info** on a photo lists its camera, exposure, aperture, focal length, ISO, and when it was taken. Teak never shows where a photo was taken.

## A hands-on start for new accounts

- New accounts start with a **Start here** folder of eight cards, one of each type: a welcome checklist, a photo, a palette, a short video tour, a voice memo, a search cheat sheet PDF, a quote, and a link to the Teak apps. Each card's note suggests something to try, such as searching for a color or for a word spoken in the memo.
- A **Moodboard** smart folder shows how a saved search fills itself, collecting your images and palettes as you save them.
- The cards are ordinary cards tagged `getting-started`. Select them in **Start here** and move them to Trash when you're done.

## Sign in again after the sign-in update

Teak moved to a new sign-in system on October 7. Your library, cards, Pro plan, and account ownership stay exactly as they were.

- **Everyone:** sign in again on the web, iPhone, iPad, Mac (including Safari saving), Chrome, Raycast, and the CLI.
- **If you signed in with a password:** reset your password from the sign-in page before you sign in. Sign-in emails come from Teak.
- **If you use Google or Apple:** continue with the same option as before.
- **Raycast:** update the extension to the latest version from the Raycast Store, then sign in again. See the [Raycast guide](/docs/raycast).
- **MCP clients connected with browser sign-in:** remove Teak from the client, add it again, and sign in. API keys keep working without changes. See the [MCP guide](/docs/mcp).

New accounts can sign up again on the new sign-in system. On the web, signing in, creating an account, and resetting your password now open the sign-in page directly, with no extra step. The mobile app works the same way: **Login with Email** and **Register with Email** go straight to the sign-in page, and logging out takes you back to the app's welcome screen without opening a browser. **Continue with Apple** on iPhone and iPad works again, and new accounts open their library right away instead of sometimes showing a sign-in error.

Install it from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teak-save-inspirations/id6756574989) or the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/teak-save-inspirations/id6756574989?mt=12) and sign in; your library is waiting.

- A faster grid with more columns and a details panel on iPad.
- Save from any app with the share sheet, or from Safari with the new **Teak** Safari extension.
- Edit a card's text, notes, and tags in one place, and swipe a card to favorite or delete it on iOS 27.

## Capture and organize on Mac

- Your whole library in a native Mac window, with a sidebar for Favorites, Trash, and each card type, search by words, dates, colors, and `#tag`, and familiar Mac keyboard shortcuts.
- Save notes, links, voice memos, and files from the toolbar or the **File** menu, drag them onto the window, or press ⌘V to save the clipboard.
- Save from anywhere with **Quick Capture** (⌃⌥⌘T), **Save to Teak** in the Services menu, or by dropping onto the menu bar icon.
- Right-click a card to tag, favorite, share, or delete it, or **Copy Link** and **Open on Web**. Select several cards to favorite them or move them to Trash together, and restore or delete them forever from Trash.

- Settings opens in its own window and shows your plan, with **Upgrade…** or **Manage…** on the web.

See the [Mac app guide](/docs/mac) to get started.

- Select several cards to delete or restore them together, and pick up to 20 photos or files at once.
- Save text or links from any shortcut or Siri with the **Save to Teak** action.
- Notes show their formatting, links show their details and post photos, and documents open in a viewer. Tap a tag to search for it.
- Settings shows your plan and how many cards you've used.

## Video, audio, and PDF previews

- **Video hover previews:** on the web, move your mouse across a video card to skim through the video.
- **Real audio waveforms:** audio cards on the web show the shape of the recording, and the player shows your progress through it.
- **More thumbnails:** HEVC, MKV, and AVI videos now get thumbnails, and WebM videos and large PDFs get them more reliably.
- **More transcripts:** recordings the transcriber can't read directly, like some Ogg and Opus files, are converted first, so more of them get transcripts.
- **Instagram posts:** saving an Instagram post keeps its photos and videos when Instagram shows them, and its cover image otherwise.

These apply to videos, recordings, and links you save from now on.

## Raycast, CLI, API, and MCP

- **Raycast:** save files from Finder or the clipboard, see palette colors and transcripts, scroll through all results, filter by color, date, and Trash, restore cards, and remove tags Teak added. Saving a page you already have says so. See the [Raycast guide](/docs/raycast).
- **CLI:** `teak trash`, `teak restore`, `teak open`, `teak download`, `teak export`, `teak whoami`, and `teak tags add` / `rm`, plus color, style, and plain-English date filters. Text you add becomes a quote or palette when it is one, like on the web. See the [CLI guide](/docs/cli).
- **API:** `GET /v1/me` includes your plan and card usage, `PATCH /v1/cards/:id` can remove tags Teak added, and the new export endpoints start a ZIP export and return its download link. Cards include `audioWaveform` and `videoScrub` for building your own previews. See the [API guide](/docs/api#export-your-cards).
- **MCP:** the list and search tools take the same filters as the API, and new tools restore cards from Trash, check whether a URL is saved, and show your plan. See the [MCP guide](/docs/mcp).

## Fixes across the web, API, CLI, and MCP

**Everywhere**

- Editing a card again right after saving it no longer fails with "This card is already queued for reprocessing", on any app or the API. Every edit saves right away, and Teak refreshes the card's summary and tags within a minute.

**Web**

- Searching for a color, like `orange` or `#FFA050`, or a style, like `vintage`, now finds your matching images and palettes instead of showing nothing.
- A dropped connection, like when your laptop wakes up or switches Wi-Fi, no longer asks you to sign in again. Teak reconnects on its own once you're back online.
- A link pasted on its own into **Write a note…** saves as a link card with its preview, instead of as a text note.
- Notes with underscores or asterisks, like `file_name_v2` or `2*3`, show correctly in the grid instead of with stray backslashes.
- A card deleted on another device or through the API now disappears from search results right away.
- **Delete Forever** and deleting several cards at once ask you to confirm first. The delete message no longer covers the bar for selected cards.
- Downloading a file starts right away and saves it under its original name.
- Imported bookmarks and Raindrop links keep the names you gave them.
- Adding or removing tags quickly no longer drops one of the changes, and the save shortcut (⌘↩ or Ctrl+Enter) saves a new note even while the previous one is still saving.

**Summaries and tags**

- Recordings with no speech no longer get made-up transcripts, summaries, or tags.
- A card no longer shows the same AI tag twice.
- PDF summaries are written from the document's text instead of only its file name.
- Links that no longer exist fail quickly instead of retrying.

**API**

- `PATCH /v1/cards/:id` can change a card's text and URL in one request.
- `GET /v1/cards/changes` now includes cards deleted forever, so synced apps remove them too.
- Paging through cards and search results no longer repeats or skips cards when new ones are saved in between.
- `GET /v1/tags` lists every tag you can filter by, including tags Teak added, in lowercase, with an `isComplete` flag for very large libraries.
- Deleting a card forever needs it to be in Trash first; otherwise the API answers `409 NOT_IN_TRASH`. Busy moments return `503` with `Retry-After` instead of an error, and `RateLimit-Reset` is now in seconds. See the [API guide](/docs/api).

**CLI**

- `teak add "text" --url …` keeps your text alongside the link.
- `teak rm` and `teak restore` with several cards keep going past a card that fails, and say which ones did. `teak rm --permanent` works on cards that aren't in Trash yet.
- `teak export download -o` saves where you asked, and `teak open` prints the link instead of crashing on machines without a browser. See the [CLI guide](/docs/cli).

**MCP**

- Unknown tools and out-of-range limits return clear errors, and a bulk action that only partly succeeds is reported as an error with the cards that failed. See the [MCP guide](/docs/mcp).

**Chrome extension**

- Browser pages Teak can't save, such as other browsers' settings pages and extension stores, show a clear message instead of an error.

## A cleaner Chrome extension popup

- The popup now matches the Teak web app and shows what it saved, with the site name.
- Opening it on a page you already saved says **Already in your Teak** and stays open, instead of briefly showing that the page was saved.
- The pending saves notice only appears for saves that didn't finish, not while a save is still in progress.
- Inline save buttons no longer appear on Web Designer News, which changed its layout. Right-click **Save Page to Teak** or the popup still saves any of its pages.
