- Drop files anywhere in the web app to upload them, with one progress summary per batch.
- The box at the top of your library is now a focused note, with the other ways to save beside it.
- Mobile is steadier: photos, videos, audio and documents save again, you stay signed in, your appearance choice sticks, and going offline shows a clear retry screen.
- Link previews share only a site's name, never the full address you saved, so private links stay private.
- Raycast adds one-step saving, **Save Selected Text**, a tag browser and more AI tools, and filtering by a tag with spaces works.

## For developers

- Paging through cards with the API returns every card, and bulk requests take up to 100 cards.
