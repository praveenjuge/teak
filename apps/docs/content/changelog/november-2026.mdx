## Teak for Android

- Get Teak on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.praveenjuge.teak) for Android phones and tablets, and sign in with Google, Apple or email. See [Android](/docs/android).
- Share links, text, photos, videos and files into Teak from any app, or select text and choose **Save to Teak**.
- It's the same library as on the web, iPhone and Mac, and it keeps working offline.

## Pin cards to the top

- On the web, pin up to 12 cards to keep them in a **Pinned** row above **Everything**: choose **Pin to top** from a card's menu, or press `P`. Drag pinned cards to reorder them. See [Pinned cards](/docs/features#pinned-cards).
- Search `is:pinned` to find them all. Favorites and pins are separate, so a card can be both.

## Folders that tag and file cards for you

- Give a folder rules: tags that every card put in it gets, or a search whose matching cards Teak files into it automatically. See [Folder rules](/docs/features#folder-rules).
- Saving a rule applies it to the cards you already have, with **Undo**.

## A calmer card view

- On the web, an open card's actions now sit in its details, under its title: **Open** or **Download**, then **Favorite**, **Done**, **Copy**, **Folder** and **More**. They stay in view as you scroll. See [Grid](/docs/features#grid).
- Photos and videos fill the card with nothing laid over them.
- Change a link's address or image from its **Details**.

## A calmer Settings and extension popup

- Every Settings page on the web is a short, scannable list, and **Appearance** picks System, Light or Dark in one place.
- In Chrome and Firefox, the extension popup shows what you're saving, with its tags and note, and whether it's saved or already in Teak. **Open Teak**, **Upload file** and **Sign out** moved into its **⋯** menu. See [Browser extension](/docs/extension).

## Quicker capture on the Mac

- **New Note** (⌘N) opens the small Quick Capture window, and **Voice Memo** (⇧⌘R) records in a small window that stays on top. See [Mac](/docs/mac#saving).
- Dropping something on the menu bar icon shows when it's saved.

## Saving and search

- A note you're typing at the top of your library survives a reload until you save or clear it.
- Pasting a list of links with repeats saves each link once, and saving a link that's in Trash offers to restore it.
- When uploads fail, Teak names each file and says why.
- Search understands everyday words like `note`, `pdf` and `photo`, and a date beside other words, like `kestrel last week`, filters by date.
- Searches show how many cards match and how many are related by meaning, and when nothing matches, Teak suggests a close tag or title.
- **View** has a **Compact** card size.
- Imports handle more messy files and tell you which lines they skipped. See [Import](/docs/import).

## Fixes

- Right-clicking a card always opens its menu, and clicking a tag on an open card shows every card with that tag.
- Select every card shown with ⌘A, and delete several cards in Trash together.
- Highlights snap to whole words.
- Teak no longer writes a summary or tags for a link whose page couldn't be reached.
- **Security** lists every signed-in device, this one first, and **Sign out other devices** signs out the rest.
- Keyboard focus is easier to see, and a **Skip to content** link comes first.

## For developers and self-hosters

- Pins and folder rules are in the API, MCP, SDK and CLI.
- API keys can have names.
- A self-hosted Core Worker now deploys without editing its config, so updates pull cleanly. Existing installs keep working. See [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting#core-worker).
- A self-hosted Teak sends no analytics until you add your own PostHog token, and a web app off Vercel can report to it too. See [Telemetry](/docs/self-hosting#telemetry-optional).
- A self-hosted Teak no longer needs Polar: turn billing off and every account gets Pro with no card limit. See [Billing](/docs/self-hosting#billing-optional).
