## A card's actions sit beside it

- On the web, an open card no longer has a bar across its top. Its details beside it now start with where the card is from, its type and its title, which wraps onto a few lines instead of being cut off. Under the title are **Open** (or **Download**) and a row of labelled buttons: **Favorite**, **Done** for a link (it marks the link read, watched, bought and so on), **Copy**, **Folder** and **More**. Scroll the details and the buttons stay pinned to the top in one line. See [Cards](/docs/features#grid).
- Photos and videos fill the card, with nothing over them when you point at them.
- To change a link's address, click the pencil beside its URL in **Details**. **Replace** and **Reset** for a link's image sit beside **Image** there too.
- A card in Trash shows **Restore** and **Delete forever** at the top of its details.
- The details now stay beside the card on wide screens, so `I` no longer hides them.

## A calmer extension popup

- In Chrome and Firefox, the popup now shows what you're saving: the page's icon and title with its site, or the file's name and size. Its tags and note sit in the same card, and the line above it says whether it's saving, saved or already in your Teak. See [Browser extension](/docs/extension#auto-save-on-open).
- **Open Teak**, **Upload file** and **Sign out** moved into the **⋯** menu at the top, and your email shows at the top of that menu.

## Quicker capture on the Mac

- **New Note** (⌘N) now opens the small Quick Capture window, which follows your Light or Dark setting. Its placeholder lines up with where you type. See [Saving](/docs/mac#saving).
- **Voice Memo** (⇧⌘R) records in its own small window that stays above other apps, and it's in the menu bar item's menu too.
- Drop something on the menu bar icon and it shows an arrow while it saves, then a check. Dropping a file there no longer crashes Teak.
- **New folder** is now the last row of **Folders** in the sidebar, and the note, notes and tag fields in **Edit card** keep your text on the left.

## Saving is harder to lose

- A note you're typing in the box at the top of your cards stays there through a reload until you save it or clear it. Press **Esc** to leave the box, so single-key shortcuts work again.
- Pasting a list of links with repeats saves each link once, and the count says so before you save.
- Saving a link that's in Trash offers **Restore** instead of quietly making a second copy.
- When some uploads fail, the message names every file and says why in plain words, and the file picker only offers files Teak can save.
- Press **R** to record audio. In a note, a table's **⋯** button no longer covers the header, and `[[` lists the closest title match first without covering **Save**.

## Cards, selection and Trash

- Right-clicking a card always opens its menu; releasing the button no longer picks the item under the pointer.
- Clicking a tag on an open card shows every card with that tag.
- Card previews read cleanly, with no stray Markdown symbols, and headings and lists read as sentences.
- With cards selected, ⌘A (Ctrl+A) selects every card shown. In Trash you can select cards and **Delete forever** them together, and Trash says cards are deleted after 30 days.
- Inside a folder, **Remove from this folder** is in each card's menu, with Undo.
- Highlights snap to whole words, and the reader leaves out pages' own **[edit]** and **Read more** buttons.

## Search finds what you asked for

- A smart folder lists exactly the cards its search listed.
- `type:` understands everyday words like `note`, `pdf`, `photo` and `bookmark`, and an unknown type shows a chip saying so. A date beside other words, like `kestrel last week`, becomes a date filter plus the word.
- Searches show how many cards match and how many are related by meaning, with **Hide related**. When nothing matches, Teak suggests a close tag or title and lets you remove one filter at a time.
- The command palette always offers to search everything for what you typed.
- **View** has a **Compact** density. `G` then `L` opens Tags, and the shortcuts sheet lists the search filters.

## Imports, Settings and devices

- A CSV with a broken quote now imports every other row and lists the broken one. Semicolon-separated files import, lines in a text file that aren't links are listed instead of dropped, and **Last import** shows the file's name. See [Import](/docs/import).
- Teak no longer writes a summary or tags for a link whose page couldn't be reached.
- API keys can have names. **Security** lists this device first with when each device was last active, in your language, and **Sign out other devices** signs out everything but this one.
- Keyboard focus is easy to see on every button, a **Skip to content** link comes first, and each Settings page reads its own name aloud.

## Fixes

- **Settings → Security** now lists every signed-in device when you have more than 25, with **Show more devices** for the rest, instead of "Could not load devices." Signing out a device works on accounts with more than 100 sign-ins too.
