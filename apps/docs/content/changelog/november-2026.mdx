## A card's actions sit beside it

- On the web, an open card no longer has a bar across its top. Its details beside it now start with where the card is from, its type and its title, which wraps onto a few lines instead of being cut off. Under the title are **Open** (or **Download**) and a row of labelled buttons: **Favorite**, **Read** (or **Watched**, **Bought** and so on) for a link, **Copy**, **Folder** and **More**. Scroll the details and the buttons stay pinned to the top in one line. See [Cards](/docs/features#grid).
- Photos and videos fill the card, with nothing over them when you point at them.
- To change a link's address, click the pencil beside its URL in **Details**. **Replace** and **Reset** for a link's image sit beside **Image** there too.
- A card in Trash shows **Restore** and **Delete forever** at the top of its details.
- The details now stay beside the card on wide screens, so `I` no longer hides them.

## A calmer extension popup

- In Chrome and Firefox, the popup now shows what you're saving: the page's icon and title with its site, or the file's name and size. Its tags and note sit in the same card, and the line above it says whether it's saving, saved or already in your Teak. See [Browser extension](/docs/extension#auto-save-on-open).
- **Open Teak**, **Upload file** and **Sign out** moved into the **⋯** menu at the top, and your email shows at the top of that menu.

## Quicker capture on the Mac

- **New Note** (⌘N) now opens the small Quick Capture window, which follows your Light or Dark setting. Its placeholder lines up with where you type. See [Saving](/docs/mac#saving).
- **Voice Memo** (⇧⌘R) records in its own small window that stays above other apps, and it's in the menu bar item's menu too.
- Drop something on the menu bar icon and it shows an arrow while it saves, then a check. Dropping a file there no longer crashes Teak.
- **New folder** is now the last row of **Folders** in the sidebar, and the note, notes and tag fields in **Edit card** keep your text on the left.

## Fixes

- **Settings → Security** now lists every signed-in device when you have more than 25, with **Show more devices** for the rest, instead of "Could not load devices." Signing out a device works on accounts with more than 100 sign-ins too.
