- Your library opens with cards already loaded.
- Sharing into Teak from other apps works in more places and fails less often.
- Fewer saved cards get stuck while Teak processes them.
- Link, palette and other previews look better when a page shares little about itself.
- Upgrading is simpler, with plan details and billing explained before you pay.
- An open card is cleaner to read and edit.
